The **Users** page lists every **portal account** (login) in your school — administrators, teachers,
students, guardians and staff. From here you assign roles, reset a password, or turn an account off.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators.
</Callout>

## Open Users

From the admin sidebar, go to **Access → Users**.

The page has two tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Users** | Everyone who already has a login. |
| **Pending portal accounts** | People who don't have a login yet. See [Create portal accounts](../getting-started/onboard-people/create-portal-accounts). |

## The users list

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Full Name** | The person's photo and name. Click the name to open their profile. |
| **Username** | The name they sign in with. You can copy it. |
| **Email** | Their email address. You can copy it. |
| **Role** | Every role the person has — one person can have several. |
| **Status** | **Enabled** (they can sign in) or **Disabled** (they can't). |
| **Actions** | What you can do with the account — see below. |

### Search and filter

- Type in the search box to find someone.
- Open the filter to narrow the list by **Role** (**Admin**, **Teacher**, **Student**,
  **Guardian** or **Staff**) and **Status** (**Enabled** or **Disabled**), then click **Apply**.
  **Reset** clears the filters.

## What you can do with an account

Open the **Actions** menu on a row.

### Assign roles

Click **Assign roles** to change which roles the person has.

- **Assignable roles** lists **Admin** and your staff roles. Tick every staff role the person
  should have — a staff member can hold several, for example **Accountant** and **Librarian**.
  An administrator keeps **Admin** here.
- **Kept on account** shows roles that come from the person's profile, such as **Teacher** or
  **Parent**. They stay on the account and can't be changed here. **Parent** is added when the
  person becomes a guardian of a student, and removed when that guardian profile is deleted.
- A person can combine only two kinds of roles: **staff + parent**, or **teacher + parent**. An
  administrator can't also teach or hold a staff role, and a teacher can't hold a staff role.
- Student accounts can't hold other roles. Their role is always **Student**.
- The person must keep at least one role.

Click **Save**. The roles you assign apply to the school you're working in.

<Callout title="The Admin role">
Only the **organization owner** can give someone the **Admin** role. For everyone else, **Admin**
is greyed out with the note *Only the organization owner can give the Admin role*.
</Callout>

To decide what each staff role can do, see [Roles & permissions](./roles-and-permissions).

### Reset password

Click **Reset password**, then **Confirm**. The **Temporary password set** dialog shows the
**Username** and a new **Temporary password**. Copy them now — the temporary password is shown only
once. The person must change it the next time they sign in.

### Disable or enable an account

Click **Disable** and confirm to stop someone from signing in. Their records stay in the platform.
Click **Enable** on a disabled account to let them sign in again.

<Callout title="People from another campus">
If your organization has several campuses, a login belongs to one home campus. For someone whose
home campus is a different one, **Reset password** and **Disable** / **Enable** are greyed out
with the note *Managed by the home campus*. Ask an administrator there, or the organization owner.
</Callout>

## Add an administrator

Only the **organization owner** sees the **Add Admin** button.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the form

Click **Add Admin**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Fill in the details

Enter the **First Name**, **Last Name** and **Email**. **Phone Number** is optional. The username
and password are created for you.
</Step>

<Step>
### Create the account

Click **Create**. A dialog shows the **Username** and **Temporary password** once — copy them.
They are also sent by email.
</Step>

</Steps>
