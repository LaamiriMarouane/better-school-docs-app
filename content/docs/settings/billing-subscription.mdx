**Billing & Subscription** is where you manage your school's **subscription** — trial status,
enrollment usage, upgrades, downgrades, invoices, and cancellation. It is **not** the same as
school fee billing (installment schedules for families).

<Callout title="Two different Billings">
| Place | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| Sidebar → **Billing & Subscription** | Your school's subscription and usage. |
| **Settings → Finance → Billing** | How student fee installments are timed. See [Billing configuration](./billing-configuration). |
</Callout>

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators who manage the school's subscription. Staff members who have been given access can
open it too.
</Callout>

## Open Billing & Subscription

From the admin sidebar (Settings group), go to **Billing & Subscription**.

## What you see

### Current plan

| Item | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Plan** | **Free Trial**, or a paid plan (**Starter**, **Growth**, **Standard**, **Enterprise**). |
| **Status** | **Trialing**, **Active**, **Past due**, **Paused**, or **Cancelled**. |
| **Trial Ends** / **Next Billing** | Depending on whether you are on trial or paid. |
| **Billing cycle** | **Monthly** or **Yearly** (when on a paid plan). |
| **Enrollments** | Active enrollments against the plan limit (unlimited during the trial or on unlimited plans). |

If a change is already scheduled (a cancellation or a downgrade), a notice shows the date.

### Choose a plan

In the plans list, pick **Monthly** or **Yearly**, then choose a plan:

| Action | When it appears |
| --- | --- |
| **Subscribe** / **Choose a Plan** | No paid plan yet, or still on trial. |
| **Upgrade** | Moving to a higher plan while Active or Past due. |
| **Downgrade** | Moving to a lower plan (not available if your enrollments exceed that plan's limit). |
| **Contact Sales** | Enterprise or custom needs. |
| **Current** | Label on the plan you already have. |
| **Scheduled** | Label on a downgrade waiting for the next billing date. |

## Upgrade, downgrade, or subscribe

<Steps>

<Step>
### Pick the cycle and plan

Select **Monthly** or **Yearly**, then the plan action (**Upgrade**, **Downgrade**, **Subscribe**,
or **Choose a Plan**).
</Step>

<Step>
### Confirm or check out

- **Upgrade** takes effect immediately. You are billed now for the rest of the current period.
- **Downgrade** takes effect at the next billing date. You keep your current plan until then.
- With no paid plan yet, you go to the checkout page to pay for the selected plan and cycle through
  Paddle, our payment provider. When payment succeeds, you return to the app with the updated
  subscription.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Invoices

The **Invoices** list shows each invoice with its amount and status. Use **Download** to get a copy.

## Update your payment method

Use **Update payment method** to change the card you pay with. If a payment fails, a **Payment
failed** notice appears with the same button: update your payment method and the overdue payment is
retried automatically.

## Cancel a subscription

When the status is **Active** or **Past due** and no change is already scheduled, use
**Cancel subscription** and confirm. The page then shows the date the cancellation takes effect.
