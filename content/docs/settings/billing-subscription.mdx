**Billing & Subscription** is where you manage the school's **SaaS plan** with BetterSchool —
trial status, enrollment usage, upgrades, downgrades, and cancellation. It is **not** the same
as school fee billing (installment schedules for families).

<Callout title="Two different Billings">
| Place | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| Sidebar → **Billing & Subscription** | Your school's BetterSchool plan and usage. |
| **Settings → Finance → Billing** | How student fee installments are timed. See [Billing configuration](./billing-configuration). |
</Callout>

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators who manage the school's subscription.
</Callout>

## Open Billing & Subscription

From the admin sidebar (Settings group), go to **Billing & Subscription**.

## What you see

### Current plan

The left side shows:

| Item | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Plan** | Free Trial, or a paid tier (Starter, Growth, Standard, Enterprise). |
| **Status** | Trialing, Active, Past due, Paused, or Cancelled. |
| **Trial Ends** / **Next Billing** | Depending on whether you are on trial or paid. |
| **Billing cycle** | Monthly or Yearly (when on a paid plan). |
| **Usage** | Active enrollments vs the plan limit (unlimited during trial or on unlimited plans). |

If a change is already scheduled (cancel, pause, or resume), a notice shows the date.

### Choose a plan

On the right, pick **Monthly** or **Yearly**, then choose a plan:

| Action | When it appears |
| --- | --- |
| **Subscribe** / **Choose a Plan** | No paid plan yet, or still on trial. |
| **Upgrade** | Moving to a higher tier while Active or Past due. |
| **Downgrade** | Moving to a lower tier (disabled if your enrollments exceed that plan's limit). |
| **Contact Sales** | Enterprise / custom needs. |
| **Current** | Label on the plan you already have. |

## Upgrade, downgrade, or subscribe

<Steps>

<Step>
### Pick the cycle and plan

Select **Monthly** or **Yearly**, then the plan action (**Upgrade**, **Downgrade**, **Subscribe**,
or **Choose a Plan**).
</Step>

<Step>
### Confirm or check out

- If you already have an **Active** or **Past due** subscription, confirm the change in the dialog.
- Otherwise you go to **Checkout** to pay for the selected plan and cycle (Paddle). When payment
  succeeds, you return to the app with the updated subscription.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Cancel a subscription

When the status is **Active** or **Past due** and no change is already scheduled, use
**Cancel subscription** and confirm. The UI may then show a scheduled cancel date if one is set.
