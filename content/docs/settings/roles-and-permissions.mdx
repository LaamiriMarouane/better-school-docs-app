**Roles & permissions** is where you decide what your non-teaching staff can do in the platform.
You create a staff role (for example *Front desk*), switch on the permission sets it needs, and
then give that role to people from the **Users** page.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators. Other people don't see this page.
</Callout>

## Open Roles & permissions

From the admin sidebar, go to **Access → Roles & permissions**.

The page has two parts:

- **The roles list** on the side, with a search box (**Search roles**). Each role shows how many
  users have it and how many permissions it gives. Built-in roles show a lock icon and are listed
  first.
- **The role details**, which open when you click a role. Click the same role again to close it.
  On a phone, the details open in a panel.

## Built-in roles

Some roles come with the platform. They show a lock icon, and their details say: *This role is
defined by the product. The name and permissions cannot be changed.* You can open them to see the
**Permissions** they give, but you can't edit or delete them.

| Role | Who it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | School administrators — full access. |
| **Teacher** | Teachers. |
| **Student** | Students. |
| **Parent** | Guardians. |
| **Driver** | Bus drivers (Transport). |
| **Bus Monitor** | Bus monitors (Transport). |

Your school also starts with ready-made staff roles — **Secretary**, **Accountant**,
**Librarian**, **Janitor**, **Security Guard** and **Kitchen Staff**. These are ordinary staff
roles: you can rename them and change their permissions like any role you create.

## Create a staff role

<Steps>

<Step>
### Start a new role

Click **New role**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Pick a starting point

Choose a template. Each one comes with a set of permissions you can change later:

| Template | Starts with |
| --- | --- |
| **Driver** | Operate trips and mark transport attendance |
| **Bus monitor** | Ride along and mark transport attendance |
| **Secretary** | Front desk: students, guardians, enrollment |
| **Accountant** | Fees, expenses, and financial reports |
| **Librarian** | Equipment inventory |
| **Janitor** | Building maintenance and cleanliness |
| **Security guard** | Campus security and access control |
| **Kitchen staff** | Canteen and meal preparation |
| **Blank** | Baseline only: profile, leave, own attendance |
</Step>

<Step>
### Name it and create it

Type a **Name** (for example *Front desk*), then click **New role**. The role opens so you can
adjust its permissions.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Choose what a staff role can do

Permissions for staff roles are grouped into **permission sets**. Open a staff role to see them.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Pick a permission set

The list shows each set with how many of its actions are on (for example *2 of 5 actions*). Sets
include **Enrollment**, **Admissions**, **Student attendance**, **Finance**, **School
subscription**, **Transport**, **Equipment**, **HR & staff**, **Portal & accounts** and
**Dismissal**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Turn the set on

Use the switch to turn the set on. This gives the role access to that area. Turn it off to remove
the access.
</Step>

<Step>
### Tick the actions

Under **Actions**, tick what the role may do in that area — for example **Record payment** and
**Refund / void payment** in **Finance**, or **Approve / reject leave** in **HR & staff**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Save

You can also rename the role at the top. Click the **Save** icon to keep your changes. If you
leave the role with unsaved changes, the platform asks you to confirm first.
</Step>

</Steps>

Changes apply right away to everyone who has the role.

<Callout title="Sets that depend on another set">
Some sets and actions need another set first. They show *Requires … to be enabled* until you turn
that set on. You also can't turn off a set while other sets you've turned on still depend on it.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Modules that are off">
Sets and actions for a module your school has turned off (such as Transport, Equipment, Dismissal,
Expenses, Staff Management or Leave Management) are hidden. Turn the module on to see them.
</Callout>

## Delete a staff role

Open the role and click the **Delete** (bin) icon, then confirm. Only staff roles that **nobody
has** can be deleted. Built-in roles can never be deleted.

## Give roles to people

Roles are given to people from the **Users** page:

- When you create a staff member's portal account, you choose their **Portal role**. See
  [Create portal accounts](../getting-started/onboard-people/create-portal-accounts).
- To change someone's roles later, or to give a staff member **several staff roles**, use **Assign roles**
  on the **Users** page. See [Users](./users#assign-roles).

<Callout title="The Admin role">
Only the **organization owner** can give someone the **Admin** role.
</Callout>
