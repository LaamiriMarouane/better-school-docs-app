Your account is an **organization**. Each school in it is a **campus**. Most groups have one
campus; a group with several schools can add more campuses and run them all under the **same
subscription**.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators who can add campuses. Other people don't see this page.
</Callout>

## Open Campuses

From the admin sidebar, go to **Settings → Campuses**.

At the top you see how many campuses you have and how many your plan allows (for example
*1 of 3 campuses*).

The table lists every campus of your organization:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The campus name. |
| **City** | Its city. |
| **Country** | Its country. |
| **Status** | **Active** marks the campus you are working in now. |

## Add a campus

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the form

Click **Add campus**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Fill in the details

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Campus name** | Yes | For example *Downtown campus*. |
| **Country** | No | Pick from the list. |
| **City** | No | |
| **Timezone** | No | Pick from the list. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Create it

Click **Add campus**. The new campus appears in the table. You are an administrator of the new
campus, so you can switch to it right away.
</Step>

</Steps>

A new campus shares your current subscription. It has its own set-up, so set it up as you did your
first school: school profile, modules, academic year and people. See
[First-time school setup](../getting-started/first-time-school-setup).

## When you can't add a campus

Instead of **Add campus**, the page shows a card that explains why:

| Card | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Campuses need an active plan** | During the trial, or without an active plan, your organization has one campus. | Click **View billing** to subscribe. |
| **Campus limit reached** | Your plan doesn't allow more campuses. | Click **Contact sales** for a multi-campus contract, or **View billing** to change plan. |

## Switch between campuses

When you can work in more than one campus, a **school picker** appears in the top bar (on a
computer or large screen). Pick a campus and the page reloads in that campus: menus, lists and
settings now show that campus.

Picking an academic year of another campus in the year picker also switches to that campus.

<Callout title="One login for the whole organization">
People sign in once for the whole organization. What they can do depends on the roles they have
at each campus. Roles are assigned on the [Users](./users#assign-roles) page of the campus you're
working in.
</Callout>
