The **Audit log** records the actions people take in your school: who did it, when, and on what.
Use it to answer questions such as *who changed this payment?* or *who signed in yesterday?*

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators. Other people don't see this page.
</Callout>

## Open the Audit log

From the admin sidebar, go to **Settings → Audit log**.

The newest entries are at the top.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **When** | Date and time of the action, in your school's time zone (shown next to the column name). |
| **Who** | The person who did it. |
| **Role** | The role they were using. |
| **Action** | What they did — for example **Create**, **Update**, **Delete**, **Login**, **Login failed**, **Password reset**, **Approve**, **Export** or **Permission change**. |
| **Module** | The area of the platform — for example **Finance**, **Enrollment**, **Exams & grades**, **Attendance & leave**, **Users** or **Settings**. |
| **Entity** | The item that was changed (for example a student's name) and its type. |
| **Description** | A short sentence describing what happened. |

## Search and filter

- Use the search box to find entries by **email, username, full name, or entity**.
- Open the filter to narrow the list:
  - **Action** — one kind of action, or **All actions**.
  - **Module** — one area, or **All modules**.
  - **Date range** — **Pick a date range**.

  Click **Apply**. Clear the filters to see everything again.

## Export

Click **Export CSV** to download the entries as a CSV file you can open in a spreadsheet. The
export follows the search and filters you have applied, so filter first to export only what you
need.

## How long entries are kept

Click the **Retention settings** (gear) button next to **Export CSV**. Enter the **Retention
(days)** and click **Save retention**. Entries older than this are deleted every night.
