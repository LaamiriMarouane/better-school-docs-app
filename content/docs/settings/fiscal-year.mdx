---
title: Fiscal years
description: Define the fiscal year used for the money figures on the admin dashboard.
---

A **fiscal year** is an accounting period for the school's finances. It is separate
from the academic year and can start in any month. The active fiscal year sets the period
covered by the money figures on the admin dashboard. It does not change fees, payments, or
financial reports.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators managing the school's finance periods.
</Callout>

## Create a fiscal year

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the Fiscal Years settings

Go to **Settings → Finance** and open the **Fiscal Year** tab, then add a new fiscal year.

<Screenshot
  label="Create fiscal year form"
  caption="The form: label, start and end dates, and an optional description."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Fill in the fiscal year details

Complete the fields below, then click **Submit**.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Label** | Yes | The display name, e.g. *FY 2025*. |
| **Start Date** | Yes | The first day of the accounting period. |
| **End Date** | Yes | The last day of the accounting period. |
| **Description** | No | Optional notes about the fiscal year. |
</Step>

</Steps>

## Activate a fiscal year

One fiscal year is the **active** one. In the list, use **Activate** on the year you want to
make current and confirm; the year that was active is deactivated. You can also
deactivate the active year.

<Callout title="Fiscal year vs academic year">
The **academic year** organises classes, terms, and enrollments. The **fiscal year**
organises accounting. They do not have to line up — keep them separate.
</Callout>
