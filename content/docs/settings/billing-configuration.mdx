**Billing configuration** sets the rules behind how a student's fees turn into
installments — which day installments fall on, when charges apply, and what a student who
joins mid-month pays. It applies to the schedules created from then on.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators — only administrators can change billing settings.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Not your subscription">
This page is about **when student fee installments fall due**. Your school's own
subscription is managed under [Billing & Subscription](./billing-subscription).
</Callout>

## Settings

Go to **Settings → Finance** and open the **Billing** tab to set the options below.

| Setting | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| **Billing Cycle Anchor** | The day installments fall on. **1st of each month**, **Student enrollment date** (that day every month), or **Custom day of month**. |
| **Custom Anchor Day** | The day (1 to 28) used when the anchor is a custom day. |
| **Charge Timing** | **In advance** charges at the start of the period. **In arrears** charges at the end, and skips a period whose due day already passed before the student joined. |
| **Student joining mid-month** | What a student who joins after the month's due day pays for that first month (monthly fees): **Full month** (the default), **Only the days left (prorated)**, or **Start next month**. |

Adjust the settings, then click **Save**.

<Callout type="warn" title="Set this before enrolling students">
These settings shape how installments are created. Set them before creating payment plans
and enrolling students so every schedule follows the same rules.
</Callout>

## How a due date is chosen

Billing starts at the student's **enrollment date**. After that, installments fall on the
anchor day.

With **1st of each month**, **In advance**, and **Full month**, a student who enrolls on
3 October is not billed for September. October is due on **3 October**, the joining day,
so nothing is overdue on day one. November through the end of the year are due on the 1st.

With **Start next month**, October is not billed and the first installment is due on
1 November. With **Only the days left (prorated)**, October is reduced to the days left and
due on 3 October.

**In arrears** with the same anchor skips October. The first due date is 1 November.

The worked examples, including what happens when you change a student's plan, are in
[Installments and schedules](../finance/fees-and-payments/installments-and-schedules).
