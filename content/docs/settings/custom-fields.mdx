---
title: Custom fields
description: Add your own fields to student, teacher, staff, and guardian records.
---

**Custom fields** let your school collect information the platform does not ask for by
default. A custom field you define appears on the record's form — for students,
teachers, staff, or guardians — so everyone captures the same extra details.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators tailoring what information the school records about people.
</Callout>

## Add a custom field

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the Custom Fields settings

Go to **Settings → School** and open the **Custom Fields** tab. Pick the profile the field is
for — **Students**, **Teachers**, **Staff**, or **Guardians** — then click **Add Field**.

<Screenshot
  label="Custom field dialog"
  caption="Define the field's profile, type, label, and options."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Define the field

Complete the options below. A **Live preview** shows how the field will look on the form.

| Option | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Field Type** | **Text**, **Paragraph**, **Number**, **Date**, **Dropdown**, **Yes / No**, **Email**, **Phone**, **URL**, **Radio**, or **Checkbox**. |
| **Field label** | The field's name, shown on the form. |
| **Placeholder** | Optional hint text shown inside the input. |
| **Options** | For **Dropdown**, **Radio**, and **Checkbox** fields: the choices, separated by commas. |
| **Required** | When on, the field must be filled in before the form can be submitted. |
| **Active** | When off, the field is kept but no longer shown on forms. |
| **Visible to** | Turn **Teacher**, **Guardian**, and **Student** on or off to choose who else can see the field. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Save

Click **Create**.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Where custom fields appear">
A field defined for *students* shows in the student form; one for *staff* shows in the staff
form, and so on. Existing records simply gain the new field. Changes to a field apply to every
form where it appears.
</Callout>
