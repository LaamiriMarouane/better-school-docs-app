**Settings** is where a school's configuration lives. Most settings are organised into
tabs; some are documented in their own chapters, and the rest are covered here.

## Settings tabs

| Tab | What it does | Documented in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **School Info** | School name, contact details, logo, social links | [Set up the school profile](./getting-started/first-time-school-setup/set-up-the-school-profile) |
| **Modules** | Turn optional feature areas on or off | [Configure school modules](./getting-started/first-time-school-setup/configure-school-modules) |
| **Regional** | Timezone, currency, date and time formats | [Set regional preferences](./getting-started/first-time-school-setup/set-regional-preferences) |
| **School Documents** | The school's official files | [Upload school documents](./getting-started/first-time-school-setup/upload-school-documents) |
| **Academic Years** | School cycles and their terms | [Create an academic year](./getting-started/build-the-academic-foundation/create-an-academic-year) |
| **Fiscal Years** | Accounting periods for finance | [Fiscal years](./settings/fiscal-year) |
| **Billing** | How and when billing cycles run | [Billing configuration](./settings/billing-configuration) |
| **Custom Fields** | Extra fields collected on people | [Custom fields](./settings/custom-fields) |
| **Transport Map** | How route maps look — markers and lines | [Transport map appearance](./settings/transport-map) |

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators — most settings can only be changed by an administrator.
</Callout>
