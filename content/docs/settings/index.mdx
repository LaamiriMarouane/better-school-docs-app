title: Settings
description: The school-wide settings area — school details, campuses, modules, finance periods, audit log, subscription, and more.
---

**Settings** is where a school's configuration lives. In the admin sidebar, **Settings** opens into
separate pages; each page has its own tabs. Subscription and support tools sit next to Settings
in the same sidebar group.

## Settings pages

| Page | Tabs | Documented in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Academic** | Academic Year, Term Structure, School Schedule, Dismissal | [Create an academic year](../getting-started/build-the-academic-foundation/create-an-academic-year), [Academic year setup](../getting-started/build-the-academic-foundation/academic-year-setup), [School schedule](./school-schedule) |
| **School** | School Info, School Documents, Regional, Reclamations, Custom Fields, Map appearance | [Set up the school profile](../getting-started/first-time-school-setup/set-up-the-school-profile), [Custom fields](./custom-fields), [Transport map appearance](./transport-map) |
| **Campuses** | — | Add and review the schools of your organization under one subscription. |
| **Finance** | Fiscal Year, Billing | [Fiscal years](./fiscal-year), [Billing configuration](./billing-configuration) |
| **Modules** | Optional feature toggles | [Configure school modules](../getting-started/first-time-school-setup/configure-school-modules) |
| **Audit log** | — | See who did what, and when, across your school. You can filter and export it. |

Some tabs appear only when their module is on: **Dismissal** (Dismissal module), **Reclamations**
(Reclamations module), and **Map appearance** (Transport module). **Campuses** shows only to people
who can add campuses.
