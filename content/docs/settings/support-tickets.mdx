**Support** lets your school open tickets with the BetterSchool team, follow the conversation,
and close or reopen tickets when work is done.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators who need help from BetterSchool support.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Active subscription required">
Creating tickets, replying, closing, reopening, and rating need an **Active** paid subscription.
If you only have a trial (or no active plan), open **Billing & Subscription** to upgrade first.
</Callout>

## Open Support

From the admin sidebar (Settings group), go to **Support**.

The page is a list on the left and the selected ticket on the right. Summary cards show Open,
In progress, Resolved, and Closed counts. You can search and filter by status.

## Create a ticket

<Steps>

<Step>
### New ticket

Click **New ticket**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Fill in the details

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Subject** | Yes | Short summary. |
| **Category** | Yes | Bug report, Feature inquiry, Billing, Account access, Data import, Training, or Other. |
| **Priority** | Yes | Low, Medium, High, or Urgent (defaults to Medium). |
| **Description** | Yes | What happened and what you need. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Submit

Click **Submit ticket**. The ticket starts as **Open**.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Ticket statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Open** | Newly submitted. |
| **In progress** | BetterSchool is working on it. |
| **Waiting on you** | The team needs a reply from your school. |
| **Resolved** | Marked resolved by the team. |
| **Closed** | Closed (by you or after resolution). |

Your school can **Close** a ticket that is not closed, and **Reopen** one that is closed or
resolved. Status changes such as In progress are made by the BetterSchool team.

## Reply and rate

In the ticket detail:

- Read the **Conversation** (your school vs the BetterSchool team).
- **Send reply** while the ticket is not closed.
- After a ticket is resolved or closed, you can **Rate experience** (1–5 stars and an optional
  comment) once.
