The administrator dashboard is a **grid of widgets**. You choose which widgets to show, drag them into
order, and resize them. Your layout is saved to **your account**, so each administrator can arrange
their own.

<Callout title="Who can customize">
Customizing is for **administrators**. Teachers, students, and parents have fixed
[role dashboards](./role-dashboards) instead.
</Callout>

## Arrange your widgets

<Steps>

<Step>
### Enter edit mode

On the dashboard, turn on **Edit**. The widgets become movable.
</Step>

<Step>
### Add or remove

Open the widget panel to **add** a widget, or remove ones you don't need.
</Step>

<Step>
### Drag and resize

**Drag** a widget to reposition it and pull its edge to **resize** it on the grid.
</Step>

<Step>
### Done

Turn off edit mode. Your layout is **saved automatically** for next time.
</Step>

</Steps>

## The widgets

Widgets fall into four groups.

### KPIs (single numbers)

Total **students**, **staff**, **teachers**, **enrollments**, **pre-inscriptions**, **guardians**,
**expenses**, and **payments** collected.

### Charts

Distributions and trends, including:

- **Students** — by gender and by age.
- **Staff and teachers** — by gender, by contract type, and by age.
- **Enrollments** — by status.
- **Pre-inscriptions** — by gender and by status.
- **Guardians** — by type and by age.
- **Fees** — fee installments by status.
- **Expenses** — overall breakdown, by category, by vehicle, and by month.

### Attendance

Breakdowns for **student**, **teacher**, and **staff** attendance.

### Lists

**Upcoming events** and **recent announcements**.

<Callout title="Some widgets need a module">
**Staff** and **non-teacher attendance** widgets appear only with the **Staff Management** module, and
the **expense** widgets only with the **Expenses** module. Widgets for modules you don't use won't be
offered.
</Callout>

## Filters

The dashboard follows the **academic year** in the switcher at the top. Widgets that cover a span — like
attendance and expenses — use a **date range** as well, so you can focus a widget on a specific period.
