When you sign in you land on a **dashboard** built for your role. Administrators get an overview of
the whole school; teachers, students, and parents get focused dashboards for their day. Behind the
dashboards sits a catalog of **statistics** — counts, rates, and breakdowns across the whole school.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Everyone has a dashboard. Only **administrators** see the school-wide statistics; teachers, students, and parents see numbers about their own work or child.
</Callout>

## The pieces

| Piece | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **The admin dashboard** | An overview of the school — headcounts, money in and out, today's attendance, and what's coming up. |
| **Role dashboards** | The fixed dashboards teachers, students, and parents see. |
| **Reports & statistics** | The numbers behind the dashboards: people, attendance, finance, and teaching. |

<Callout title="Everything follows the selected year">
The figures reflect the **academic year** shown in the switcher at the top of the screen. Some
reports also take a **date range**. Switch the year to report on a different one.
</Callout>

## The admin dashboard

The administrator dashboard gives a one-page view of the school:

- **Headline cards** — students, teachers, staff, guardians, and enrollments, with how many were added this month.
- **Money** — spent this month, collected this month, and what remains pending or overdue.
- **Quick actions** — add a student, add a teacher, record a payment, or create an event.
- **Charts and lists** — expenses, enrollment status, students by age, pre-inscriptions, and today's
  attendance for students, teachers, and staff.
- **Upcoming events** and **recent announcements**.
