title: Dashboards & reports
description: "The dashboard you land on, the widgets admins can arrange, and the statistics behind them."
---

When you sign in you land on a **dashboard** built for your role. Administrators get a **customizable**
dashboard of widgets; teachers, students, and parents get focused dashboards for their day. Behind the
widgets sits a catalog of **statistics** — counts, rates, and breakdowns across the whole school.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Everyone has a dashboard. Only **administrators** can rearrange theirs and see the school-wide
statistics; teachers, students, and parents see numbers about their own work or child.
</Callout>

## The pieces

| Piece | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **The admin dashboard** | A grid of widgets — KPIs, charts, attendance, and lists — that you add, arrange, and resize. |
| **Role dashboards** | The fixed dashboards teachers, students, and parents see. |
| **Reports & statistics** | The numbers behind the widgets: people, attendance, finance, and teaching. |

<Callout title="Everything follows the selected year">
The figures reflect the **academic year** shown in the switcher at the top of the screen. Some
reports also take a **date range**. Switch the year to report on a different one.
</Callout>
