This page explains what each figure on the [admin dashboard](./) measures, where to find quiz
results, and where the school reports live.

<Callout title="What the figures cover">
The figures follow the **academic year** in the switcher at the top of the screen; expense figures
cover your current fiscal year. There is no date filter on the dashboard.
</Callout>

## People

| Figure | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Students** | The total, split into male and female, and how many were added this month. **Students by age** shows how many students fall into each age band (3–5, 6–10, 11–14, 15–18, 18+). |
| **Teachers** | The total, split into full time and part time, and how many were added this month. |
| **Staff** | The total, split into full time and contract, and how many were added this month. |
| **Guardians** | The total, split into fathers and mothers, and how many were added this month. |
| **Enrollments** | The total, split into active and pending. The **Enrollment status** chart shows how many are active, pending, suspended, withdrawn, transferred, or completed, with each one's share. |
| **Pre-Inscriptions** | The total, and how many are pending, enrolled, rejected, or cancelled. |

<Callout title="Staff figures need a module">
The **staff** figures come from the **Staff Management** module. With it off, they stay at zero.
</Callout>

## Attendance

| Figure | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Today's attendance** | For students, teachers, and staff: how many are absent today, and what share of the people marked today that is. Each row opens the matching attendance page. |

See [Student attendance](../teaching/sessions-and-attendance) and
[Staff attendance](../staff-hr/staff-attendance).

## Finance

| Figure | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Spent this month** | What the school spent this month, with the total number of expenses. |
| **Collected this month** | What the school collected this month, with how many payments were successful, cancelled, or refunded. |
| **Remaining** | What is still owed on fee installments, with how many installments are pending or overdue. |
| **Expenses** | Total spent, number of expenses, and this month's spending, with a chart by month. **Expenses by type** splits spending into manual entries and vehicle costs (fuel, maintenance, insurance, vignette, inspection); **Expenses by category** splits it by your expense categories. |

## Teaching

Open a quiz, or a quiz assignment, and go to its **Analytics** tab to see the **Avg Score**,
**Completion Rate**, **Pass Rate**, **Total Attempts**, **Question Performance**, and
**Score Distribution**. See [Quizzes](../teaching/quizzes).

## School reports

Administrators also have ready-made reports under **Reports → Academic reports** and
**Reports → Financial reports**, to view on screen and download.
