The figures behind the dashboard widgets come from a set of **statistics** the system keeps for your
school. This page is a catalog of what each one measures. They surface as **dashboard widgets** and
inside each module's own pages.

<Callout title="Filters">
Most figures follow the **academic year** in the switcher. Attendance and expenses also accept a
**date range**, and expenses can be filtered by **fiscal year**.
</Callout>

## People

| Report | What it measures |
| --- | --- |
| **Students** | Total students, new today / this month / this year, and breakdowns by **gender** and **age**. |
| **Enrollments** | Total, broken down by **status** (active, inactive, suspended, withdrawn, transferred, completed, pending) and by **gender**, plus new enrollments over time. |
| **Pre-inscriptions** | Counts by **status** (pending, enrolled, rejected, cancelled) and by **gender**, plus new ones over time, with a per-level breakdown. |
| **Guardians** | Counts by **type** (father, mother, legal guardian, other) and **gender**, the **average number of students per guardian**, and new guardians over time. |
| **Staff** | Counts by **contract type** (full-time, part-time, contract, temporary, internship), by **gender**, and by **age**. |

<Callout title="Staff figures need a module">
The **staff** report is part of the **Staff Management** module. With it off, staff counts and charts
don't appear.
</Callout>

## Attendance

| Report | What it measures |
| --- | --- |
| **Student attendance** | Counts of present, absent, late, and half-day, plus an **attendance rate** and a **punctuality rate** — for the whole school, a class, or one student, over a date range. |
| **Staff attendance** | Present, absent, late, on-leave, and half-day counts, total **work hours**, and **attendance** and **punctuality** rates — split between **teachers** and **non-teaching staff**. |

Attendance rate is the share of days marked present; punctuality compares those who showed up
(present, late, and half-days). See [Student attendance](../teaching/sessions-and-attendance) and
[Staff attendance](../staff-hr/staff-attendance).

## Finance

| Report | What it measures |
| --- | --- |
| **Payments** | Total **amount collected** (successful payments), the payment count, and counts by status (successful, cancelled, refunded). |
| **Fee installments** | Counts and **amounts** by status (pending, paid, overdue, cancelled, waived) — total due, paid, and remaining — and a breakdown **by level and fee**. |
| **Expenses** | Total spend and count, new today / month / year, and breakdowns **by category**, **by vehicle**, and **by month**. |

<Callout title="Expense figures need a module">
The **expenses** report is part of the **Expenses** module, and supports a **fiscal-year** filter
alongside the date range.
</Callout>

## Teaching

| Report | What it measures |
| --- | --- |
| **Quiz analytics** | For a quiz assignment or across a quiz: **average** score and percentage, **min / max / median**, participants and attempts, **completion rate**, **pass rate**, a **per-question** breakdown, and a **score distribution**. |

See [Quizzes](../teaching/quizzes).

## Where these appear

- **Dashboard widgets** — the KPIs and charts on the [admin dashboard](./customize-the-dashboard).
- **Module pages** — each area (finance, expenses, attendance, quizzes) also shows its own figures in
  context.
