---
title: Role dashboards
description: "What teachers, students, and parents see on their dashboards."
---

Teachers, students, and parents each get a dashboard focused on what matters to them — the right
things for the role, ready as soon as they sign in.

## Teacher dashboard

A teacher's home for the day:

- **KPIs** — **Today's Sessions**, **Pending Grading**, **Upcoming Exams**, and **Total Students**.
- **My Classes** — the classes they teach, with each class's level, track, and number of students.
- **Pending Grading** — assignments and quizzes with submissions still to mark.
- **Upcoming Exams** — the next exams for their classes.
- **Class Attendance** — the attendance rate of each of their classes.
- **Upcoming Events** and the latest **Announcements**.

## Student dashboard

A student's overview of their own progress:

- **KPIs** — **Attendance Rate**, **Average Grade**, **Pending Assignments**, and **Upcoming Exams**.
- **Today's Schedule** — the day's classes.
- **Pending Assignments** and **Recent Grades**.
- **Attendance** — how many days they were present, absent, late, or on a half day.
- **Upcoming Exams**.
- **Upcoming Events** and the latest **Announcements**.

<Callout title="Behaviour podium">
If your school uses the **Behaviour** module, the dashboard also shows the top three in the student's
class this week. See [Points & leaderboard](../behaviour/leaderboard).
</Callout>

## Parent dashboard

A parent sees the overview of the child selected in the sidebar — the same sections as the student
dashboard, plus their fees:

- **KPIs** — **Attendance Rate**, **Average Grade**, **Upcoming Exams**, **Next due date**, and
  **Overdue Amount**.
- **Upcoming payments** — what's due next for the child.
- The behaviour podium, if your school uses the **Behaviour** module.

<Callout title="Pick the child first">
Choose the child in the sidebar. With more than one child, switch there to see another child's
dashboard.
</Callout>
