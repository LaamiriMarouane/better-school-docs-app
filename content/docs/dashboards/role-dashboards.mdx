---
title: Role dashboards
description: "What teachers, students, and parents see on their dashboards."
---

Teachers, students, and parents each get a **fixed** dashboard focused on what matters to them. Unlike
the [admin dashboard](./customize-the-dashboard), these aren't rearranged — they show the right things
for the role.

## Teacher dashboard

A teacher's home for the day:

- **KPIs** — today's sessions, pending grading, upcoming exams, and total students.
- **My classes** — the classes they teach.
- **Pending grading** — assignments and quizzes waiting to be marked.
- **Class attendance** — a quick view of attendance to take or review.
- **Upcoming events and exams**.

## Student dashboard

A student's overview of their own progress:

- **KPIs** — attendance rate, average grade, pending assignments, and upcoming exams.
- **Today's schedule** — the day's classes.
- **Attendance** — their own attendance, with a simple chart.
- **Recent grades** and **pending assignments**.
- **Upcoming events and exams**.

<Callout title="Behaviour podium">
If your school uses the **Behaviour** module, the student dashboard also shows their **podium**
standing. See [Points & leaderboard](../behaviour/leaderboard).
</Callout>

## Parent dashboard

A parent picks a **child** (and the academic year), then sees that child's overview — the same view as
the student dashboard, plus:

- **KPIs** — attendance rate, average grade, next payment due date, and upcoming exams.
- **Upcoming payments** — what's due for the child.
- **Upcoming exams** and **upcoming events**.

<Callout title="Pick the child first">
With more than one child, choose which one from the selector before the figures fill in.
</Callout>
