The school reports answer the questions that come up every week: how many students are in each
class, which grades are still missing, who is absent too often, which families still owe money, and
what came into the cash desk today. Each report opens on screen, and many can be printed or
downloaded.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators, and staff members who handle money (financial reports only). If a report is missing
from your menu, ask an administrator to give you access.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Everything follows the selected year">
Reports show the **academic year** selected in the switcher at the top of the screen. Switch the
year to see an earlier one.
</Callout>

## Open the reports

- **Academic reports**: go to **Reports → Academic reports**.
- **Financial reports**: go to **Reports → Financial reports**. Staff members find it in their own
  menu as **Financial reports**.

Each page lists its reports as cards, grouped by topic. Click a card to open the report, then choose
what it should cover — a level, a class, a term — at the top. Click **All reports** (or **All financial
reports**) to go back to the list.

## Academic reports

### Enrollment

| Report | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Enrollment summary** | How many students are in each class and track of a level, boys and girls, and how full each class is compared with its capacity. Use **Export** to download the full student list as a spreadsheet. |
| **Class roster** | A printable list of the students in a class. |

### Grades

| Report | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Grade distribution** | How a class's results are spread, with the class mean and median. Choose a term, or leave **All terms**. Use **Export** to download the class's grade sheet as a PDF, the grades grid as a spreadsheet, or the end-of-term summary as a PDF. |
| **Pending grades** | Assessments that are not marked or not published yet, by class. Clear this list before you close a term. |
| **Report cards (batch)** | Downloads all the report cards already generated for a class or a whole level, in one ZIP file. Generate the cards first — see [Report cards](../teaching/report-cards). |

### Attendance

| Report | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Attendance rate** | Attendance and punctuality across the year, class by class. |
| **Absence watchlist** | Students who went over the absence limit set for their level. To set the limit, open the level, click **Thresholds** on its **Overview** tab and fill in **Max absences (year)**. Without a limit, this report stays empty. |

### Curriculum

| Report | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Curriculum coverage** | How much of the curriculum each class of a level has covered, with the level average. |
| **Class coverage** | The same, subject by subject, for one class. |

Coverage only counts lessons that teachers recorded in the **Activity journal**. See
[Curriculum progress](./curriculum-progress) for the week-by-week view.

<Callout title="Can't see Export?">
Downloading spreadsheets of student data is for administrators only. It can't be given to a
staff role, so staff members don't see the **Export** button.
</Callout>

## Financial reports

### Overview

| Report | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Collection summary** | Money collected against money expected, with charts. |
| **Collection by class** | Money expected and collected for each level and class, and which students are fully paid. |

### Collections

| Report | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Unpaid students** | Installments still unpaid, how late they are, and the monthly collection trend. You can export the list. |
| **Receipt journal** | Every payment receipt, with filters, and a spreadsheet export. |
| **Family balance** | What each family owes for all of its children together. |
| **Upcoming payments** | Installments due in the coming days, and the money expected each week. |
| **Student statement** | Everything billed to one student, what was paid and what is left — as a PDF you can hand to the parents. |

### Cash desk

| Report | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **End-of-day cash check** | The money each cashier took in and gave back, by day and by payment method. Use it to check the cash drawer at closing time. |
| **Refunds** | Money given back to families, who gave it back, and why. |

### Adjustments

| Report | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Discounts and reductions** | Reductions, manual discounts and waivers — the money the school decided not to collect. |

### Billing setup

| Report | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Enrolled without a plan or class** | Active students who will never be billed because they have no payment plan, or who are not placed in a class yet. Check it after each enrollment campaign. |

## Common use cases

- **End of term**: run **Pending grades** until it is empty, then generate the report cards and
  download them with **Report cards (batch)**.
- **Closing the cash desk**: open **End-of-day cash check** for today and compare it with the cash
  drawer.
- **Chasing late payments**: start from **Unpaid students**, then send a **Student statement** to the
  families concerned.
- **After enrollments**: open **Enrolled without a plan or class** to catch students you forgot to
  set up.

## Frequently asked questions

### A report is empty. Why?

Most reports fill up as the school works: grades appear once teachers enter them, attendance once it
is taken, curriculum coverage once teachers record their lessons. The **Absence watchlist** also
needs an absence limit on the level.

### Why don't I see the Reports menu?

Reports are only shown to people who have access to them. Ask an administrator.
