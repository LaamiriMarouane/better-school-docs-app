title: Audiences

An **audience** is a reusable group of recipients. Instead of listing people every time, you
define an audience once — for example *All parents* or *Year 5 students* — and reuse it across
[announcements](./announcements) and [events](./events).

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators who decide who receives announcements and events.
</Callout>

## Three types of audience

Each audience has one **Audience Type**:

| Audience Type | Who it includes |
| --- | --- |
| **Person Group** | Everyone with one **Role** — Admin, Teacher, Student, Parent, or Staff (optionally one staff role). Tick **Include All** for the whole role, or list specific people in **Include Emails**. |
| **Level Audience** | **Students**, **Guardians**, or both, in chosen **Levels** (or **All levels**). |
| **Custom** | A list of people you enter by email in **Person Emails**. |

<Callout title="Leave people out">
Person Group and Level Audience both have **Exclude Emails**, and a Level Audience also has
**Exclude Classes**. For example, *every Year 5 guardian except one class*. This lets you target
broadly and then trim.
</Callout>

## Create an audience

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Audiences

Go to **Communication → Audiences**, then choose **Add Audience**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Name it and choose its type

Give the audience a clear **Audience Name** (you'll see this name when choosing audiences later),
then pick the **Audience Type** and fill in its settings:

- **Person Group** — pick the **Role**, then tick **Include All** or enter people's email addresses
  in **Include Emails**, one per line.
- **Level Audience** — tick **Students**, **Guardians**, or both, then pick the **Levels** (or tick
  **All levels**).
- **Custom** — enter people's email addresses in **Person Emails**, one per line.
- Add exclusions if you need to leave some classes or people out.
</Step>

<Step>
### Save

Choose **Create Audience**. If some email addresses don't match anyone, you're told which ones.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Use an audience

When you create an [announcement](./announcements) or an [event](./events), choose one or more
audiences in the **Audiences** field.

## View, rename, or delete

Select an audience to see its details:

- **The people in it** — search the list. Only people with a user account appear.
- **Where it's used** — how many announcements and events use it.
- Rename it, or exclude more people by email or from the list.
- **Delete** — remove it when you no longer need it. An audience that's still used by an announcement or
  event can't be deleted.

## Common use cases

- **All families** — a *Person Group* audience with the *Parent* role and **Include All**.
- **One year group** — a *Level Audience* including the students and their guardians.
- **A committee** — a *Custom* audience for a small, fixed group.
- **A year group but one class** — a *Level Audience* with that class excluded.
