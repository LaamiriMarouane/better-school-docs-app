title: Audiences
description: Build reusable groups of recipients so you can target announcements and events without rebuilding the list each time.

An **audience** is a reusable group of recipients. Instead of picking people one by one every
time, you define an audience once — for example *All parents* or *Year 5 students* — and reuse it
across [announcements](./announcements) and [events](./events).

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators (and teachers) who decide who receives announcements and events.
</Callout>

## Three ways to define an audience

You can build an audience from any of these, and combine them:

| Building block | Who it includes |
| --- | --- |
| **By role** | Everyone of a role — all students, all teachers, all parents, and other staff roles. You can include everyone of the role or just specific people. |
| **By level** | Students and/or parents in chosen **levels** (year groups). You can include all levels or specific ones. |
| **Specific people** | A hand-picked list of individuals. |

<Callout title="Include and exclude">
Each building block supports **exceptions**. For example, *all parents except a few*, or *every
class in Year 5 except one*. This lets you target broadly and then trim.
</Callout>

## Create an audience

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Audiences

Go to **Communication → Audiences**, then choose **Add audience**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Name it and choose who's in it

Give the audience a clear **name** (you'll see this name when choosing an audience later), then
add one or more building blocks:

- Pick **roles** (e.g. *Parents*), and optionally narrow to specific people.
- Pick **levels**, and choose whether to include the **students**, their **parents**, or both.
- Add any **specific people** by name.
- Add **exclusions** if you need to leave some classes or people out.
</Step>

<Step>
### Save

The audience is saved for the academic year and ready to use.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Use an audience

When you create an [announcement](./announcements) or an [event](./events), choose your audience
in the **Audience** field. The system works out exactly who's in it at that moment and sends to
them. Changing the audience definition afterwards doesn't change who already received a past
announcement.

## Edit or delete

- **Edit** — change an audience's name or membership rules at any time.
- **Delete** — remove an audience you no longer need. An audience that's currently used by an
  announcement or event can't be deleted until it's no longer in use.

<Callout title="Audiences belong to an academic year">
Audiences are scoped to the selected academic year, because the people in a role or level change
from year to year. Build them in the year you're working in.
</Callout>

## Common use cases

- **All families** — a *Parents* role audience for school-wide notices.
- **One year group** — a *level* audience including the students and their parents.
- **A committee** — a *specific people* audience for a small, fixed group.
- **Everyone but one class** — a level audience with that class excluded.
