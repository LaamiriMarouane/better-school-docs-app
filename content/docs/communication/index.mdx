The **Communication** module is how your school keeps everyone informed and connected. You can
broadcast **announcements**, publish **events** to the school calendar, hold two-way
conversations in **chat**, decide exactly **who receives** each message with audiences, and keep
everyone up to date with **notifications**.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators and teachers who send information out, and the students and families who receive it.
</Callout>

## The pieces and how they fit

| Tool | Direction | Use it to… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Announcements** | One-way | Share news with a chosen group. People read it; they don't reply. |
| **Events** | Calendar | Put dates on the school calendar — holidays, exams, activities, closures. |
| **Chat (messaging)** | Two-way | Hold conversations: class channels, direct messages, and broadcast channels. |
| **Audiences** | Shared building block | Define *who* receives an announcement or event — by role, by level, or specific people. |
| **Notifications** | Per person | Each person sees alerts about what concerns them — in the app and on their phone. |

**Audiences** sit underneath announcements and events: when you send either one, you pick an
audience to decide who sees it. **Notifications** sit on top: once something is sent, the people
in the audience are alerted.

## Good to know

- **Audiences are reusable.** Define a group once (for example *All Year 5 parents*) and reuse it
  across announcements and events.
- **Some events close the school.** Marking a day as a **holiday** or **closure** tells the rest
  of the platform the school isn't open that day — so, for example, no bus trips are generated.
- **Class chats are automatic.** A chat channel is created for each class from its teacher
  assignments — you don't set them up by hand.
