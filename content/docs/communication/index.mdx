The **Communication** module is how your school keeps everyone informed and connected. You can
publish **announcements**, put **events** on the school calendar, hold two-way conversations in
**Messages**, decide exactly **who receives** announcements and events with audiences, and keep
everyone up to date with **notifications**.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators, who create announcements, events and audiences. Teachers, students and families,
who read announcements and events, and use Messages to stay in touch.
</Callout>

## The pieces and how they fit

| Tool | Direction | Use it to… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Announcements** | One-way | Share news with chosen audiences. People read it; they don't reply. |
| **Events** | Calendar | Put dates on the school calendar — holidays, exams, activities, closures. |
| **Messages** | Two-way | Hold conversations: class channels and direct messages. |
| **Audiences** | Shared building block | Define *who* receives an announcement or event — by role, by level, or a list of people. |
| **Notifications** | Per person | Each person sees alerts about what concerns them — in the app and on their phone. |

**Audiences** sit underneath announcements and events: when you publish either one, you pick one
or more audiences to decide who sees it. **Notifications** sit on top: once something is
published, the people in the audiences are alerted.

## Good to know

- **Audiences are reusable.** Define a group once (for example *All Year 5 guardians*) and reuse
  it across announcements and events.
- **Some events close the school.** Marking a day as a **Holiday** or **Closure** tells the rest
  of the platform the school isn't open that day — timetable sessions and bus trips skip it.
- **Class chats are automatic.** A channel is created for each class when a teacher is assigned to
  it — you don't set them up by hand.
- **Reclamations.** Students and parents can send a reclamation to the school from
  **Communication → Reclamations**, and administrators follow them up from the same menu.
