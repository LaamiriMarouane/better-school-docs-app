**Notifications** keep you up to date when something happens — a new announcement, an upcoming
event, a graded assignment, a payment due. They collect in one place so you can catch up at a glance.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Everyone. Each person sees the notifications that concern them.
</Callout>

## Where notifications appear

| Where | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **The bell** | The bell at the top of the screen shows your latest notifications and how many are unread. |
| **Notifications page** | The full list of your notifications. Open it from **View All Notifications** in the bell, or from **Communication → Notifications**. |
| **Mobile app** | Push notifications on your phone, so you're alerted even when the app is closed. |

## Read and clear notifications

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the bell

Select the **bell** to see your most recent notifications. Unread ones are highlighted.
</Step>

<Step>
### Open a notification

Select a notification to go straight to what it's about — the announcement, assignment, payment, and
so on. Use **Mark as read** to clear one without opening it.
</Step>

<Step>
### Catch up in one go

Use **Mark all read** to clear everything at once, or open **View All Notifications** to go through
the full list.
</Step>

</Steps>

## What you can be notified about

You'll only see the notifications that apply to your role. Common ones:

| Category | Notifies you about |
| --- | --- |
| **Announcements** | A new announcement published to you. |
| **Events** | A new event shared with you, and a reminder the day before it starts. |
| **Assignments & grades** | New assignments, deadlines, graded work, and published exam results. |
| **Payments** | New invoices, payments due or overdue, and confirmed, cancelled, or refunded payments. |
| **Timetable** | For administrators: the automatic timetable generation has finished. |
| **Leave** | A leave request, and when it is approved, rejected, or cancelled. |
| **Reclamations** | A new reclamation, or a change in its status. |
| **Transport** | Bus updates for parents, and vehicle and driver document expiries for administrators. |
| **Dismissal** | For teachers, a pickup requested for a student in their class. For parents, when their child is released or dismissed. |
| **Re-enrollment** | For parents, an invitation to confirm next year's re-enrollment, and the answer once given. For administrators, when a family confirms or declines. |
| **Reminders** | Reminders such as an academic or fiscal year nearing its end. |
