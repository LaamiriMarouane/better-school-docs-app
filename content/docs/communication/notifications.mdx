**Notifications** keep you up to date when something happens — a new announcement, an upcoming
event, a graded assignment, a payment due. You control **what** you're notified about and **how**
each alert reaches you.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Everyone. Each person manages their own notification settings.
</Callout>

## How you can be notified

Each kind of notification can reach you through one or more **channels**:

| Channel | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **In-app** | The notifications list (the bell) inside the app. |
| **Email** | Sent to your email address. |
| **Web push** | A pop-up from your browser, even when the app isn't open. |

## What you can be notified about

You'll only see the categories that apply to your role. Common categories:

| Category | Notifies you about |
| --- | --- |
| **Announcements** | A new announcement published to you. |
| **Events** | A new event, or a reminder before one starts. |
| **Assignments & grades** | New assignments, deadlines, graded work, and published exam results. |
| **Payments** | New invoices, payments due, and confirmed payments. |
| **Timetable** | Schedule changes — a new timetable, room changes, teacher substitutions, exam schedules. |
| **Transport** | Vehicle document expiries (for administrators). |
| **Reminders** | System reminders, such as an academic or fiscal year nearing its end. |

## Change your notification settings

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open your notification settings

Go to your **Notification settings** (in your profile or settings area).
</Step>

<Step>
### Turn categories on or off

For each category, turn notifications **on** or **off**. Turn off the ones you don't want to hear
about.
</Step>

<Step>
### Choose how you're notified

For the categories you keep on, choose which **channels** to use — in-app, email, web push, or a
combination. Save your changes.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Muting a chat is separate">
Notification settings control the platform's alerts. To silence one specific conversation, **mute**
it from within the chat instead — see [Chat and messaging](./messaging).
</Callout>

## Common use cases

- **Email for important things only** — keep payments and announcements on email, everything else
  in-app.
- **Quiet in-app only** — turn off email and web push, and check the bell when you're in the app.
- **Stay on top of grades** — keep assignments & grades on with web push so you're alerted right
  away.
