**Messaging** is for two-way conversations, unlike one-way [announcements](./announcements). It
works on web and mobile, in real time. There are two kinds of conversation, and clear rules
about who can message whom.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Teachers, students, parents, and administrators staying in touch.
</Callout>

## The two kinds of conversation

| Conversation | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Class channel** | A group chat created **automatically** for each class, from its teacher assignments. The teacher leads it and the class's students are members. |
| **Direct message** | A private one-to-one conversation between two people — allowed only between people connected through a class (see below). |

<Callout title="Class channels set themselves up">
You don't create class channels by hand. When a teacher is assigned to a class, a channel is
created with the teacher as owner and the students as members. Students are added or removed as
they enrol, transfer, or leave, and old channels are archived when the year ends.
</Callout>

## Who can message whom

Direct messages are limited to people who share a real connection, to keep messaging safe:

- **Teachers** can message the **students they teach** and **those students' parents**.
- **Teachers** can message **other teachers** they share a class with.
- **Administrators** can reach staff and families more broadly.
- **Students cannot** directly message other students.
- **Parents cannot** message other parents, or students who aren't their own children.

If you can't start a direct message with someone, it's because you don't share a class connection
that allows it.

## Start a conversation

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Messaging

Go to **Messaging** (or **Communication → Messages**). Your conversations are listed down the
side; open one to read and reply.
</Step>

<Step>
### Start a direct message

Choose **New conversation**, then pick the person. You'll only see people you're allowed to
message.
</Step>

</Steps>

## In a conversation

- **Send messages** and **attach files** or images.
- **Reply** to a specific message to keep a thread clear.
- **React** with an emoji.
- **Mention** someone with **@** to get their attention.
- **Edit** or **delete** a message you sent — edited messages keep a history.
- **Read receipts** show when your message has been read.
- **Typing indicators** show when someone is writing.

## Manage a conversation

- **Report a message** — flag an inappropriate message for moderators to review.

## Common use cases

- **A parent's question** — a parent messages their child's teacher directly.
- **Class updates** — a teacher posts in the class channel; all the class's students see it.
