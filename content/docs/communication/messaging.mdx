**Messages** is for two-way conversations, unlike one-way [announcements](./announcements). It
works on web and mobile. There are two kinds of conversation, and clear rules about who can
message whom.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Teachers, students, parents, and administrators staying in touch.
</Callout>

## The two kinds of conversation

| Conversation | Where you find it | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Class channel** | **Channels** tab | A group chat created **automatically** for each class and teacher. The teacher leads it and the class's students are members. |
| **Direct message** | **Personal** tab | A private one-to-one conversation between two people. |

<Callout title="Class channels set themselves up">
You don't create class channels by hand. When a teacher is assigned to a class, a channel is
created with the teacher and the class's students. Students are added or removed as they join or
leave the class.
</Callout>

## Who can message whom

Direct messages are limited to keep messaging safe:

- **Teachers** can message any teacher, the **students they teach**, and **those students'
  parents**.
- **Students** can message **their teachers**. They can't message other students or parents.
- **Parents** can message **their children's teachers**. They can't message students or other
  parents.
- **Administrators** can message teachers, students, parents, and staff.

Anyone can reply when an administrator starts a conversation with them. You only see the people
you're allowed to message.

## Start a conversation

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Messages

Go to **Communication → Messages**. Your conversations are listed in the **Personal** and
**Channels** tabs; open one to read and reply.
</Step>

<Step>
### Start a direct message

Choose **New message** and search for the person by name. If you can message several groups, first
pick one — for example **Teachers** or **Parents**.
</Step>

</Steps>

## In a conversation

- **Send messages** and **attach files** or images.
- **React** with an emoji, and **mention** someone with **@** to get their attention.
- See who's typing, and open **Who read this** to see who has read your message.
- Open **View participants** or **Shared files** from the conversation header.

Right-click a message, or press and hold it on a touch screen, to:

- **Reply** to it, to keep a thread clear.
- **Copy** its text.
- **Edit** or **Delete** a message you sent. Edited messages keep their history — use **View edit
  history** to see earlier versions.
- **Report message** to flag a message someone else sent as inappropriate.

## Common use cases

- **A parent's question** — a parent messages their child's teacher directly.
- **Class updates** — a teacher posts in the class channel; all the class's students see it.
