A **reclamation** is a complaint sent to the school — about a grade, a fee, or anything else.
Parents (and students, if the school allows it) submit it and follow its status. Administrators
take it in charge and close it with a note the family can read.

<Callout title="Module required">
Reclamations need the **Reclamations** module. If you don't see them, ask whoever manages your
school's modules to turn it on.
</Callout>

## Statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Sent, not handled yet. |
| **In progress** | An administrator has taken it in charge. |
| **Resolved** | Closed, with a note explaining how it was resolved. |
| **Rejected** | Closed, with a note explaining why it was rejected. |

## Types

| Type | For… |
| --- | --- |
| **Grade issue** | A question or disagreement about a grade. |
| **Fee issue** | A question or problem with a fee or a payment. |
| **General** | A general school matter. |
| **Other** | Anything else. |

## Let students submit reclamations

Parents can always submit while the module is on. Students can submit only if you allow it.

Go to **Settings → School**, open the **Reclamations** tab, and turn on **Students can create
reclamations**. When it is off, students see a message telling them to contact a parent or the
administration.

## Submit a reclamation (parents and students)

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Reclamations

On the web, go to **Communication → Reclamations**. In the mobile app, open **Reclamations** from
the menu.
</Step>

<Step>
### Pick the child (parents)

A reclamation is about one child. Select that child in the child switcher first — the form shows
which child it is for.
</Step>

<Step>
### Fill in the form

Click **New reclamation**, then choose the **Type** and enter a **Title** and a **Description** of
the issue.
</Step>

<Step>
### Submit

Click **Submit**. The reclamation appears in your list as **Pending**.
</Step>

</Steps>

Your list shows each reclamation with its status. When the school answers, you get a notification,
and the reclamation shows who handled it and the **Resolution note**.

## Handle reclamations (administrators)

Go to **Communication → Reclamations**. The list shows every reclamation with who submitted it.
Use the search box, **All statuses** and **All types** to narrow it down. Click a reclamation to
see the details: the **Student**, who it was **Submitted by**, who **Handled by**, the
**Message**, and its **History**.

| Action | When | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Take in charge** | **Pending** | It moves to **In progress**, with your name as the handler. |
| **Resolve** | **In progress** | Enter a **Resolution note**, then confirm. It moves to **Resolved**. |
| **Reject** | **Pending** or **In progress** | Enter a **Resolution note** saying why, then confirm. It moves to **Rejected**. |
| Delete | Any time | Removes the reclamation for good, after you confirm. |

You get a notification when a new reclamation arrives, and the family is notified when its status
changes.
