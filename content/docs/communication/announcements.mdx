An **announcement** is a one-way message you broadcast to a chosen group — news, reminders, or
notices. People in the audience read it; they don't reply (for two-way conversations, use
[Chat and messaging](./messaging)).

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators and teachers who share news with students and families.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- Decide **who should receive** the announcement. You can pick an existing
  [audience](./audiences) or build one as you go.

## Create an announcement

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Announcements

Go to **Communication → Announcements**, then choose **Add announcement**.

<Screenshot
  label="Announcements list with the Add announcement button"
  caption="Your school's announcements are listed here."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Write the announcement

Complete the fields below.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Title** | Yes | A short headline. |
| **Content** | Yes | The body of the announcement. You can format it — headings, bold, lists, and links. |
| **Audience** | Yes | Who receives it. Pick at least one [audience](./audiences). |
| **Attachments** | No | Files to share alongside the message. |
| **Thumbnail** | No | A cover image shown with the announcement. |

<Screenshot
  label="Create announcement form"
  caption="Title, rich-text content, audience, and optional attachments."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Save as draft or publish

- **Save as draft** to keep working on it — only staff can see drafts.
- **Publish** to send it. The announcement becomes visible to its audience, and the people in
  that audience are [notified](./notifications).
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Publishing is immediate">
An announcement goes live the moment you **publish** it — there's no scheduled or timed release.
If you need it to appear later, publish it at that time.
</Callout>

## Edit, unpublish, or delete

- **Edit** — open an announcement to change its title, content, audience, or attachments.
- **Unpublish** — hide a published announcement again (for example if it was sent by mistake). It
  returns to draft and is no longer visible to the audience.
- **Delete** — remove an announcement entirely.

## Who sees an announcement

Only the people in the announcement's **audience** see it, on web and mobile. Choose the audience
carefully — for example *all parents*, *Year 5 students*, or a few specific people. See
[Audiences](./audiences).

## Common use cases

- **School-wide notice** — publish to an audience that includes everyone.
- **Class reminder** — a teacher publishes to the parents of one class.
- **Draft now, publish later** — prepare the announcement as a draft and publish it when the time
  is right.
