---
title: Release students as a teacher
description: "See which of your students' families have arrived, and release them from class."
---

When a family checks in, the teachers of the child's class get a notification and the child appears
on their **Dismissal** page. The teacher sends the child out with **Release**.

<Callout title="Where to find it">
Teachers open **Dismissal** in their menu. It appears when the **Dismissal** module is on.
</Callout>

## Release your students

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Dismissal

Open **Dismissal**. The page shows the students of your classes whose family has arrived, with
three counters: **To dismiss**, **On the way** and **Handed off**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Release

Click **Release** on a student's row when they leave the classroom. The status becomes **On the
way** and the parent is told the child is coming.

To send everyone out at once, click **Release all waiting**.
</Step>

</Steps>

Students already handed off are grouped under **Done**. Click it to open the list.

The page updates on its own. Use the sound button to hear a sound when a student is released, and
**Full screen** to hide the menus.

<Callout title="No release step at your school?">
If your school has turned off **Require classroom release**, there is nothing to release: gate
staff hand the child over directly. The page still shows who is being collected.
</Callout>
