---
title: Run dismissal at the gate
description: "Follow the pickup queue live, check families in by student code, release students and hand them off at the gate."
---

The **Dismissal** page is the live pickup queue for office and gate staff. It updates on its own
as families check in, teachers release students, and staff hand them off. Show it on a classroom
TV or a tablet at the gate.

<Callout title="Where to find it">
Administrators: **Operations → Dismissal**. Staff: **Dismissal** in their menu, when their role
allows it. Needs the **Dismissal** module.
</Callout>

## Read the page

At the top, three counters show **To dismiss** (waiting for release), **On the way** and
**Handed off** (for the whole day).

Each row shows the student, their class, the gate, the arrival number (for example **#12**), and
how many children came in the same arrival. Staff also see the adult who checked in and the
student's authorized pickups with their phone numbers — use them to check the adult at the gate.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Zones** / **List** | Show one column per gate, or a single list. |
| **All gates** | Show only the gates you pick (when the school has more than one). |
| Search | Filter by student, class or code. |
| Sound button | Play a sound when a student is released, or mute it. |
| **Full screen** | Hide the menus — for a TV or a gate tablet. **Exit full screen** brings them back. |

After a handoff, the name stays on the board for the time set in **Board auto-remove**, then
leaves. The **Handed off** counter keeps counting.

When pickup is closed, a notice says why — the school is closed today, no window is set for today,
or today's window is at another time. Administrators get a **Set pickup windows** link.

## Check a family in at the gate

Use this when a family arrives without the app.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Type the student code

Type the student's code (or just its number) in the search box. The student appears above the
queue.
</Step>

<Step>
### Choose the gate

Pick the gate in **Choose a gate**. With only one gate, it is chosen for you.
</Step>

<Step>
### Check in

Click **Check in**. The student joins the queue as **Waiting for release**, and the student's
teachers are notified.
</Step>

</Steps>

If the student is already in today's queue, the page says so. A student who takes the bus today
can only be checked in at the gate by staff allowed to override, with the override switch on (see
below).

### Override

Staff whose role allows it see the **Override (bus rider, outside window, or unlisted adult)**
switch. Turn it on to check a child in outside the pickup window, a bus rider, or for an adult who
is not on the list. Overrides are recorded in the [history](./dismissal-history). An adult marked
**May not collect** can never collect the child, even with an override.

## Release, hand off or cancel

Each row shows the buttons that apply to it:

| Button | When | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Release** | **Waiting for release**, when classroom release is required | The child leaves class. Status becomes **On the way** and the parent is notified. |
| **Handoff** | **On the way** — or straight from **Waiting for release** when classroom release isn't required | The child is with the adult. Status becomes **Handed off** and the parent is notified. |
| **Cancel** | **Waiting for release** | The check-in is removed from the queue. |

<Callout title="When the window ends">
Children still waiting or on the way are marked **No-show** at the end of the day (in the
evening), and leave the queue. Until then they stay on the board after the window ends.
</Callout>
