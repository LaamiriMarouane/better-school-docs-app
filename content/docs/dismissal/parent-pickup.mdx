title: Pick up your child

---

When you arrive to collect your child, tap **I'm here**. The school sees your arrival straight
away, sends your child out, and you follow each step on your phone.

<Callout title="Where to find it">
In the parent portal, open **Pickup**. In the mobile app, open **Dismissal** from the menu. It
appears when your school uses the **Dismissal** module.
</Callout>

## Check in when you arrive

<Steps>

<Step>
### Check that pickup is open

On the web, the top of the page shows today's pickup: **Pickup open until** a time, **Opens at** a
time, or **Closed for today**. You can only check in while pickup is open.
</Step>

<Step>
### Choose who you're picking up

Under **Who are you picking up?** (**Who is being collected?** in the mobile app), tap each child
you're collecting. You can pick several children in one arrival.
</Step>

<Step>
### Choose your gate

If the school has more than one gate, choose the one where you're waiting in **Choose a gate**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Tap I'm here

Tap **I'm here**. The school and your child's teachers are told you have arrived.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Follow your child

Each child you checked in shows a card with your queue number, the gate, and three steps:

| Step | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Waiting** | The school knows you're here. Your child will be sent out soon. |
| **On the way** | Your child has left class and is coming to the gate. |
| **Handed off** | Your child has been handed over to you. |

In the mobile app, each child shows the same progress as a status: **Waiting for release**,
**On the way**, then **Handed off**. Pull down to refresh.

You also get a notification when your child is on the way and when they are handed off. On the
web, children collected today are listed under **Done today**.

While your child is still **Waiting**, you can call off the check-in with **Cancel check-in**
(**Cancel** in the mobile app), then confirm.

## When you can't check in

| You see | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **On the bus today — contact the school to collect at the gate.** | Your child takes the school bus today. Call the school if you want to collect them yourself. |
| **Parent check-in is turned off for this school.** | Your school checks families in at the gate instead. |
| **The school has not set a pickup gate yet.** | The school hasn't finished setting up pickup. |
| **No children are linked to this account.** | Ask the school to link your children to your account. |

If the school has told you that you may not collect a child, you won't be able to check in for
that child.
