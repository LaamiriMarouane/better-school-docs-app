---
title: Set up dismissal
description: "Set the weekly pickup windows, the gates families use, how families check in, and who may collect each child."
---

Dismissal is set up in **Settings → Academic**, on the **Dismissal** tab. You set when pickup is
open, which gates families use, and how check-in and handoff work. Then, on each student's
profile, you can list the adults allowed to collect the child.

<Callout title="Module required">
The **Dismissal** tab appears only when the **Dismissal** module is on. See
[Configure school modules](../getting-started/first-time-school-setup/configure-school-modules).
</Callout>

<Callout title="Set it again for each new year">
The tab shows the settings of the academic year selected at the top of the page. When you start a
new year, open the tab with that year selected and set the windows again.
</Callout>

## Before you start

Set your working days and school hours in **Settings → Academic → School Schedule** first (see
[School schedule](../settings/school-schedule)). Pickup windows can only be added on working days,
and their times must stay inside school hours.

## Weekly windows

A **window** is the time when pickup is open on a given day — for example 16:00 to 17:00 on
Monday. Families can tap **I'm here** only inside a window.

1. Under **Weekly windows**, click **Add window** on a day.
2. Set **Starts** and **Ends**. The end must be after the start.
3. Leave **Active** on, then **Save** the window.

A day can have more than one window (for example a midday pickup on Wednesdays). Use the switch on
a window to turn it off without deleting it, or delete it. Days that are not school days show
**Not a school day**.

When you're done, click **Save** at the top of the tab.

## Pickup zones

**Pickup zones** are the gates or car lanes families use. When a parent checks in, they choose
the gate where they are waiting, and the live page can show one column per gate.

1. Under **Pickup zones**, click **Add zone**.
2. Enter a **Name** (for example *Main gate*). **Code** and **Color** are optional; the color is
   used on the live board.
3. Leave **Active** on and click **Create**.

If you have only one active zone, families don't have to pick a gate — it is chosen for them. To
stop using a gate, edit it and turn **Active** off. A zone still in use may not be deletable;
make it inactive instead.

## Check-in and handoff

Tick the options your school uses, then click **Save** at the top of the tab.

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Parent app check-in** | Parents can tap **I'm here** in the parent portal and the mobile app. When off, parents see that parent check-in is turned off. |
| **Student code check-in** | Gate staff can check a child in by typing the student number (for example *1* becomes *STU-00001*). |
| **Require classroom release** | On: a teacher or staff member must **Release** the child from class before the gate **Handoff**. Off: staff use one **Handoff** button. |
| **Board auto-remove (seconds)** | How long a handed-off name stays on the live board. **0** keeps names until the window closes. |

## Authorized pickups

Each student has an **Authorized pickups** tab on their profile. Gate staff see these names and
phone numbers on the student's row in the queue, so they can check the adult at the gate.

1. Go to **Academic → Students** and open the student.
2. Open the **Authorized pickups** tab. The student's **Guardians** are listed first.
3. To add someone else, click **Add person** and enter the **Full name**, and optionally the
   **Phone**, **Relationship**, **Valid from** and **Valid until** dates. Click **Save**.

To stop an adult from collecting the child, click **May not collect** and choose the person — one
of the student's guardians, or someone who is not a guardian. That adult can never collect the
child, even with an override, and a guardian marked this way can't check in from the parent app.
Parents don't see this entry.
