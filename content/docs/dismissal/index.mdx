**Dismissal** organises the end of the school day. A family announces it has arrived, the child
is released from class, and staff hand the child over at the gate. Everyone follows the same
queue live: office and gate staff on the **Dismissal** page, teachers on their own **Dismissal**
page, and parents on **Pickup**.

<Callout title="Module required">
Dismissal needs the **Dismissal** module. If you don't see it, ask whoever manages your school's
modules to turn it on.
</Callout>

## How a pickup works

<Steps>

<Step>
### The family checks in

During the pickup window, a parent taps **I'm here** in the parent portal or the mobile app and
chooses the gate. Gate staff can also check a child in by typing the student code on the
**Dismissal** page.
</Step>

<Step>
### The child is released from class

The child's teacher (or a staff member) clicks **Release**. The parent sees **On the way**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Staff hand the child over

At the gate, staff check the adult and click **Handoff**. The parent sees **Handed off**.
</Step>

</Steps>

If your school turns off **Require classroom release**, there is no release step: staff use a
single **Handoff** button straight from the queue.

## Statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Waiting for release** | The family has checked in; the child is still in class. |
| **On the way** | The child has been released and is going to the gate. |
| **Handed off** | The child has been handed to the adult. |
| **Cancelled** | The check-in was called off before the child was released. |
| **No-show** | The child was not handed off by the end of the day. The row leaves the queue in the evening. |

## Who does what

| Role | Where | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Administrator | **Settings → Academic → Dismissal** | Set pickup windows, gates and check-in options. |
| Administrator, gate staff | **Operations → Dismissal** (staff: **Dismissal**) | Check families in, release, hand off, cancel. Administrators also open the history. |
| Teacher | **Dismissal** | Release their students when the family arrives. |
| Parent | **Pickup** (web) or **Dismissal** (mobile app) | Tap **I'm here** and follow the child until the handoff. |

What a staff member can do on the **Dismissal** page depends on the permissions of their role.
