**Dismissal history** lists everything that happened at pickup: check-ins, releases, handoffs,
cancellations, no-shows and overrides, plus changes to the dismissal settings, pickup zones and
authorized pickups.

## Open the history

The history is for administrators. On the **Dismissal** page (**Operations → Dismissal**), click
the history button next to the counters. You can also find **Dismissal history** with the search
bar.

## Use the list

Each line shows **When**, **Who** did it, their **Role**, the **Action**, the **Entity** (for
example the student) and a **Description**.

- Type in the search box to find a student or a person.
- Click **Export CSV** to download the list (when your role allows exporting).
