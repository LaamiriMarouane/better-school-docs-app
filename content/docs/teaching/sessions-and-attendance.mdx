A **class session** is one real meeting of a class, created from the [timetable](../timetable).
Teachers see their sessions in the **Activity Journal** and on the **timetable** page, where they
record what was covered and take **attendance**.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Teachers running their classes day to day, and administrators overseeing attendance.
</Callout>

## Where to find your sessions

Sessions appear in two places, and you can open one and **take attendance** from either:

- **Activity Journal** — your running list of sessions. Administrators see every class's sessions;
  teachers see their own. This is where the daily teaching record lives — you log what happened in a
  session here.
- **Timetable** — sessions also show on the timetable page, so you can open the session for a slot
  and take its attendance straight from the schedule.

## Session statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Scheduled** | An upcoming session from the timetable. |
| **In progress** | The session is happening now. |
| **Completed** | The session is finished and the teacher has logged it. |
| **Auto-completed** | A past session the system closed automatically because it wasn't logged in time. |
| **Cancelled** | The session was called off. |
| **No-show** | The class didn't take place. |

If the usual teacher is replaced for one session, a **substitute teacher** can be recorded on it.

## Log what was taught

Open a session and record what happened:

- The **lesson** that was taught.
- The **topics** covered.
- Any **homework** set.
- **Notes** and **attachments** — observations or files for the record.

This builds a clear history of what each class has covered.

## Take attendance

How attendance is taken depends on your school's **attendance mode**, set once for the school:

| Mode | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Per day** | Attendance is taken **once a day** for the whole class — usually by an administrator. |
| **Per period** | Attendance is taken **in each session**, by the teacher. |

See [Configure attendance modes](../getting-started/configure-core-operations/configure-attendance-modes).

### Attendance statuses

For each student, mark:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Present** | The student attended. |
| **Absent** | The student didn't attend. |
| **Late** | The student arrived late. |
| **Half day** | The student attended part of the day. |

You can mark a whole class at once and add a **remark** to any student. Attendance can be entered
for today and a few recent days, within a window your school sets — so a missed session can still be
recorded shortly after.

## Common use cases

- **Daily teaching log** — after class, record the topic and homework so families can follow along.
- **Mark the register** — take attendance each period (or once a day, depending on your mode).
- **Cover lesson** — record the substitute teacher on the session.
- **Backfill** — enter yesterday's attendance within the allowed window.
