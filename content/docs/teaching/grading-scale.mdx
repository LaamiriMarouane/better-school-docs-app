title: Grading scale
description: Set up the school's grading scale and its grade bands so numeric scores are shown as letter grades, mentions and GPA values on the gradebook and report cards.

The **grading scale** is a set of **grade bands** that turn a numeric score into a letter grade,
a mention (e.g. *Bien*, *Très bien*) or a GPA value, and say which scores count as a pass. The
school has one grading scale. It is used everywhere a grade is displayed — the gradebook, the
student view and the report card.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators configuring how grades are displayed.
</Callout>

## How the grading scale works

Each band covers a range of scores. A grade falls into the band that contains it, and the band's
letter, mention and GPA can be shown alongside (or instead of) the number, depending on the
display format you choose.

**Example — a /20 scale:**

| Range | Letter | Mention | GPA | Passing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 18 – 20 | A+ | Excellent | 4.0 | Yes |
| 16 – 18 | A | Très bien | 4.0 | Yes |
| 14 – 16 | B | Bien | 3.0 | Yes |
| 12 – 14 | C | Assez bien | 2.0 | Yes |
| 10 – 12 | D | Passable | 1.0 | Yes |
| 0 – 10 | F | Insuffisant | 0.0 | No |

You do not need to fill in every column — for example, you can use mentions without GPA values,
or letter grades without mentions.

## Edit the grading scale

Go to **Assessments → Grading setup → Year policy**. The **Grading scale** card is edited directly
on the page, and its range goes from 0 to the **Display scale** set in the
**Grade scale & rounding** card (e.g. 20 or 100).

<Steps>

<Step>
### Add bands

Click **Add band**. The new band is cut out of the lowest band, so the scale always covers the
whole range. The first band always starts at 0 and the last band always ends at the maximum.
</Step>

<Step>
### Adjust the ranges

In **Range**, change where a band starts or ends. The neighbouring band follows automatically, so
bands stay side by side. Deleting a band gives its range to the band next to it.
</Step>

<Step>
### Fill in each band

| Column | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Letter** | Optional. A letter code, e.g. *A*. |
| **Mention** | Optional. A descriptive label, e.g. *Très bien*. |
| **GPA** | Optional. The GPA value for this band. |
| **Passing** | Turn on for the bands that count as a pass. |

Each band also has a colour, used for its grade chips.
</Step>

<Step>
### Save

Click **Save** at the top of the page. The scale applies right away wherever grades are shown.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="No gaps or overlaps">
The scale can't be saved while there is a gap or an overlap between bands, or while the bands
don't run from 0 to the maximum.
</Callout>

## Test a grade

Under the bands, type a value in **Test a grade** to preview it. You see **Pass** or **Fail** and
the band's letter, mention and GPA — or **No matching band**. Use it to check the scale before
saving.

## Display format

The grading scale sets what *can* be shown. The **Primary format** sets what is shown by default
to students and on reports. In **Assessments → Grading setup → Year policy**, open the
**Display formats** card and choose:

| Primary format | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Numeric** | The score (e.g. 14.5). |
| **Percentage** | The score as a percentage (e.g. 72.5%). |
| **Letter** | The band's letter (e.g. B). |
| **Mention** | The band's mention (e.g. Bien). |
| **GPA** | The band's GPA value (e.g. 3.0). |

A level or a marking period can use a different primary format. See
[Marking periods and grading scheme](./marking-periods).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to fill in letters or mentions to use the gradebook?

No. The scale always has at least one band covering 0 to the maximum. Letters, mentions and GPA
values are optional; without them, grades are shown as numbers.

### Can I use letter grades only, without numeric scores?

Teachers always enter scores as numbers. Set the **Primary format** to **Letter** and give every
band a letter to show grades as letters.

### What decides whether a grade is a pass?

The band the grade falls into: bands with **Passing** turned on count as a pass. The
**Pass threshold** in **Display formats** is used only when no band applies.

### Can students see the grading scale?

Students and parents see their grade in the configured primary format. The full band table is
not shown to them.

### Can a level use a different grading scale?

No — the school has one grading scale. A level or a marking period can still use its own primary
format and pass threshold. See [Marking periods and grading scheme](./marking-periods).
