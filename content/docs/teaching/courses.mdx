title: Course templates

A **course template** is the outline for one **subject at one level** (and, for levels with tracks,
one track). It's organised into **chapters**, and each chapter holds **lessons**. Every class at that
level follows the same template, so the structure stays consistent; teachers then add their own
[lesson content](./lessons) on top.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators and teachers who plan what's taught in each subject.
</Callout>

## How a template is organised

```
Course template  (a subject, at a level — or a track)
└─ Chapter
   └─ Lesson
      └─ Lesson content  (written by the teacher — see Lessons)
```

For a level with tracks, a subject can have one template per track, or a single template marked
**Common (all tracks)** that every track follows.

## Before you start

- The subject must be **assigned to the level**. See
  [Build the academic foundation](../getting-started/build-the-academic-foundation).

## Build a template

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Course Templates

Go to **Academic** → **Course Templates**, then click **Choose a subject**. Pick a term to filter
the list, search by level or subject, and select the level subject you want. Its outline opens. You
can change subject at any time with **Switch subject** in the header.

<Screenshot
  label="Courses list"
  caption="Each course is a subject at a level, organised into chapters and lessons."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Add chapters

Click **Add chapter**, give it a title, and save. Add chapters in the order they're taught.
</Step>

<Step>
### Add lessons

Click **Add lesson** on a chapter and fill in:

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Chapter** | Yes | The chapter the lesson goes in. |
| **Title** | Yes | The lesson name. |
| **Lesson Type** | Yes | Theory, practical, lab, exercise, review, project, and so on. |
| **Duration (Weeks)** | No | How many weeks the lesson takes — a whole number, at least 1. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Reorder and refine

Drag chapters and lessons to put them in order. Click a lesson to open its details panel, where you
can change the title, type, and duration, and add a **Description**, **Prerequisites**,
**Objectives**, and **Activities**. Click **Save** to keep your changes.
</Step>

</Steps>

There's no publish step: what you save is what the classes see.

## When the outline is frozen

Once a class has recorded progress on a template, you'll see an **Outline frozen** notice. From then
on the order of chapters and lessons stays fixed and you can't add or remove them, but titles,
descriptions, and lesson details can still be edited.

## Who can edit a template

Administrators can edit every template. A teacher can edit a template when an administrator has given
them a **supervision** that covers the subject (in the teacher's profile, **Supervisions** tab):

| Supervision permission | What the teacher can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit curriculum** | Add and edit chapters, lessons, and lesson details. |
| **Manage curriculum** | Everything above, plus delete chapters. |

Everyone else can browse the template but not change it — the page shows a read-only notice.

<Callout title="The template is not the lesson content">
Supervision controls the **outline**. The **lesson content** a teacher writes for their own class is
separate, and comes from teaching the subject. See [Lessons](./lessons).
</Callout>

## Common use cases

- **New subject outline** — choose the subject, add chapters, then lessons.
- **Same outline, several classes** — build it once for the level; every class follows it.
- **Tracks with a shared programme** — use **Common (all tracks)** instead of repeating the outline.
