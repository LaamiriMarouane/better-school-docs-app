The **gradebook** is where every mark a student earns comes together. Results from
[exams](./exams), [assignments](./assignments), and [quizzes](./quizzes) are grouped into
**categories**, weighted, and combined into each student's grade for a subject.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Teachers and administrators managing grades, and students and parents viewing them.
</Callout>

## Where grades come from

A student's subject grade is built from the marked work across the term:

- **Exam** results,
- **Assignment** grades,
- **Quiz** scores.

Each of these is placed in a **grade category**.

## Grade categories

A **grade category** groups assessments of a kind — for example *Exams*, *Assignments*, *Quizzes* —
and gives each group a **weight**. You can:

- set how much each category **counts** toward the final grade (its weight),
- mark a category as **not counting**, when it's for information only,
- define categories for the whole school or per **level**.

## How a grade is worked out

1. Each piece of work is marked out of its own maximum.
2. Marks are grouped and averaged **within each category**.
3. The category averages are combined by **weight** into the **subject grade** for the term.
4. Term grades combine into the **year grade**.

Grades use your school's **grading scale** and **rounding**, set in the academic year's gradebook
settings — for example, marks out of 20, rounded to one decimal place.

## Grade statuses

In the gradebook grid, each cell shows where a student stands:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Graded** | A mark has been recorded. |
| **Pending** | The work isn't marked yet. |
| **Absent** | The student was absent for the assessment. |
| **Exempt** | The student is excused from it. |

## The gradebook views

- **Class gradebook** — a grid of students against subjects, showing each subject grade.
- **Assignment gradebook** — a grid of students against assignments and quizzes, where you enter
  grades quickly.
- **Student view** — what a student (and their parents) see: their grade per subject, the breakdown
  by category, and their class rank.

## Adjust a grade

- **Override** — set a student's subject grade by hand, with a reason, when the calculated grade
  shouldn't apply.
- **Remark** — add a note about a student's performance without changing the grade.

## Report cards

You can produce a student's **report card** — their grades per subject for the term or year — ready
to share with families.
