---
title: Exams
description: Create exam templates, schedule exams for classes, grade them, publish results, and record exam attendance.
---

Exams work in two parts. An **exam template** is the blueprint — its name, length, maximum score,
and how much it counts. A **scheduled exam** is an actual sitting of that template for a class, on a
date, in a room. You build the template once, then schedule it for each class.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators and teachers who set, schedule, and grade exams.
</Callout>

## Step 1 — Create exam templates

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the subject's exams

Go to **Assessments → Grading setup** and open the **Subjects** step. Choose the **Term**, pick a
subject in the list, choose the **Marking period**, then open the **Exams** tab.
</Step>

<Step>
### Add the exams

<Callout title="Exam categories come first">
Exams are grouped by exam category. If the subject has none yet, add an exam category with its
weight in the **Categories** tab first.
</Callout>

For each exam category, choose **Add Exam** and fill in one row per exam:

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The exam's name, e.g. *Mid-term*. |
| **Duration** | 30 min, 45 min, 1 h, 1 h 30, 2 h, 2 h 30, or 3 h. |
| **Max Score** | The score the exam is marked out of. |
| **Weight** | How much this exam counts within its category. |

The weights in a category must total 100% before you can **Save exams**. The **Total** at the
bottom shows where you are, and each row shows roughly what share of the term that exam is worth.

For example, if the *Exams* category is worth 60% of the subject and holds a *Mid-term* at 40% and
a *Final* at 60%, the mid-term is worth about 24% of the term (40% of 60%).
</Step>

<Step>
### Reuse the set (optional)

Use **Copy to other subjects** to give other subjects the same exams instead of typing them again,
or **Copy from last year** to bring back last year's categories and exams for subjects not set up yet.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Step 2 — Schedule the exam

Go to **Scheduled Exams** — under **Assessments** for administrators, under **Academic** for
teachers — and choose **Schedule Exam**.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Term** | Yes | The term of the exam. |
| **Level** | Yes | Narrows the class list. |
| **Class** | Yes | Who sits it. |
| **Subject** | Yes | The subject being examined. |
| **Marking period** | — | Narrows the templates to one marking period. |
| **Exam Template** | Yes | Which exam to sit. |
| **Scheduled Date** | Yes | When the exam takes place. |
| **Room** | No | Where it's held. The list shows each room's capacity. |

## Step 3 — Mark the exam as completed

Once the exam date has passed, choose **Mark as Completed** on the exam in the list. Grading opens
only after that.

## Step 4 — Grade the exam

Choose **Grade Exam** on the exam. The page has two tabs:

- **Grade entry** — enter each student's score and an optional note, then **Save Results**.
- **Attendance** — record who sat the exam, then **Save Attendance**.

**Export PDF** produces a printable sheet of the grades. You choose the **Display Order** (**By
Name**, **By Rank** or **By Student Number**), the **Language**, and whether to **Include Teacher
Remarks**.

## Step 5 — Publish the results

When marking is done, choose **Publish grades** on the grading page, or **Publish Results** on the
exam in the list. The list shows each exam as **Published** or **Not published**. Use **Unpublish
Results** to hide them again.

<Callout title="Results stay private until you publish">
Scores you enter are not visible to students or families until you publish the results. This lets
you finish marking before anyone sees a grade.
</Callout>

## Exam attendance

You can record attendance from the **Attendance** tab, or with **Mark Attendance** on the exam in
the list. For each student, choose:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Present** | The student sat the exam. |
| **Absent** | The student didn't attend — add the reason. |
| **Late** | The student arrived late. |
| **Excused** | An approved absence — add the reason. |

A reason is required for every **Absent** and **Excused** student.

## Exam statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Scheduled** | Set up, not yet taken. |
| **Completed** | The exam has taken place and can be graded. |

## Common use cases

- **Termly exams** — add the term's exams with weights that add up to 100%, then schedule each one
  per class.
- **Same exams across subjects** — build the set once and use **Copy to other subjects**.
- **Absent student** — mark them **Absent** (or **Excused**) with a reason in the exam attendance.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between an exam template and a scheduled exam?

An **exam template** is the blueprint — its name, duration, maximum score, and weight within its
category. A **scheduled exam** is one actual sitting of that template: a specific class, date, and
room. You create the template once and can schedule it for many classes without redefining it.

### Can the same template be used for multiple classes?

Yes. Schedule the template once per class. Each scheduled exam has its own grades, attendance
record, and publication state, independently of the others.

### Why can't I enter grades yet?

The exam must be marked as completed first, and that is only possible once its date has passed.
Choose **Mark as Completed**, then **Grade Exam**.

### When should I publish results?

Publish once you have finished marking and are confident in the grades. There is no time limit —
you can publish right after marking or wait until results for all classes are ready.

### Do exam scores feed into the gradebook automatically?

Yes. Once the results are published, they count in the [gradebook](./gradebook) under the
template's marking period, category, and weight. No manual transfer is needed.
