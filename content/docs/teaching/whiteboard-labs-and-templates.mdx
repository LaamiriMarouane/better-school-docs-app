Beyond drawing and writing, a [board](./whiteboard) comes with ready-made content you can drop on
it: maths **manipulatives**, **science** content, class **games**, and full **lab pages** with
experiments. Each one opens from a button on the board's toolbar.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Teachers building boards for their lessons.
</Callout>

## Add an item to the board

Open a panel from the toolbar, then **tap** an item to place it on the board, or **drag** it to
where you want it. Once on the board, an item works like any object: select it to move, resize or
remove it, and use the controls on the item itself to change it.

| Panel | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Manipulatives** | Maths tools and shapes. |
| **Science** | The periodic table, molecules and chemistry lab equipment. |
| **Games** | Games to play with the whole class. |
| **Library** | The items and labs you saved yourself. |

## Manipulatives

| Section | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| **Geometry** | **Ruler**, **Protractor**, angles, triangles, quadrilaterals, circles, and your own polygons. |
| **Solids** | Cube, cylinder, cone, pyramid, sphere. |
| **Fractions** | Fraction bars and circles. |
| **Algebra tiles** | Positive and negative tiles. |
| **Time** | A clock, a **Timer** and a stopwatch. |
| **Chance** | A **Spinner**, including a yes / no spinner. |
| **Scale** | A balance with weights, including an unknown *x*. |
| **Coordinates** | A **Coordinate plane**, a **Formula** to draw on it, and a **Vector**. |
| **Trigonometry** | The unit circle, in degrees or with π. |

## Science

- **Elements** — the periodic table, or a part of it such as the first 20 elements.
- **Molecules** — common molecules and compounds, which you can search by name or formula.
- **Lab equipment** — glassware, heating, liquids, solid chemicals, and tools and supports.

Use **What the class sees** to hide answers when you want to ask the class questions.

## Games

| Game | How it plays |
| --- | --- |
| **Flashcards** | Tap a card to turn it over, swipe for the next one. |
| **Linking cards** | Link each card to its match, then check. |
| **Memory** | Turn two tiles at a time to find the pairs. |
| **Tug of war** | Two teams answer at the same time; each right answer pulls the rope. |
| **Quiz** | Quick questions for the class: pick an answer, reveal, next. |

You write the cards and questions yourself on the game.

## Lab pages and experiments

A lab is a full page of the board with a lab bench. In **Pages**, tap **Add a lab page** and choose
the **Chemistry lab**, **Physics lab** or **Life and Earth sciences lab**.

On the bench, add **Equipment** from the side panel (chemistry and physics labs) and use **Play**, **Pause** and **Reset** to run
the lab. **Check** points out mistakes in the setup, and in physics **Show forces** draws the forces.

To start from a ready-made experiment, pick one under **Experiments**, or tap **See all** to browse
the **Experiment templates** by lab and search them. Experiments are grouped by area:

| Area | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| **Mechanics** | Simple pendulum, projectile motion, Hooke's law, Archimedes' principle. |
| **Electricity** | Ohm's law, simple electric circuit, series and parallel circuits. |
| **Optics and waves** | Law of reflection, converging lens, dispersion of light by a prism. |
| **Chemistry** | Acid-base titration, acids, bases and pH, changes of state of water. |
| **Life and Earth sciences** | Starch test on a leaf, digestion of starch by saliva, erosion and sedimentation. |

<Callout title="Experiment settings are not kept">
The values you change inside an experiment are not saved with the board. **Reset** puts the starting
values back.
</Callout>

## Your library

Keep what you build so you can use it again on any board:

- Select an object or a group, then choose **Save to library**. On a touch screen, find it under
  **More actions**.
- On a lab page, choose **Save this lab**.

Open **Library** to find your saved **Games**, **Labs** and **Board items**, or **Search your
library**. Tap or drag an item to place it; tap a lab to add it as a new page. Each item's menu lets
you **Rename** or **Remove** it.
