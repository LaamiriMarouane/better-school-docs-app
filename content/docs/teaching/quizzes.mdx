A **quiz** is an online test built from questions. You create questions in a reusable **question
bank**, assemble them into a quiz, decide how it runs, and assign it to a class. Students take it on
screen, and their answers are **marked automatically**.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Teachers building and assigning quizzes, and the students who take them.
</Callout>

## Where to find it

Everything is under **Assessments**: **Quizzes** for your quizzes and **Question Bank** for your
questions (teachers see it as **Quiz Questions**).

## The building blocks

| Piece | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Question Bank** | Your reusable questions. Each can be **tagged** (e.g. *algebra*, *chapter 3*) and is a **Draft** until you publish it. |
| **Quiz** | A set of questions plus settings. A quiz is a **Draft** until you publish it, after which you can assign it. |
| **Quiz assignment** | A published quiz given to a class, with a release and due date. |
| **Attempt** | A student's go at the quiz. |

Publishing a question or a quiz makes it visible to the school's administrators and teachers, so
colleagues can reuse it.

## Question types

Choose **Add Question**, then pick a type:

- **True/False**
- **Multiple Choice**
- **Ordering** — put items in the right sequence
- **Type Your Answers** — fill in the blanks by typing
- **Select Your Answers** — fill in the blanks from dropdowns
- **Drag & Drop** — drag words into the blanks
- **Labeling Tags** — place labels on an image
- **Categorize** — sort items into groups
- **Image Hotspot** — tap the correct area on an image
- **Text Highlight** — highlight the correct words in a passage

Each question can include a **Hint**, an **Explanation** (shown in feedback), and images.

## Build a quiz

On **Quizzes**, choose **Add Quiz**. The editor walks you through five steps.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Basic Information

Give the quiz a **Quiz Title**, an optional **Description**, **Tags** and a **Thumbnail**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Add Questions

Pick questions from the bank. Only published questions can go into a quiz.
</Step>

<Step>
### Manage Questions

Drag questions into order, and set each one's **Points** and whether it is mandatory (**Required**)
or optional. When the quiz uses a time limit per question, you set each question's time here too.
</Step>

<Step>
### Configuration

Choose how the quiz runs (see below).
</Step>

<Step>
### Review

Check everything and choose **Create Quiz**. The quiz starts as a **Draft**: use **Publish** on
its card so it can be assigned.
</Step>

</Steps>

### How a quiz runs

| Setting | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| **Max Attempts** | How many times a student may take it. Leave empty for **No Limit**. |
| **Passing Score (%)** | The percentage needed to pass. |
| **Duration** | **No Limit**, **Per Question** (a time for each question), or a total time from 15 minutes to 3 hours. |
| **Scoring Strategy** | **Standard** (full points for a fully correct answer, partial credit for a partly correct one; in a multiple-select question each wrong choice cancels a correct one) or **Negative Marking** (each wrong part takes off a quarter of its share). A question never scores below zero. |
| **Feedback Mode** | Show corrections **Never**, **After Quiz** or **After Question**. |
| **Navigation Mode** | **Free** (move back and forth), **Sequential** (forward only) or **Lock on Answer** (an answer can't be changed once given). With per-question times, use Sequential or Lock on Answer. |
| **Shuffle Questions** / **Shuffle Options** | Randomise the order of questions and of answer choices. |
| **Require Answer to Progress** | Students must answer before moving on. |
| **Show Scores** | Whether students see their score when they finish. |
| **Choose Questions Randomly From Pool** | Give each attempt a random set of **Questions Per Attempt**. Required questions are always included. |

## Assign the quiz to a class

Open **Assessments → Assignments**, choose **Add Assignment**, then **Quiz**: you land on
**Quizzes**, where you pick a published quiz and choose **Assign**. You can also choose **Assign**
straight from a quiz.

Then fill in the **Term**, **Class** (and **Class group** if the class is split), **Subject**,
**Marking period**, **Release Date** and **Due Date**. You can adjust the quiz settings just for
this assignment.

## How students take it

The student opens the quiz under **Assignments** and chooses **Start Quiz**. They answer within
any time limit and submit. Answers are marked straight away, so the score is ready immediately
(shown if you turned on **Show Scores**). Depending on the feedback mode, they see corrections
after each question or at the end. If attempts remain, they can take it again.

## See how the class did

Open the quiz assignment to see the **Participants** (each student's attempts and **Best Score**)
and the **Analytics** tab: pass rate, **Score Distribution** and **Question Performance** — which
questions were answered well and which tripped students up.

## Counts toward the gradebook

A quiz has no weight of its own. As with [assignments](./assignments), turn on **Counts in the
final average** when you assign it and choose a **Category**: the student's best attempt then
counts in the [gradebook](./gradebook), within that category's weight. You can type a different
mark in the gradebook; clear it to go back to the best attempt.

## Common use cases

- **Quick check** — a short multiple-choice quiz with feedback after each question.
- **Timed test** — set a total duration and one attempt.
- **Practice** — leave attempts unlimited, show feedback, and don't count it in the final average.
