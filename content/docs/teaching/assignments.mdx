title: Assignments

An **assignment** is a piece of work you set for a class — homework, a project, an essay. Students
hand in their work online or in class, you grade it with feedback, and the grade can count in the
[gradebook](./gradebook). An assignment is either a **File** assignment (described here) or a
**Quiz** (see [Quizzes](./quizzes)).

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Teachers setting and grading work, and the students who submit it.
</Callout>

## Create an assignment

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Assignments

Go to **Assessments → Assignments**, choose **Add Assignment**, then pick **File**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Fill in the details

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Title** | Yes | The assignment's name. |
| **Description** | No | Instructions for students. |
| **Term** | Yes | The term the work belongs to. |
| **Class** | Yes | Who it's for. Administrators pick the **Level** first. |
| **Class group** | Depends | Shown when the class is split into groups. Pick a group, or **Whole class** when it's offered. |
| **Subject** | Yes | The subject the grade goes to. |
| **Marking period** | Yes | The part of the term the grade counts in. |
| **Release Date** | Yes | When students can first see it. It starts at now; set a later date to release it later. |
| **Due Date** | Yes | The deadline. It must be after the release date. |
| **Attachments** | No | Files you provide — a worksheet, a brief, a rubric. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Set how it's handed in and graded

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Submission Mode** | **Online** (students upload files) or **In Class** (handed in on paper — you just record the grade). |
| **Max Files** | How many files a student can attach (online only). |
| **Accepted submission file types** | Restrict uploads, for example to PDF or Word files (online only). |
| **Max Grade** | What the work is marked out of (e.g. 20). |
| **Counts in the final average** | Turn on so the grade counts in the subject average, then choose its **Category**. Off by default — leave it off for practice work. |
| **Allow late submission** | Let students submit after the due date, optionally until a **Final submission deadline**. |
| **Allow resubmission** | Let students update their submission until it is graded. |
| **Track views** | See which students have opened the assignment. |
| **Allow comments** | Let students leave comments on the assignment. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Save

Choose **Create Assignment**. Students see it from the release date.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Once students have handed in">
After the first submission, the class, subject and marking period can no longer be changed. The
other details stay editable.
</Callout>

## How students submit

Students find their work under **Assignments**. Each card shows where it stands:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Upcoming** | Not released yet. |
| **Open** | Released and before the due date. |
| **Overdue** | The due date has passed and nothing was handed in. |
| **Submitted** | The student has handed in their work. |
| **Graded** | You've graded the work. |

For an **online** assignment, the student opens it, uploads their files with an optional note, and
chooses **Submit Assignment**. If you allowed resubmission, they can update their work until you
grade it. If you allowed late submission, they can still hand in after the due date, up to the final
deadline if you set one. An **In Class** assignment simply tells students it is handed in during
class.

Once graded, the student sees their grade and your feedback.

## Grade and give feedback

Open the assignment: its tabs are **Overview**, **Submissions** and, when views are tracked,
**View Tracking**. Go to the **Submissions** tab. Pick a student's submission, enter a
**Grade** (up to the max grade) and optional **Feedback**, then choose **Submit Grade**.

For **In Class** work there's nothing to open: enter the marks in the assignment's column in the
[gradebook](./gradebook).

## Publish grades

For work that **counts in the final average**, grades stay hidden until you choose
**Publish grades** on the assignment. Until then, students see *Graded — awaiting publication*.
Use **Unpublish grades** to hide them again.

Practice work (not counted) has no publish step: students see each grade as soon as you give it.

<Callout title="Finish marking first">
Because counting grades stay private until you publish, you can mark the whole class before anyone
sees a result.
</Callout>

## Counts toward the gradebook

An assignment has no weight of its own. When **Counts in the final average** is on, its grade joins
the **Category** you chose, and the category's weight (set in [Grading setup](./grading-setup))
decides how much it counts. For example, if *Homework* is worth 20% of the subject, all counting
homework together makes up that 20%.

## Common use cases

- **Homework with a file** — set an online assignment; students upload a PDF.
- **Paper hand-in** — set an *In Class* assignment and enter the grades in the gradebook.
- **Second chance** — allow resubmission so students can improve their work until you grade it.
- **Practice** — leave **Counts in the final average** off; students see grades straight away.
