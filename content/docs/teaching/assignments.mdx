An **assignment** is a piece of work you set for a class — homework, a project, an essay. Students
can submit their work online, you grade it with feedback, and the grade flows into the
[gradebook](./gradebook).

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Teachers setting and grading work, and the students who submit it.
</Callout>

## Create an assignment

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Assignments

Go to **Assignments** and choose **Add assignment**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Fill in the details

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Title** | Yes | The assignment's name. |
| **Description** | No | Instructions for students. |
| **Class** *(and group)* | Yes | Who it's for. Pick a group to set it for part of a divided class. |
| **Release date** | No | When students can first see it. Leave empty to release now. |
| **Due date** | Yes | The deadline. |
| **Max score** | Yes | What the work is marked out of (e.g. 20). |
| **Attachments** | No | Files you provide — a worksheet, a brief, a rubric. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Set the submission rules

Decide how students hand in their work:

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Submission type** | **Online** (students upload files) or **In class** (handed in on paper — you just record the grade). |
| **Allow late submissions** | Let students submit after the due date, optionally up to a later cut-off. |
| **Files per submission** | How many files a student can attach. |
| **Accepted file types** | Restrict uploads (for example *pdf, docx*). |
| **Allow resubmission** | Let students replace their submission before the deadline. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Save

The assignment appears for the class, and students can submit from the due window.
</Step>

</Steps>

## How students submit

For an **online** assignment, a student opens it and **uploads** their files, with an optional note.
If you allowed resubmission, they can replace their work until the deadline (or the late cut-off).

A student's assignment shows as **Pending** until they submit, then **Done** — or **Not done** if
the deadline passes with nothing submitted.

## Grade and give feedback

Open the assignment's grading view to see every student's submission in one place. For each student,
enter a **score** and optional **feedback**. When you're ready, **publish the grades** to make them
visible to students and families.

<Callout title="Grades stay private until you publish">
Scores and feedback you enter aren't visible to students until you **publish** them — so you can
finish marking first.
</Callout>

## Counts toward the gradebook

An assignment can be placed in a **grade category** (e.g. *Homework*) and given a **weight**, so its
grades feed into each student's subject grade. See [Gradebook](./gradebook).

## Common use cases

- **Homework with a file** — set an online assignment; students upload a PDF.
- **Paper hand-in** — set an *in class* assignment and just record the grades.
- **Second chance** — allow resubmission so students can fix and resubmit before the deadline.
