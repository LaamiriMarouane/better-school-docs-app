A **board** is an interactive whiteboard: an endless canvas where you write, draw, add shapes,
images and videos, and place maths tools, science labs and games. You can split a board into
**pages**, present it in class, and attach it to a lesson so your students find it there.

The whiteboard works with a finger on phones, tablets and classroom touch screens, as well as with
a mouse.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Teachers preparing and teaching with boards. Students and parents open the boards attached to
lessons.
</Callout>

## Your boards

Go to **Academic** → **Boards**. Your boards are listed as cards showing a preview, the title and
when each was last modified.

- **Search boards** — find a board by its title.
- **Favorites** — show only the boards you starred. Tap the star on a card to **Add to favorites**
  or **Remove from favorites**.
- Tap a card to open the board.

Your boards are private: other teachers don't see them. To share a board with a class, attach it to
a lesson, as described below.

### Create a board

Tap the **New board** tile, the first card in the list. A board called **Untitled board** opens
straight away, ready to use.

### Rename, duplicate or delete a board

Open the **Board actions** menu (⋮) on a card:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit** | Change the **Title** and the **Thumbnail** — the cover image shown on the card. Without a thumbnail, the card shows page 1 of the board. |
| **Duplicate** | Make a copy of the board, with "(copy)" added to its title. |
| **Delete** | Remove the board from your library. |

You can also rename a board from inside it: tap its title in the top corner.

<Callout title="Deleting a board attached to a lesson">
Unattach the board from all lessons before you delete it. Copies your students already made of it
are kept.
</Callout>

## Work on a board

The toolbar runs along the top of the board (at the bottom on phones and large touch screens).
Choose a tool, then tap or drag on the board.

| Tool | Use it to… |
| --- | --- |
| **Select** | Pick objects to move, resize or change them. |
| **Hand (move board)** | Move around the board. You can also pinch to zoom on a touch screen. |
| **Sticky note** | Add a coloured note. |
| **Shape** | Draw a rectangle, oval, triangle, star, arrow, line and more. |
| **Frame** | Drag to draw a frame around part of the board — frames are the slides you present. |
| **Connector** | Link two objects with a straight or elbow line, with or without arrows. |
| **Text** | Type text anywhere on the board. |
| **Table** | Add a table. |
| **Pen** | Write and draw freehand. In its options, switch to **Highlighter**, pick the width and colour, or turn a rough stroke into a clean drawing. |
| **Magic pen** | Draw a stroke that fades away on its own — handy for pointing things out. |
| **Clean eraser** | Erase what you drew. |

After the tools come the panels of ready-made content — **Manipulatives**, **Science**, **Games**
and **Library**. See [Labs, manipulatives and games](./whiteboard-labs-and-templates).

The **More** menu adds **Images or PDF from device**, images from **Unsplash**, a **YouTube video**,
and **Export** (PNG, SVG or PDF, for **This page**, **All pages** or the **Frames**). When the
screen is narrow, some tools move into **More** too.

Use **Undo** and **Redo** in the corner of the board to step back and forth through your changes.

### On a touch screen

- With **Select**, tap an object to select it. Turn on **Select several** to add more objects with
  each tap.
- A bar of actions appears next to the selection: **Delete**, **Duplicate**, **Copy**, **Paste**,
  **Group**, **Bring to front**, **Send to back**, **Lock**, and **More actions** for everything
  else.

### Pages

A board can have several pages, like the sheets of a notebook. Tap **Pages** to open the page strip:

- **Add page** adds a blank page; **Add a lab page** adds a science lab.
- Tap a page to open it. Press and hold a page, then drag it, to change the order.
- **Page options** (⋯) lets you **Rename**, **Duplicate** or **Delete page**.

### Saving

There's no save button: the board **saves automatically** as you work. The badge in the top corner
shows **Saving…**, then **Saved**.

If the same board was changed on another device or tab, saving pauses and you choose **Load latest
version** or **Keep my version**.

### Preferences

Open **Preferences** to switch between a **Light board** and a **Dark board**, change the **Board
color**, move the toolbar to the **Top** or **Bottom**, show or hide **Tool names**, and turn
**Smart guides** on or off. It also has **Clear this page** (Undo brings the objects back) and
**Reset board**, which empties the whole board after you confirm.

## Present in class

Presenting uses **frames**: each frame is one slide. Draw frames around the parts of the board you
want to show, then tap **Present**:

| Mode | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Slideshow** | Full screen, one frame at a time, without the editing tools. |
| **Board tour** | Glides from frame to frame on the live board. |
| **Frames** | Change the order of the frames, or leave some out of the presentation. |

Tap **Next** and **Previous** to move between frames, and **Exit presentation** to finish. The
spotlight button blurs the objects in a frame so you can reveal them one by one with a tap.

To make an object appear only when you're ready, select it, then under **Reveal in presentation**
choose **Hidden until revealed** or **Covered until revealed**.

<Callout title="No frames yet?">
If the page has no frames, **Present** shows **Add a frame to present it as a slide**. Tap it to pick
the **Frame** tool.
</Callout>

## Attach a board to a lesson

Students and parents see a board only once it's attached to a lesson. Open the lesson in the lesson
editor (see [Lessons](./lessons)), then in the **Assets** panel, under **Boards**:

<Steps>

<Step>
### Choose Attach board

Tap **Attach board**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Pick a board

Search your boards and tap one to attach it, or choose **Create new board** to start a blank one.
</Step>

</Steps>

Each attached board has **Open**, to edit it, and **Unattach**, to remove it from the lesson.
Students see the board when the lesson content is visible to their class.

## What students and parents see

In the lesson, students and parents find the attached boards under **Boards**. A board opens in view
mode: they can move around it and turn its pages.

- **Students** can tap **Enable editing** to work on their **own copy** of the board. Their changes
  save automatically and never change your board. **Reset to teacher board** replaces their copy
  with your current board, after they confirm.
- **Parents** see the board **View only**. If their child has made their own copy, they see the
  child's copy.

**Back to lesson** returns to the lesson.

## Common use cases

- **Prepare a lesson in advance** — build the board with one frame per step, then present it in class
  with **Slideshow**.
- **Homework on the board** — attach a board with exercises to a lesson; each student completes it on
  their own copy.
- **Reuse a board with another class** — **Duplicate** it and adapt the copy.
