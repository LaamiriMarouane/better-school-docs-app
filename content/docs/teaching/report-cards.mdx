A **report card** is a snapshot of a student's grades at a point in time. It captures the period
grades, term grades, subject year grade, overall average, class rank, mention, and pass/fail
status — and, when the level has an external exam, a separate exam average line.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators and teachers who generate and publish student grade reports.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Grades are live in the gradebook, frozen on the card">
The [gradebook](./gradebook) always shows live, recalculated grades. A report card takes a
snapshot of those grades at the moment it is generated — they do not change afterwards, even if
assessment marks are edited later. See [Staleness and reissue](#staleness-and-reissue).
</Callout>

## What a report card shows

| Section | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Subject rows** | Each subject: period grade(s), term grade, year grade, and subject coefficient |
| **Overall average** | Weighted average across all subjects using school coefficients (*moyenne générale*) |
| **Class rank** | Student's position in the class by overall average (when ranking is enabled) |
| **Mention** | Letter grade or label derived from the grading scale (e.g. Bien, Très bien) |
| **Pass / fail** | Whether the student met the minimum pass threshold for each subject |
| **Exam average** | Shown only when the level has an external exam — computed from the exam's own coefficients over its covered subjects, independent of the overall average |

## Generate a report card

### Single student

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the student's profile or the class gradebook

Navigate to the student directly, or open the class gradebook and select the student's row.
</Step>

<Step>
### Choose the period

Select whether to generate the card for a **term** (mid-year progress) or the **full year**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Generate

Click **Generate report card**. The system computes and snapshots all grade values. The card is
ready to download as a PDF.
</Step>

</Steps>

### Whole class — bulk generation

Go to the class gradebook and use **Generate all report cards**. The system creates one card per
student and packages them into a **ZIP file** you can download in one click. Generation runs in
the background — you will be notified when the file is ready.

<Callout title="Tip — generate after grading is complete">
Generate report cards once all assessment marks have been entered and any overrides or remarks
are in place. Cards are a snapshot; anything entered after generation will not appear unless you
reissue.
</Callout>

## Publish to families

Generating a card makes it available to **you** (admin and teacher view). Students and parents
cannot see it until you **publish** it.

| State | Who can see it |
| --- | --- |
| **Generated** | Administrators and teachers only |
| **Published** | Students and parents can view and download |

You can publish a single card or publish the whole class at once. Published cards can be
unpublished if you need to correct and reissue them first.

## Staleness and reissue

If assessment marks change **after** a card was generated, the card is flagged as **stale** —
the live gradebook no longer matches the snapshot. A stale badge appears on the card so you know
it is out of date.

To update it, **reissue** the card: the system generates a fresh snapshot from the current
gradebook values and replaces the old one. You can reissue a single card or the whole class.

<Callout title="Published cards">
Reissuing a published card automatically unpublishes it. Republish it once you are happy with
the new version so families see the corrected grades.
</Callout>

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between a term card and a year card?

A **term card** snapshots grades for a single term: the period grades within that term, the term
average, and the student's rank at that point. A **year card** covers the full year: all terms
and any external exam, the subject year grade, overall average, final rank, and mention.

### Can I generate report cards before all marks are entered?

Yes, but any assessment not yet marked will show as Pending on the card, and averages will
reflect only what has been entered. It is best to generate after all marking is complete. You can
reissue the card once the remaining marks are in.

### What happens if I edit a grade after generating a card?

The card is flagged as **stale**. The live gradebook reflects the new mark immediately; the card
stays on the old snapshot until you reissue it.

### Are report cards visible to students and parents automatically?

No. Generating a card makes it visible to administrators and teachers only. You must explicitly
**publish** it for students and parents to see it.

### Does the report card include the external exam average?

Yes, when the level has an external exam configured in its grading scheme. The exam average
appears as a separate line, computed from the exam's own coefficients over its covered subjects.
It is independent of the overall average (*moyenne générale*).

### Can I download report cards for the whole class at once?

Yes. Use **Generate all report cards** from the class gradebook. The cards are packaged into a
ZIP file that you can download once generation is complete.
