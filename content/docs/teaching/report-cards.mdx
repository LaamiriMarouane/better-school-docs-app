A **report card** records a student's grades at the moment it is generated: term and period grades,
subject averages, the overall average, class rank, mention, the teacher's remark for
each subject and, when the level has an external exam, a separate exam average line.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators who generate and publish report cards. Students and parents download the cards once
they are published.
</Callout>

<Callout title="The gradebook changes, the card doesn't">
The [gradebook](./gradebook) always shows up-to-date grades. A report card keeps the grades as they
were when it was generated, even if marks are edited later. See
[Keep cards up to date](#keep-cards-up-to-date).
</Callout>

## What a report card shows

| Section | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Subject rows** | Each subject: term and period grades, subject average, class average, coefficient, and the teacher's remark |
| **Overall average** | Average across all subjects, weighted by the school's coefficients |
| **Class rank** | The student's position in the class (when class ranking is turned on) |
| **Mention** | The label from the grading scale (e.g. Bien, Très bien) |
| **Failed** | Shown on a subject when the student didn't reach a marking period's pass gate. See [Marking periods](./marking-periods). |
| **Attendance and behaviour** | The student's attendance rate and behaviour summary |
| **Exam average** | Only when the level has an external exam: worked out from the exam's own coefficients, separate from the overall average |

## Choose the scope

Every report card covers one scope:

- **Full year**: all terms and any external exam.
- **A term**: that term's grades.
- **A marking period**: offered when the term is split into several marking periods.

## Generate for one student

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the student's grades

Go to **Academic → Students**, open the student's profile, then the **Grades** tab. Click
**Report cards**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Generate

Click **Generate**, choose the **Scope**, then click **Generate**. If grades are still being entered
or the student has no grades yet, a warning appears and the button becomes **Generate anyway**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Check and publish

The new card appears in the list as **Draft**. Use **Download PDF** to check it, then **Publish**.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Generate for a whole class or level

1. Go to **Assessments → Gradebook** and click **Report cards** above the grid.
2. Choose **Class** or **Level**, pick the class or level, then the **Scope**.
3. Check the summary: how many students already have a card, how many need an update, and any
   warning (grades still open, unpublished assessments, students with no grades).
4. Click **Generate for class** or **Generate for level**. Progress is shown while cards are created,
   and you can **Cancel**.

From the same panel you can:

- **Publish all** the draft cards in one step,
- **Reissue stale** cards whose grades changed (see below),
- **Download all (ZIP)** to get every card as a PDF.

## Publish to families

A new card is a **Draft**: only staff can see it. **Publish** makes it visible to the student and
their parents, who can download it from their **Grades** screen.

A term or marking-period card can only be published once that period's grading is **Locked** or
**Finalized** (see [Marking periods](./marking-periods)). A full-year card can be published at any
time.

Publishing can't be undone. To correct a published card, re-issue it.

## Report card statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Generated, visible to staff only. |
| **Issued** | Official but not yet visible to families. Publish it like a draft. |
| **Published** | Visible to the student and parents. |
| **Needs update** | Published, but grades have changed since. Families still see this card until you re-issue it. |
| **Superseded** | Replaced by a newer version. Kept in the history, with its version number. |

## Keep cards up to date

When marks change after a card was published, the card is marked **Needs update** and a banner on the
student's report cards lists the cards that are out of date. Click **Recheck** to check a student's
cards again.

To fix a card, click **Re-issue** on it, or **Re-issue all** for every out-of-date card of the
student. Re-issuing builds a new version from the current grades and publishes it straight away;
the previous version becomes **Superseded**. For a whole class or level, use **Reissue stale** in
the gradebook's **Report cards** panel.

Generating a card again for the same scope also creates a new version, as a **Draft** that you need
to publish.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I generate report cards before all marks are entered?

Yes. You'll see a warning, and the averages only reflect what has been entered. Generate again or
re-issue once the remaining marks are in.

### Can I unpublish a report card?

No. Re-issue it instead: families then see the corrected version.

### Are report cards visible to students and parents automatically?

No. A card stays a **Draft**, visible to staff only, until you **Publish** it.

### Does the report card include the external exam average?

Yes, when the level has an external exam in its grading scheme. It appears as a separate line and
doesn't change the overall average.

### Can I download report cards for the whole class at once?

Yes. In **Assessments → Gradebook**, open **Report cards** and click **Download all (ZIP)**.
