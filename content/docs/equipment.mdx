The **Equipment** register is a simple catalog of your school's equipment items. You record each item
with a type, a title, a description, and an optional purchase date, then search, edit, and remove items
as things change.

<Callout title="Module required">
Equipment is an optional module. If you don't see it, ask whoever manages your school's modules to
turn on **Equipment**.
</Callout>

<Callout title="A lightweight register">
This is a catalog of items — not an asset-tracking system. There's no check-out and return, no
maintenance log, condition rating, or cost tracking. Each entry is one item with a type, title,
description, and purchase date.
</Callout>

## What an item has

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Equipment Type** | The kind of item — **Board** or **Data Show** (projector). Required. |
| **Title** | The item's name. Required. |
| **Description** | Details about the item. Required. |
| **Purchase Date** | Optional, for your records. |

## Add an item

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Equipment

In the sidebar, go to **OPERATIONS** → **Equipment** and choose **Add Equipment**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Fill in the details

Pick the **Equipment Type**, give it a **Title** and a **Description**, and add a **Purchase Date** if
you like.
</Step>

<Step>
### Submit

Choose **Submit**. The item joins the register.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Find and manage items

- **Search** by name and **filter** by **Equipment Type** to find an item.
- **Edit** an item from its row menu to update its details.
- **Delete** an item from its row menu. Items are deleted one at a time.
