title: Equipment
description: "Keep a simple register of your school's equipment — boards, projectors, and more."
---

The **Equipment** register is a simple catalog of your school's equipment items. You record each item
with a type, a name, and an optional purchase date, then search, edit, and remove items as things
change.

<Callout title="Module required">
Equipment is an optional module. If you don't see it, ask whoever manages your school's modules to
turn on **Equipment**.
</Callout>

<Callout title="A lightweight register">
This is a catalog of items — not an asset-tracking system. There's no check-out and return, no
maintenance log, condition rating, or cost tracking. Each entry is one item with a type, title,
description, and purchase date.
</Callout>

## What an item has

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Type** | The kind of item — **board**, **data show** (projector), or **other**. |
| **Title** | The item's name. Required. |
| **Description** | Optional details. |
| **Purchase date** | Optional, for your records. |

## Add an item

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Equipment

Go to the **Equipment** page and choose **Add**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Fill in the details

Pick the **type**, give it a **title**, and add a description or purchase date if you like.
</Step>

<Step>
### Save

The item joins the register.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Find and manage items

- **Search** by name and **filter** by type to find an item.
- **Edit** an item to update its details.
- **Delete** items one at a time, or select several and remove them together.

<Callout title="Kept per school">
The register belongs to your **school**, not to a single academic year — it carries across years, so
you keep it up to date as equipment comes and goes.
</Callout>
