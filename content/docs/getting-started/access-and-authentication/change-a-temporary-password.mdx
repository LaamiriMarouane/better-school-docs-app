---
title: Change a temporary password
description: Set your own password the first time you sign in with temporary credentials.
---

When the school creates an account for you, it comes with a **temporary
password**. The first time you sign in, you are required to replace it with one
only you know.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
New users signing in for the first time with credentials issued by the school.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- The **temporary password** given to you by the school — see
  [Create portal accounts](../onboard-people/create-portal-accounts).

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Sign in with the temporary password

Sign in as usual with your email or username and the temporary password. Because
the password is temporary, you are sent straight to the change-password screen —
you cannot skip it.
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter the passwords

Fill in three fields:

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Old password** | The temporary password you just used. |
| **New password** | A new password only you know. It must meet the strength requirement. |
| **Confirm password** | The new password again — both must match. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Save

Choose **Save**. For security, you are signed out so the change takes effect.
</Step>

<Step>
### Sign in again

Return to the sign-in page and sign in with your **new** password. From now on,
that is the password you use.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="This step cannot be skipped">
Until the temporary password is replaced, every sign-in returns to this screen.
You can sign out instead of changing it, but you will not reach your dashboard.
</Callout>
