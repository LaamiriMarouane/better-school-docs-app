---
title: Log in to your account
description: Sign in with your email or username and password.
---

Signing in is the same on the web app and the mobile app — one account works on
both.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Anyone with an account — administrators, teachers, guardians, and students.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- Your **email or username** and **password**.
- If your account was created for you, you may have a **temporary password** —
  see [Change a temporary password](./change-a-temporary-password).

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the sign-in page

Go to your school's address and open the **Sign in** page.
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter your credentials

Type your **email or username** and your **password**. Tick **Remember me** to
keep your email filled in next time on the same device.
</Step>

<Step>
### Complete the security check

If a security check is shown, complete it. It confirms a real person is signing
in and protects the account.
</Step>

<Step>
### Sign in

Choose **Sign in**. On success you land on your dashboard, opened for your role.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="First sign-in with a temporary password">
If your account uses a temporary password, you are sent straight to a screen that
asks you to set your own — see [Change a temporary password](./change-a-temporary-password).
</Callout>

## If you cannot sign in

- Check the email or username and password for typos.
- Forgot your password? Use the **Forgot password** link — see
  [Reset your password](./reset-your-password).
- If you hold more than one role, you choose which workspace to use after signing
  in — see [Switch between available roles](./switch-between-available-roles).
