---
title: Switch between available roles
description: Move between the roles your account holds without signing out.
---

Some people have more than one role — a teacher or a staff member who is also the
guardian of a student at the school. One account holds them all, and you switch
between them whenever you need to.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Users whose account has more than one role. A person can combine only **Teacher** +
**Guardian**, or **Staff** + **Guardian**.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- An account with **two or more roles**. With a single role there is nothing to
  switch — no roles are listed in the profile menu.
- Switching roles is done in the web app. The mobile app has no role switch.

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the profile menu

At the bottom of the sidebar, tap your name to open the profile menu.
</Step>

<Step>
### Choose a role

Your roles are listed at the top of the menu, each with its icon. The role you are
currently using is highlighted and marked with a check. Tap the role you want.
</Step>

<Step>
### Confirm the workspace changed

You are taken to the home page of that role — the sidebar, pages and available
actions all change to match it.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Each role is a separate workspace">
Switching does not sign you out. It changes what you see: an admin, teacher,
guardian, student or staff workspace — each with its own menu. The next time you
sign in on the same device, the platform opens the role you last used.
</Callout>

## If a role is missing

If a role you expect is not listed, your account does not have it yet:

- The **Guardian** role is added when the school adds you as a guardian of a student
  (with your email), or picks you as a guardian on the student's profile.
- The **Teacher** role comes from your teacher profile.
- Staff roles are given by an administrator, from **Access** → **Users** → **Assign
  roles**.
- An administrator can't also be a teacher or hold a staff role, and a teacher can't
  hold a staff role.
- A student account keeps the **Student** role only and cannot hold other roles.
