---
title: Switch between available roles
description: Move between the roles your account holds without signing out.
---

Some people have more than one role — for example a teacher who is also a
guardian. One account holds them all, and you switch between them whenever you
need to.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Users whose account has more than one role assigned.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- An account with **two or more roles**. With a single role there is nothing to
  switch — the option does not appear.

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the profile menu

In the sidebar, open your **profile menu** at the bottom.
</Step>

<Step>
### Choose a role under Switch Role

The menu lists every role on your account under **Switch Role**. The role you are
currently using is marked with a check.
</Step>

<Step>
### Confirm the workspace changed

Selecting a role reloads the workspace for that role — the sidebar, pages, and
available actions all change to match it.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Each role is a separate workspace">
Switching does not sign you out. It changes what you see: an admin workspace, a
teacher workspace, or a guardian workspace — each with its own menu.
</Callout>

## If a role is missing

If a role you expect is not listed, your account does not have it yet. Roles are
assigned by an administrator — ask them to add it.
