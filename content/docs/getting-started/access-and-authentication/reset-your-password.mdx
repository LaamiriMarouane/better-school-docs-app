---
title: Reset your password
description: Regain access when you have forgotten your password.
---

If you cannot sign in, you can reset your password yourself by email — no need to
contact the school.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Any user who has forgotten their password or lost access.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- Access to the **email address** on your account — the reset link is sent there.

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Forgot password

On the sign-in page, choose **Forgot password**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter your email

Type the email address on your account, complete the security check if shown, and
choose **Send reset link**. A link is emailed to that address.
</Step>

<Step>
### Open the reset link

Open the email and follow the link. It carries a token that the platform checks:

| Result | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Valid** | The reset form opens — continue below. |
| **Invalid or expired** | Request a new link from **Forgot password**. |
| **Missing** | The link was incomplete — request a new one. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Set a new password

Enter a **new password** and confirm it. It must meet the strength requirement —
the strength meter shows how it scores. Both entries must match.
</Step>

<Step>
### Sign in

Submit the form. You are returned to the sign-in page — sign in with the new
password.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Reset links expire">
A reset link is valid for a limited time. If it has expired, simply request a new
one — the most recent link is the one that works.
</Callout>
