title: Announcements, notifications, and messaging
description: The ways information moves through the school — announcements, events, messages, audiences, and notifications — and how they differ.
---

The app has several ways to reach people. Some are one-way, some are conversations, and some just
alert you that something happened. This page explains each one and when to use it.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Staff planning internal and school-to-family communication.
</Callout>

## The words

| Concept | Direction | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Announcement** | One-way | News or a notice published to chosen audiences. People read it but don't reply. |
| **Event** | Calendar | A dated entry on the school calendar, such as an activity, a meeting, an exam day or a holiday. |
| **Message** | Two-way | A conversation: a class channel or a direct message between two people. |
| **Audience** | Building block | A reusable group of recipients (by role, by level, or a list of people) picked when publishing an announcement or an event. |
| **Notification** | Per person | An alert about something that concerns you, in the app and on your phone. |
| **Reclamation** | Family to school | A request or complaint sent by a student or parent from **Communication → Reclamations**, and followed up by administrators. |

## How they fit together

- **Audiences come first.** Administrators build them once, then pick them each time they publish
  an announcement or an event.
- **Notifications follow.** When an announcement or an event is published, the people in its
  audiences are notified. Graded work, payments and other updates notify the people concerned too.
- **Messages are separate.** They don't use audiences. Class channels are created automatically
  for each class and its teacher, and direct messages follow clear rules about who can message whom.

Administrators create announcements, events and audiences. Teachers, students and parents read the
announcements and events meant for them, and use **Messages** to stay in touch.

## Statuses you will see

| On | Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Announcement | **Draft** | Still being written. Only administrators see it. |
| Announcement | **Published** | Visible to its audiences, who are notified. **Unpublish** turns it back into a draft. |
| Event kind | **Normal**, **Exam day**, **Early dismissal** | Calendar labels. They don't change school operations. |
| Event kind | **Holiday**, **Closure** | The school is closed: no timetable sessions and no bus trips that day. |
| Notification | Unread / read | Unread notifications are highlighted. **Mark all read** clears them. |

## Which one to use

| You want to… | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Tell all parents about a school trip | An **announcement** to a parents audience |
| Put a holiday on the calendar | An **event** of kind **Holiday** |
| Answer a parent's question | A **direct message** |
| Talk with your whole class | The **class channel** |
