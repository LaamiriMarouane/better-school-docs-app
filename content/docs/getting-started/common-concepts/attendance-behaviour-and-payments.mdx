---

Once teaching starts, three kinds of records build up every day: who was there, how students
behaved, and what families owe and pay. This page explains the words used for each.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators and staff learning the vocabulary of daily school operations.
</Callout>

## Attendance

| Concept | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Student Attendance Mode** | Set on each level. **Per Period**: the teacher takes attendance in each session, from the timetable. **Per Day**: administrators take it once a day on the **Student Attendance** page. |
| **Class session** | One real meeting of a class, created from the timetable. Teachers open it to **Mark Attendance**. |
| **Exam attendance** | Who sat a scheduled exam, recorded on the exam itself. |
| **Staff attendance** | One status per staff member per day, under **Attendance → Teacher Attendance** and **Attendance → Staff Attendance**. |

### Attendance statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Present** | Attended. Every student starts as present when you mark a session. |
| **Absent** | Did not attend. |
| **Late** | Arrived late. |
| **Half Day** | Attended part of the day. |
| **Excused** | Exam attendance only: an approved absence, with a reason. |
| **On Leave** | Staff attendance only: off on leave that day. |

See [Class sessions & attendance](../../teaching/sessions-and-attendance) and
[Staff attendance](../../staff-hr/staff-attendance).

## Behaviour

| Concept | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Category** | A kind of behaviour, for example *Respect* or *Disruption*. A category can have subcategories. |
| **Level** | How significant the behaviour is. Each level is **positive** or **negative** and has default points. |
| **Incident** | One behaviour event recorded for a student, with its category, level, date and points. |
| **Points** | Positive incidents add points, negative ones take points away. |
| **Leaderboard** | Students ranked by their points over a chosen period. |

Administrators record incidents for any student. Teachers record them for the classes they teach.
Students and parents see only their own. The Behaviour screens appear only when your school has
turned on the Behaviour module. See [Record incidents](../../behaviour/record-incidents) and
[Points & leaderboard](../../behaviour/leaderboard).

## Payments

| Concept | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Service** | Something the school provides, such as tuition, transport or canteen. |
| **Fee** | The price of a service, with how often it is charged (monthly, termly, once…). |
| **Payment plan** | A bundle of fees for one or more levels. A student gets a plan when enrolled. |
| **Installment** | One amount a student owes, with a due date. Installments are created for you from the student's plan. |
| **Payment** | Money collected against one or more installments, recorded in the **Payment center**. |
| **Reduction rule** | A reusable discount given to students. |

### Installment statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Not Paid** | Nothing has been paid yet. |
| **Partially Paid** | Part of the amount has been paid. The rest is still owed. |
| **Paid** | Fully settled. |
| **Waived** | The school agreed the student doesn't owe it. |
| **Cancelled** | No longer billed. |

An unpaid installment whose due date has passed is overdue: its due date shows in red with the
number of days overdue. It can still be paid.

### Payment statuses

A recorded payment shows as **Successful**. After a refund it shows as **Partially refunded** or
**Refunded**.

See [Fees & Payments](../../finance/fees-and-payments),
[Installments and schedules](../../finance/fees-and-payments/installments-and-schedules) and
[Refund a payment](../../finance/fees-and-payments/refund-a-payment).

<Callout title="Work in the right year">
Fees, payment plans, payments and installments follow the academic year selected at the top of the
screen. In a closed year, you can still record late payments on unpaid installments.
</Callout>
