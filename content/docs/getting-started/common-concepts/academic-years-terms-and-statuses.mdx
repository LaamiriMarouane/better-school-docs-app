title: Academic years, terms, and statuses
description: What an academic year and a term are, the statuses a year goes through, and what each status lets you do.
---

Almost everything in the school is organised around time: a school year, split into terms, and
sometimes into smaller grading periods. This page explains these words and the statuses you see
on them.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Anyone learning how the school calendar is organised in the app, especially administrators.
</Callout>

## The words

| Concept | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Academic year** | One school cycle, for example *2025–2026*. Classes, enrollments, fees and timetables are prepared for a year. |
| **Term** | A part of the year, for example *Semester 1* or *Trimester 2*. Your term names are set once in **Term Structure**, then each year's terms are dated. |
| **Active term** | The term in progress. Grading and reporting start on it by default. You switch it with **Activate Term**. |
| **Marking period** | A grading unit inside a term, for example *Midterm* and *End of term*. A term has one marking period unless you split it. |
| **Year setup** | The guided steps that prepare a year: terms, levels, subjects, then classes, teachers and enrollments. |

## How they fit together

<Steps>

<Step>
### Create the year

You create the year with a label and dates. It starts as a **Draft**. See
[Create an academic year](../build-the-academic-foundation/create-an-academic-year).
</Step>

<Step>
### Prepare it in year setup

You date the terms, choose the levels and subjects for the year, then prepare classes, teachers
and enrollments. See [Academic year setup](../build-the-academic-foundation/academic-year-setup).
</Step>

<Step>
### Activate it

When setup is complete, **Activate year** makes it the **Active** year.
</Step>

<Step>
### Close it at the end

When the year is over, **Close Academic Year** makes it **Closed**.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Academic year statuses

| Status | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | The year is being prepared. You can edit its setup and enroll students. It is not the current year yet. |
| **Active** | The current school year. Day-to-day work happens here. |
| **Closed** | The year is over and read-only. Students can no longer be enrolled in it. You can still record late payments on its unpaid installments. |

### Reopening a year

**Reopen Academic Year** is offered on a closed year. There is no separate "Reopened" status: the
year goes back to **Draft**. You can edit its setup again and still enroll students in it, but it
is not the active year. Only a **Closed** year refuses new enrollments.

## Grading status of a term

Each term also has a **Grading Status**, set with **Update Grading Settings** on the term:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Open** | Teachers can enter marks and write remarks. |
| **Locked** | Counting marks and remarks can no longer be entered. Administrators can still override an average. |
| **Finalized** | The term's grades are final. Counting marks, remarks and averages can no longer be changed. |

A term or marking-period report card can be published only when that period is **Locked** or
**Finalized**. See [Gradebook](../../teaching/gradebook) and
[Report cards](../../teaching/report-cards).

## The year you are looking at

Many pages (classes, enrollments, fees, payment plans, payments and expenses) show the data of the
academic year selected at the top of the screen. Check the selected year before you start working.
