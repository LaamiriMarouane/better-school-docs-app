---
title: Review the school setup checklist
description: Walk through the setup areas once more before the school starts working in the platform every day.
---

Before teachers, families and students start using the platform every day, take one last pass
over the setup. Most of it is already in front of you: the **Year setup** shows which steps you
confirmed for the year, and which you skipped.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
The administrators who prepared the school and will sign it off for launch.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- You created the school account and chose how to start. See
  [Choose how to start](../first-time-school-setup/choose-how-to-start).
- You worked through the year's **Year setup**. See
  [Academic year setup](../build-the-academic-foundation/academic-year-setup).

## Review the setup

<Steps>

<Step>
### Check the school details

| Check | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Name, contact details and logo are correct. | **Settings → School**, **School Info** tab. See [Set up the school profile](../first-time-school-setup/set-up-the-school-profile). |
| The modules you use are switched on, and the others are off. | **Settings → Modules**. See [Configure school modules](../first-time-school-setup/configure-school-modules). |
| Country, currency and time zone are right. | **Settings → School**, **Regional** tab. See [Set regional preferences](../first-time-school-setup/set-regional-preferences). |
| If you loaded sample data, it is cleared. | The **Sample data** box on the **School Info** tab is gone once it is cleared. See [Choose how to start](../first-time-school-setup/choose-how-to-start). |
</Step>

<Step>
### Check the year

Go to **Settings → Academic**, open the **Academic Year** tab, open the year with **View
Details**, and click **Year setup**. Read the status chip on each step card:

- **Terms**, **Levels**, **Subjects** and **Subject associations** show **Confirmed**.
- Setup is completed, and the year is active. If not, click **Complete setup**, then
  **Activate year**.
- Each readiness step — **Grading**, **Classes**, **Teachers**, **Supervisions**,
  **Admissions / intake**, **Enrollments**, and **Transport** when shown — is **Confirmed**, or
  **Skipped** on purpose. A step still **Not confirmed** is work to finish or to skip knowingly.
</Step>

<Step>
### Check people and logins

- Teachers, students, guardians and staff are added, and students are enrolled with a class. See
  [Onboard People](../onboard-people) and [Enroll Students](../enroll-students).
- Everyone who must sign in has a portal account. Go to **Access → Users** and open the
  **Pending portal accounts** tab: it lists the people who still have no login. See
  [Create portal accounts](../onboard-people/create-portal-accounts).
</Step>

<Step>
### Check daily operations

| Check | See |
| --- | --- |
| Each level uses the right attendance mode (per period or per day). | [Configure attendance modes](../configure-core-operations/configure-attendance-modes) |
| Fees and payment plans are ready, if you bill through the platform. | [Configure fees and payment plans](../configure-core-operations/configure-fees-and-payment-plans) |
| The timetable is generated and reviewed. | [Configure the timetable](../configure-core-operations/configure-the-timetable) |
</Step>

<Step>
### Fix and sign off

Fix each gap from the step or settings page it belongs to, then check it again. When everything
above is in place, [validate role access](./validate-role-access) and
[activate the first timetable](./publish-the-first-timetable).
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Readiness never blocks activation">
You can activate the year with readiness steps unfinished. Use this review to decide what must be
ready on day one and what can wait.
</Callout>
