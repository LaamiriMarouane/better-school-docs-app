---
title: Validate role access
description: Before launch, check that each role sees what it needs — and nothing more — then fix any gap.
---

Each person signs in with one or more roles: **Admin**, **Teacher**, **Guardian**, **Student** or
**Staff**, plus any staff roles your school created. Before launch, check that every role opens
the right areas, and that each person holds the right roles.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators preparing the school for launch.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- People are added and have their portal accounts. See
  [Create portal accounts](../onboard-people/create-portal-accounts).
- If you use your own staff roles, they are created. See
  [Roles and permissions](../../settings/roles-and-permissions).

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Review the roles

Go to **Access → Roles & permissions**. Choose a role from the list to see its permissions.

- Built-in roles show a lock icon. They are defined by the product. Their name and permissions cannot be
  changed.
- For your own staff roles, check that each one has only the permissions that job needs. Saving
  a role updates the permissions of every user who holds it straight away.
</Step>

<Step>
### Check who holds which role

Go to **Access → Users**. Use the **Role** filter to list the users of one role, and the
**Status** filter to find **Enabled** or **Disabled** accounts. From a user's row menu:

- **Assign roles** — add or remove roles. Only the school owner can give the **Admin** role, and a
  student account keeps the **Student** role only.
- **Reset password** — when someone cannot sign in.
- **Disable** or **Enable** — stop or restore a person's access.
</Step>

<Step>
### Test each role with a real sign-in

For every role your school uses, sign in with an account that holds it and look at the menu and
the home page. Use the table below as a guide.

| Role | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| **Teacher** | Their own timetable and classes, taking attendance, assignments and grades. |
| **Guardian** | Only their own children, with their timetable, attendance and results. |
| **Student** | Their own timetable, assignments and results. |
| **Staff** | Only the areas their staff role allows. |
| **Admin** | All the areas the administrators need. |

If your own account holds several roles, switch between them from the profile menu instead of
signing out. See [Switch between available roles](../access-and-authentication/switch-between-available-roles).

Teachers, guardians, students and staff can also use the mobile app. Check it with the same
accounts. Administration is web only.
</Step>

<Step>
### Fix the gaps

- **A menu or action is missing** — add the permission to the person's staff role, or assign the
  missing role.
- **A person sees too much** — remove the permission from their staff role, or remove the extra
  role.
- **A person cannot sign in** — check that the account is **Enabled**, or reset the password.

Then check again with the same account.
</Step>

</Steps>
