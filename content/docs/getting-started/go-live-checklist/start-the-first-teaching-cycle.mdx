---
title: Start the first teaching cycle
description: Run the first days of teaching — attendance, class logs, first assignments and grading — and follow how it goes.
---

Once the timetable is active, class sessions appear on everyone's timetable and teaching starts in
the platform. This page lists what to do in the first days, and what to watch.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
School leaders and administrators launching daily use, and the teachers they support.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- The year is active. See [Academic year setup](../build-the-academic-foundation/academic-year-setup).
- Every role opens the right areas. See [Validate role access](./validate-role-access).
- The timetable is active and class sessions are showing. See
  [Activate the first timetable](./publish-the-first-timetable).

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open grading for the term

Teachers can enter marks only while the term's **Grading Status** is **Open**. In the academic
year's list of terms, use **Update Grading Settings** on the current term to check it. See
[Gradebook](../../teaching/gradebook).
</Step>

<Step>
### Take attendance from day one

- For levels in **Per Period** mode, teachers open the session on the **Timetable**, choose
  **Mark Attendance**, change only the students who are not present, and choose **Save
  Attendance**.
- For levels in **Per Day** mode, administrators take attendance once a day from **Attendance →
  Student Attendance**.

See [Class sessions & attendance](../../teaching/sessions-and-attendance).
</Step>

<Step>
### Log what was taught

After each class, teachers choose **Add Activity** on the session to record the topics covered
and any homework. They review their record in the **Activity journal**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Set the first assignments

Teachers go to **Assessments → Assignments**, choose **Add Assignment**, and pick **File** or
**Quiz**. See [Assignments](../../teaching/assignments).
</Step>

<Step>
### Tell families what to expect

Go to **Communication → Announcements** and choose **Add Announcement** to tell families that the
school now runs in the platform. See [Announcements](../../communication/announcements).
</Step>

</Steps>

## Watch the first weeks

| What to watch | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Sessions marked **Auto-completed** | Nobody marked them. Remind the teacher to take attendance and log the session. |
| Attendance not taken for a class | Check that the teacher has a portal account and can see the session. |
| A wrong teacher, room or time | Deactivate the timetable group, edit it, and activate it again. See [Activate the first timetable](./publish-the-first-timetable). |
| A family or student cannot sign in | Check the account in **Access → Users**. See [Validate role access](./validate-role-access). |
