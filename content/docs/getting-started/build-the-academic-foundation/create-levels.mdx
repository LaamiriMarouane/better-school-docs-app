---
title: Create levels
description: Define the grade or year levels that organise your school's academic hierarchy.
---

A **level** represents a grade or year of study — for example *Grade 1*, *Year 7*, or
*Première année*. Levels are the backbone of your academic structure: classes are
attached to levels, and students are enrolled into a class at a given level.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators building the core academic hierarchy for an academic year.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- An **academic year** must already exist and be selected in the year switcher at the
  top of the screen. Every level you create belongs to that year.
- Plan your full list of levels first (for example *Grade 1* through *Grade 6*) so the
  ordering and codes stay consistent.

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the Levels page

From the admin sidebar, go to **Academic → Levels**. Make sure the academic year shown
in the top switcher is the one you want to build.
</Step>

<Step>
### Start a new level

Click **Add Level**. A form opens where you enter the level details.

<Screenshot
  label="Create level form"
  caption="The create level form: Level label, Code, Description and Attendance mode."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Fill in the level details

Complete the fields below, then click **Create Level**.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Level label** | Yes | The display name shown across the app, e.g. *Grade 1*. |
| **Code** | Yes | A short identifier, max 15 characters, e.g. `G1`. Used in reports and timetables. |
| **Description** | No | Optional notes about the level. |
| **Student attendance mode** | No | How attendance is taken for this level (for example per-day or per-session). It can be locked later once attendance is in use. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Confirm the level appears

The new level is added to the Levels list immediately. Repeat for every level your
school offers.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="Attendance mode">
Once attendance has been recorded for a level, its **attendance mode** is locked and
can no longer be changed. Choose it carefully when you create the level.
</Callout>
