---
title: Create terms
description: Divide the academic year into terms or semesters used for grading and reporting.
---

A **term** is a period within the academic year — for example *Term 1*, *Semester 2*,
or *Trimester 3*. Terms organise grading, reporting, and many academic workflows, so
they are dated right after the academic year itself.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators structuring the calendar of an academic year.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- The **academic year** must already exist.
- Your school's term names (for example *Semester 1* and *Semester 2*) are defined once under
  **Settings → Academic → Term Structure**. If none exist yet, the terms table shows a hint with
  **Open Term Structure**.
- Plan the term dates so they sit **within** the academic year and **do not overlap**
  each other.

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the year's terms

Go to **Settings → Academic**, open the **Academic Year** tab, and open the year with
**View Details**. The year page shows its terms as a table. The **Terms** step of
[Academic year setup](./academic-year-setup) shows the same table, already filled with one row
per term of your term structure.

<Screenshot
  label="Create term form"
  caption="The create term form: label, dates and the active toggle."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Add and date the terms

Click **Add Term** to add a row, then fill it in directly in the table.

| Column | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Term** | Yes | Pick the term from your term structure. It cannot be changed once saved. |
| **Start Date** | Yes | Must fall within the academic year. |
| **End Date** | Yes | Must be on or after the start date, and within the academic year. |

The **Duration** column shows the number of days as you pick the dates.
</Step>

<Step>
### Save

Click **Save** to save every row at once. If two terms overlap, or a row is missing its term or
dates, a message names the problem — fix the dates and save again.
</Step>

<Step>
### Activate the current term

Use the **Activate Term** row action on the term in progress, and confirm. Use **Deactivate Term**
to switch it off. Other row actions let you **Update Grading Settings** for the term or
**Delete** it.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Tip">
Keep the current term active so grading and reporting default to the right period. Update it as
the year progresses.
</Callout>
