---
title: Create terms
description: Divide the academic year into terms or semesters used for grading and reporting.
---

A **term** is a period within the academic year — for example *Term 1*, *Semester 2*,
or *Trimester 3*. Terms organise grading, reporting, and many academic workflows, so
they are created right after the academic year itself.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators structuring the calendar of an academic year.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- The **academic year** must already exist — terms are created inside it.
- Plan the term dates so they sit **within** the academic year and **do not overlap**
  each other.

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the Terms section

Go to **Settings → Academic**, open the academic year you are building, and find
its **Terms** section. Click **Add**.

<Screenshot
  label="Create term form"
  caption="The create term form: label, dates and the active toggle."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Fill in the term details

Complete the fields below, then click **Submit**.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Label** | Yes | The display name, e.g. *Term 1*. |
| **Start date** | Yes | Must fall within the academic year's date range. |
| **End date** | Yes | Must be on or after the start date, and within the academic year. |
| **Active** | No | Marks this as the current term. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Resolve any overlap warning

If the dates overlap an existing term, the form shows a warning and blocks saving.
Adjust the start or end date until the term no longer overlaps, then submit.
</Step>

<Step>
### Repeat for every term

Add each term of the year. The new term appears in the list immediately.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Tip">
Mark the current term as **Active** so grading and reporting default to the right
period. Update it as the year progresses.
</Callout>
