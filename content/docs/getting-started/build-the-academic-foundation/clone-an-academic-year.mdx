---
title: Clone an academic year
description: Copy a previous year's setup — terms, curriculum, fees, classes, and more — into a new academic year instead of rebuilding it by hand.
---

When you start a new school year, you don't have to rebuild everything. **Cloning** copies a
previous year's setup into the new one — terms, levels and subjects, the curriculum, fees, classes,
and more — so you only adjust what changed.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators setting up a new academic year.
</Callout>

## Year statuses

An academic year moves through three statuses:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | You're still building the year. It can be edited freely, and students aren't enrolled yet. New years start here. |
| **Active** | The year in use. Enrollments and billing happen in the active year, and only **one** year is active at a time. |
| **Closed** | Archived and read-only — kept for history. |

You **activate** a draft year when it's ready, **close** an active year at the end of the cycle, and
can **reopen** a closed year or send an active year **back to draft** if you need to keep editing.

<Callout title="Switching the active year">
The year shown in the **switcher** at the top of the screen is the one you're working in. After
cloning, switch to the new year to keep building it.
</Callout>

## Clone a previous year

Cloning happens when you **create** the new year:

<Steps>

<Step>
### Start a new academic year

Go to **Settings → Academic → Academic Year → Add**, and fill in the new year's label and dates.
</Step>

<Step>
### Turn on cloning and pick the source

Enable **cloning**, then choose the **previous year** to copy from.
</Step>

<Step>
### Choose what to copy

Tick the categories you want. Everything is selected by default — untick anything you don't need
(see below).
</Step>

<Step>
### Map the term dates

If you copy terms, set each term's **new dates** in the new year. Dates don't shift automatically,
so you tell the system where each term lands.
</Step>

<Step>
### Submit

The new year is created and the copy runs in the background.
</Step>

</Steps>

## What you can copy

Everything is selected by default; untick what you don't want. The options group like this:

| Group | What it copies |
| --- | --- |
| **Foundation** | Terms, levels, and subjects. |
| **Curriculum** | Which subjects each level studies (and per term), course templates, and exam templates. |
| **Finance** | Services, fees per level, payment plans, and expense categories. |
| **Operations** | Attendance settings, timetable configs, and behaviour categories & levels. |
| **Classes** | The class sections themselves (e.g. *Grade 9A*, *9B*). |

<Callout title="Some options depend on others">
A few options need their building blocks. For example, *subjects per level* needs both **levels**
and **subjects**; *fees* need **levels** and **services**; *classes* need **levels**. The form keeps
these in sync, so you can't copy something without what it relies on.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Module-specific options">
**Expense categories** and **behaviour categories** can only be copied if your school has the
**Expenses** and **Behaviour** modules turned on.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Transport comes across automatically">
If your school uses the **Transport** module, your **routes and their stops** are copied into the new
year as part of the clone — there's no separate checkbox for them. **Vehicles and drivers** aren't
tied to a year, so cloned routes simply reuse the same ones rather than duplicating them. The stops
arrive **empty**, though — without **zones** or **assigned riders** — see below.
</Callout>

## What's not copied

Cloning copies the **setup**, not the **people or records**:

- **Student enrollments** — students are enrolled fresh each year; cloning never carries them over.
- **Records** — behaviour incidents, grades, attendance, and payments stay with their own year. Only
  the *settings* come across, not last year's records.
- **Transport zones** — zones belong to a specific year, so they aren't copied. Your **routes and
  stops do** come across, but each cloned stop starts **without a zone** and falls back to the
  level's default transport fee until you assign a new-year zone to it. Set the zones up again and
  reassign them — see [Transport map & zones](../../settings/transport-map).
- **Student stop assignments** — which students ride which stop isn't copied. A rider is tied to that
  year's **enrollment** and to the subscription that pays for the seat, and enrollments are created
  fresh — so once students are enrolled in the new year, you assign them to their stops again.

## The copy runs in the background

Cloning can move a lot of data, so it runs as a **background job**:

- A **progress** indicator shows each stage as it copies.
- Only **one clone** can run for a year at a time, and you can **cancel** one in progress.
- When it finishes, a **summary** shows how much was copied — and flags anything skipped or worth a
  second look.

You can keep working while it runs, and switch to the new year once it's done.

## Common use cases

- **New school year** — clone last year and adjust the few things that changed.
- **Same curriculum, new dates** — copy everything and just remap the term dates.
- **Fresh pricing** — copy the academic setup but untick fees and plans to rebuild them.
