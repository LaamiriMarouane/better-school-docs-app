---
title: Create subjects
description: Add the subjects taught at your school so they can be assigned to classes, teachers, and timetables.
---

A **subject** is a topic taught at your school — for example *Mathematics*, *Physics*,
or *Arabic*. Subjects are assigned to classes, linked to teachers, and placed on the
timetable. Creating them is one of the first academic-foundation steps.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators setting up the school's curriculum.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- It helps to have created your **rooms** first if you want to restrict a subject to
  certain room types (for example a *Lab* for Chemistry).

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the Subjects page

From the admin sidebar, go to **Academic → Subjects**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Start a new subject

Click **Add Subject** to open the create form. To add many subjects at once, use **Import**.

<Screenshot
  label="Create subject form"
  caption="The create subject form: name, code, description, colour and allowed room types."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Fill in the subject details

Complete the fields below, then click **Create Subject**.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Subject name** | Yes | The display name, e.g. *Mathematics*. |
| **Code** | Yes | A short identifier, e.g. `MATH`. |
| **Description** | No | Optional notes about the subject. |
| **Colour** | No | A colour used to identify the subject across the timetable and gradebook. |
| **Allowed room types** | No | Restrict where the subject can be scheduled (for example *Lab* or *Computer room*). Leave empty to allow any room. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Confirm the subject appears

The new subject is added to the Subjects list immediately. Repeat for every subject in
your curriculum.
</Step>

<Step>
### Link subjects to your levels

In [Academic year setup](./academic-year-setup), the **Subject associations** step lets you
choose which subjects each level studies in the year you are preparing.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Tip">
Pick a distinct **colour** for each subject. It makes the timetable far easier to read
once scheduling begins.
</Callout>
