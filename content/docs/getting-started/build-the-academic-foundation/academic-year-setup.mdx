**Academic year setup** is a guided workspace for a single year. You finish a short **definition
gate** (required before activation), then optional **readiness** steps (classes, teachers,
grading, and so on). When the gate is done, you **Complete setup** and **Activate** the year.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators preparing a new academic year before day-to-day operations.
</Callout>

## Open year setup

1. Go to **Settings → Academic** and open the **Academic Year** tab.
2. Open the year → **Year details**.
3. Click **Year setup** (or **Open setup hub** / **Start setup** when offered after creating a
   year).

Setup opens full screen. Use **Exit to year** to return to year details.

## Definition vs readiness

| Track | Steps | Blocks activation? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Definition** (gate) | Terms, Levels, Subjects, Subject associations | **Yes** — all must be satisfied. |
| **Readiness** | Grading, Classes, Teachers, Supervisions, Admissions / intake, Enrollments | **No** — you can skip or finish later. |

Finish the definition steps first. Readiness navigation stays locked until the gate is satisfied.

## Definition steps (gate)

| Step | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| **Terms** | Add at least one term with dates for this year. |
| **Levels** | Activate which school levels run this year. |
| **Subjects** | Ensure the school subject list exists (school-wide vocabulary). |
| **Subject associations** | Say which subjects each level teaches this year (per term as needed). |

You cannot skip a gate step. Counts and checkmarks come from live completeness for the year.

## Readiness steps (never block activation)

| Step | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| **Grading** | Year grading policy, categories, schemes, and related setup (same tools as [Grading setup](../../teaching/grading-setup)). |
| **Classes** | Create class sections for active levels. |
| **Teachers** | Associate teachers with this year (often from the previous year). |
| **Supervisions** | Copy or set supervision / homeroom-style responsibilities. |
| **Admissions / intake** | Promote or decide returning students from the previous year (first year: use admissions/manual intake instead). |
| **Enrollments** | Place students (level, optional class and payment plan) — fills in over time. |

Use **Skip for now** on a readiness step if you will finish it after activation. You can undo a skip
later.

## Complete setup and activate

<Steps>

<Step>
### Complete setup

When every definition step is satisfied, click **Complete setup**. That marks the year's setup as
completed — it does **not** activate the year by itself.
</Step>

<Step>
### Activate year

When setup is completed and the year is still a draft, click **Activate year**. Only then does the
year become the active operating year (per your school's year rules).
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Readiness is optional for activation">
You can activate with empty classes or unfinished enrollments. Operational steps are there to get
you ready to teach and bill — they do not block **Activate year**.
</Callout>
