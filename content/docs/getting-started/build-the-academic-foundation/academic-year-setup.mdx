title: Academic year setup
description: Walk through the guided year setup — confirm each step for the year, complete setup, and activate the year.
---

**Year setup** is a guided workspace for a single year. You work through a short **definition
gate** (required before activation), then **readiness** steps (classes, teachers, enrollments,
and so on). A step counts as ready only after you click **Confirm for this year** on it. When
every gate step is confirmed, you **Complete setup** and **Activate year**.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators preparing a new academic year before day-to-day operations.
</Callout>

## Open year setup

1. Go to **Settings → Academic** and open the **Academic Year** tab.
2. Open the year with **View Details**.
3. Click **Year setup** (or **Open setup hub** / **Start setup** when offered after creating a
   year).

Setup opens full screen. Everything you need is done inside it, step by step. Use **Exit to year**
to return to the year page.

## Confirm each step for the year

Every step card shows a status chip:

| Chip | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Confirmed** | You confirmed the step for this year. It counts as ready. |
| **Not confirmed** | Data may already be there, but you have not confirmed it yet. |
| **Skipped** | You chose **Skip for now** on a readiness step. |

- Click **Confirm for this year** on the step card or at the top of the step page once you have
  checked its content.
- A definition step can only be confirmed once it has data for the year.
- A readiness step can be confirmed even when empty (for example, no transport routes this
  year). The app asks you to confirm that choice first.
- Click **Unconfirm** to take a confirmation back. Click **Skipped — undo** to reverse a skip.

## Definition gate

You must confirm all of these before you can complete setup. They cannot be skipped.

| Step | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| **Terms** | Date the year's terms. |
| **Levels** | Switch on the levels that run this year. For a level with tracks, use the switch on each track to say whether it is offered this year. |
| **Subjects** | Make sure the subjects your school teaches exist. |
| **Subject associations** | Say which subjects each level teaches this year (per term as needed). |

## Readiness steps (never block activation)

The readiness cards stay locked until the definition gate is confirmed.

| Step | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| **Grading** | Review the year's grading setup (same tools as [Grading setup](../../teaching/grading-setup)). |
| **Classes** | Create classes for the levels and tracks running this year. |
| **Teachers** | Select teachers from the previous year and assign them to this year. |
| **Supervisions** | Copy or set supervision responsibilities. |
| **Admissions / intake** | Decide each returning student's place this year: **Promote**, **Retain**, **Graduate** or **Leaving**. You can decide now or send the decision to the parent to confirm, and open or close re-enrollment with **Open re-enrollment** / **Close re-enrollment**. |
| **Enrollments** | Enroll students at a level, then assign a class and payment plan (both optional). Returning students you promoted or retained appear under **Waiting to be enrolled**: pick a class (optional) and click **Enroll**. |
| **Transport** | Shown when transport is used. Copy last year's zones and routes, check where riders live, and confirm returning riders' stops. |

Promoted and retained students are re-enrolled for the new year but are not yet in a class. The
**Admissions / intake** step shows how many are waiting, with **Enroll them** to jump to the
**Enrollments** step.

### Fees & payment plans

The **Fees & payment plans** card (shown to users who can create fees and payment plans) lets you
**Copy fees and payment plans from last year**: services, fees with their per-level prices, and
payment plans. Adjust the amounts afterwards on the finance pages. This card is not a step and
does not need confirming.

Use **Skip for now** on a readiness step you will finish after activation.

## Complete setup and activate

<Steps>

<Step>
### Complete setup

When every definition step is confirmed, click **Complete setup**. If a step is missing, the
button stays disabled and the missing steps are listed under it. Completing setup does **not**
activate the year by itself.
</Step>

<Step>
### Activate year

When setup is completed and the year is still a draft, click **Activate year**. The year then
becomes the active year.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Readiness is optional for activation">
You can activate with empty classes or unfinished enrollments. Readiness steps help you get
ready to teach and bill — they do not block **Activate year**.
</Callout>
