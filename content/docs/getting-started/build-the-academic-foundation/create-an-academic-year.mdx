title: Create an academic year
description: Create the academic year your terms, classes, enrollments, and scheduling are organised around.
---

An **academic year** represents a school cycle — for example *2025–2026*. Terms, classes,
enrollments, fees, and timetables are all organised around it, so this is the very first
thing to set up.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators starting a new school cycle.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- Decide the **start and end dates** of the cycle.

<Screenshot
  label="Academic years page with the Add button"
  caption="Academic years are managed under Settings."
/>

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the Academic Years page

Go to **Settings → Academic**, open the **Academic Year** tab, then click **Add Academic Year**.

<Screenshot
  label="Create academic year form"
  caption="The form: label, start date, and end date."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Fill in the year details

Complete the fields below.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Label** | Yes | The display name, e.g. *2025–2026*. |
| **Start Date** | Yes | The first day of the cycle. |
| **End Date** | Yes | The last day of the cycle. Must be after the start date. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Submit and start setup

Click **Submit**. The new year is created as a **draft**, and you are asked what to do next:
**Start setup**, **Go to details**, or **Back to List**. Choose **Start setup** to open
[Academic year setup](./academic-year-setup), where you date the terms, choose levels and
subjects, bring teachers and returning students across from the previous year, and finally
activate the year.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Manage a year from the list

Each year in the list has a row menu:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **View Details** | Opens the year page, with its terms and the **Year setup** button. |
| **Set as active academic year** | Makes a draft year the active year. |
| **Close Academic Year** | Shown on the active year. A closed year becomes read-only. |
| **Reopen Academic Year** | Shown on a closed year. It goes back to draft so you can make changes; no enrollments are allowed while it is reopened. |
| **Edit** | Change the label or dates. |
| **Delete** | Remove the year. |
