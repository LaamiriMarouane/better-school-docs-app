Use this chapter to build online forms — admission applications and general
data-collection forms — and to process the submissions they receive.

A **form** is an online page applicants or families fill in. You design it in a
drag-and-drop builder, publish it — on a shareable link, or privately to chosen
audiences — and review every response from one place. **Admission** forms go a
step further: accepting a child creates the student and their pre-inscription.
