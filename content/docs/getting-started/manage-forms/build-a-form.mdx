The **builder** is where you design what applicants see. You drag fields onto a
canvas, arrange them into sections, and set each field's behaviour.

## The builder layout

| Area | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Left sidebar** | The field components you can drag onto the form. |
| **Canvas** | The form itself — **sections**, each holding **rows** and **columns** of fields. |
| **Right panel** | The properties of the field you select, or the **Admission Fields** panel. |
| **Header** | Undo / redo, preview, settings, and save. |

## Add and arrange fields

<Steps>

<Step>
### Drag fields onto the canvas

Drag a field component from the left sidebar onto a section. Drop it on a section
header to start a new row, or next to an existing field to share its row.
</Step>

<Step>
### Set field properties

Select a field to open the properties panel on the right. There you set the
**label**, mark it **required**, add **validation**, define **options** for choice
fields, and control its **visibility**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Organise into sections

Group related fields into sections and reorder rows and sections by dragging.
A section can be made **repeatable** so applicants add more than one entry — for
example several children on one application.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Admission fields

Admission forms come with **Student** and **Guardian** sections already built in.
To add more recognised fields, open the **Admission Fields** panel from the right.

It has two tabs — **Student** and **Guardian** — each listing:

- **System fields** — recognised fields such as date of birth or address. Adding
  them lets a submission be matched to a real person.
- **Custom fields** — extra fields your school defined. See
  [Custom fields](../../settings/custom-fields).

Drag a field from the panel into the matching section, or use its **+** button.
A field that is already on the form is marked **Added**.

<Callout title="Why system fields matter">
System fields carry a hidden mapping (for example `student.firstName`). That
mapping is what lets the platform turn an accepted submission into a student
record without re-typing anything.
</Callout>

## Preview and settings

- **Preview** — switch the header to preview to see the form as an applicant
  would, at desktop, tablet, or mobile width.
- **Settings** — open the settings dialog to set the **presentation mode**
  (a single page or a step-by-step wizard), the **languages**, the **theme**, the
  **branding** (introduction and thank-you messages, logo, terms and privacy
  policy), and the form's **schedule**.

## Save your work

The header shows an **Unsaved changes** marker while you edit. Use **Save** to
keep your changes. Undo and redo are available, along with keyboard shortcuts —
copy, paste, and duplicate a selected field.

<Callout title="Saving is not publishing">
Saving keeps your design. The form stays a **draft** and is not yet open to
applicants — see [Publish and share a form](./publish-and-share-a-form).
</Callout>
