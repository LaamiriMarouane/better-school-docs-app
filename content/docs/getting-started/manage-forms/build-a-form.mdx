title: Build a form

The **builder** is where you design what applicants see. You drag fields onto a
canvas, arrange them into sections, and set each field's behaviour. All sections
appear one after the other on a single page.

## The builder layout

| Area | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Components** list | The fields you can drag onto the form: inputs, choices, dates and times, uploads, layout elements, and your school's custom fields. |
| **Canvas** | The form itself — **sections**, each holding rows of fields. |
| **Properties** panel | The settings of the field you select, or the admission fields panel. |
| **Header** | The form name and status, **Publish**, undo / redo, **Preview**, **Levels** (Admission forms), **Settings** and **Save**. |

## Add and arrange fields

<Steps>

<Step>
### Add sections

A form needs at least one section before you can save it. Use **Add Another
Section** under the canvas, and type a title for each section.
</Step>

<Step>
### Drag fields onto the canvas

Drag a field from the **Components** list onto a section. Drop it on the section's
empty area to start a new row, or next to an existing field to share its row.
Drag rows and sections to reorder them.
</Step>

<Step>
### Set field properties

Select a field to open its **Properties**. There you set the **Label**, the
**Placeholder** and an optional helper text, the **Options** of a choice field,
and the **Validation** rules — **Required field**, lengths, number or date limits,
file size and types.
</Step>

<Step>
### Show a field only when needed

In **Properties**, use **Show only when…** to display a field only when another
field matches a condition — for example, show "Allergy details" only when
"Has allergies" is answered Yes. Pick the field in **When this field**, then a
**Condition** and a **Value**.
</Step>

</Steps>

Each field on the canvas has its own buttons to duplicate, copy or delete it.

## Admission fields

Admission forms come with the **Guardian Information** and **Student
Information** sections already built in, including the student's level. These
built-in fields are marked **Locked field** and can't be removed.

To add more recognised fields, choose **Admission Fields** at the bottom of the
**Components** list. The panel has a **Student** tab and a **Guardian** tab, each
listing:

- **System fields** — fields the platform recognises, such as a date of birth.
  When you accept an application, their answers fill the new records for you.
- **Custom fields** — extra fields your school defined. See
  [Custom fields](../../settings/custom-fields).

Drag a field from the panel into the matching section. A field that is already on
the form is marked **Added**.

## Choose the levels (Admission forms)

An Admission form can't be published until you choose who can apply.

1. In the builder header, choose **Levels**. Until you pick levels, the button
   shows **Not set**.
2. Select the levels applicants can apply to. When a level has tracks, open at
   least one of its tracks too.
3. Choose **Save**. The button now shows how many levels (and tracks) are open.

Applicants then pick one of these levels for each child on the form.

## Preview and schedule

- **Preview** — see the form as an applicant would, at desktop, tablet or mobile
  width. Choose **Edit** to go back to the canvas.
- **Settings** — set the form's schedule: **Opens at** and **Closes at**. See
  [Publish and share a form](./publish-and-share-a-form).

## Save your work

The header shows **Unsaved Changes** while you edit. Choose **Save** to keep your
changes. Undo and redo are in the header.

<Callout title="Saving is not publishing">
Saving keeps your design. A draft stays closed to applicants until you publish it
— see [Publish and share a form](./publish-and-share-a-form).
</Callout>
