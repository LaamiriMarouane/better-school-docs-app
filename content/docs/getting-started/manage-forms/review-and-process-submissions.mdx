title: Review and process submissions
description: Review responses and turn admission applications into student records.
---

Every response to a published form becomes a **submission**. This is where you
review what was sent and, for **Admission** forms, decide on each applicant.

## Open the submissions list

From the **Forms** list, use **View submissions** on a form's row. This is
available once the form is **Published**.

Each submission shows its **code**, the **submitter**, a **status**, and when it
was submitted.

## Submission statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **New** | Just received, not yet reviewed. |
| **Under review** | Being looked at. |
| **Partially processed** | Some applicants decided, others still pending. |
| **Completed** | All applicants have a final decision. |
| **Accepted** / **Rejected** / **Waitlisted** | The outcome of a single-applicant submission. |

## Review a submission

Open a submission to see the **submitted data**, grouped by the same sections the
applicant filled in. Repeatable sections show one block per entry — for example
one block per child on a family application.

## Process an admission

On an **Admission** form, each **Student** section carries its own decision. One
submission with several children produces one decision per child.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Choose a decision

On a student's section, pick one of three actions:

| Action | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Accept** | Approves the applicant and creates their student record. |
| **Reject** | Declines the applicant. |
| **Waitlist** | Holds the applicant for a later decision. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Resolve any matches

When you **Accept**, the platform checks whether the student or a guardian already
exists — usually matched by email. If matches are found, a dialog asks you to
choose, for each person:

- **Associate existing** — link the application to the record already in the
  school.
- **Create new** — add a new record anyway.

If an email already exists in your school, you **must** associate the existing
record. An email that belongs to another school cannot be reused.
</Step>

<Step>
### Confirm

Confirm the decision. An accepted applicant gets a student record, and a
**View student** link appears so you can open it.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Accept is final">
A **Rejected** or **Waitlisted** applicant can still be moved to another decision
later. **Accept** cannot be undone — once accepted, the student record exists.
</Callout>

## After accepting

Accepting creates the student record but does not place the student in a class.
To finish, enroll the new student — see
[Create an enrollment](../enroll-students/create-an-enrollment).
