title: Review and process submissions

Every response to a published form becomes a **submission**. This is where you
review what was sent and, for **Admission** forms, decide on each child.

## Open the submissions list

In the **Forms** list, open a form's row actions and choose **View Submissions**.
This is available once the form has been published.

Each submission shows its **Submission Code**, the **Submitter Name**, a
**Status** and when it was submitted. Use the search and filters to find a
submission by status or date.

<Callout title="Possible duplicates">
When the same email has already answered the form, the new submission is tagged
**Possible duplicate of** followed by the earlier submission's code. Compare the
two before you decide.
</Callout>

## Submission statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **New** | Just received. |
| **Under Review** | An application whose children are all still waiting for a decision. |
| **Partially processed** | Some children have a decision, others are still waiting. |
| **Waitlisted** | Every child has a decision, and at least one is on the waitlist. |
| **Completed** | Every child is accepted or rejected. |

The status of an application follows the decisions you take on its children —
you don't set it by hand.

## Review a submission

Open a submission to see the **Submitted data**, grouped by the same sections the
applicant filled in. When a family applied for several children, each child
appears as its own block.

## Process an admission

On an **Admission** form, each child in the **Student Information** section gets
its own decision. One application with three children means three decisions.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Choose a decision

On a child's block, pick one of three actions:

| Action | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Accept** | Creates the student, links their guardians, and creates a pre-inscription for the level they applied to. |
| **Reject** | Declines the child. You can still waitlist or accept them later. |
| **Waitlist** | Holds the child for a later decision. You can accept or reject them later. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Resolve any matches

When you **Accept**, the platform checks whether the student or a guardian is
already in your school. If it finds a match, a dialog shows each one and what it
was matched by, and you choose:

- **Associate with existing** — link the application to the person already in
  your school.
- **Create new anyway** — add a new person.

If an email already exists in your school, you must associate the existing
person. An email used in another school can't be used here.
</Step>

<Step>
### Confirm

Confirm the decision. The confirm button becomes active after a few seconds, so
you have time to read what will happen. Once a child is accepted, a **View
Student** link appears so you can open their profile.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Accept is final">
A rejected or waitlisted child can still be moved to another decision later.
**Accept** can't be undone.
</Callout>

## After accepting

Accepting creates the student and their pre-inscription, but does not place the
student in a class. To finish, enroll the student from their pre-inscription — see
[Pre-inscriptions](../enroll-students/pre-inscriptions) and
[Create an enrollment](../enroll-students/create-an-enrollment).
