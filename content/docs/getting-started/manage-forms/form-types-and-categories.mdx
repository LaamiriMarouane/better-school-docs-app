title: Form types and categories
description: Understand the Admission and General categories, and the Public and Private types.
---

Every form has two settings that decide what it does and who can submit it: a
**category** and a **type**. You choose both when you create the form.

## Category — what the form is for

| Category | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Admission** | An application form for new students. It is pre-built with a **Student** section and a **Guardian** section, and each response can be reviewed and turned into a student record. |
| **General** | A free-form data-collection form — surveys, requests, registrations of any kind. Responses are stored and reviewed, but are not linked to student records. |

### What makes Admission different

When you create an Admission form, two things happen automatically:

- The form starts with **Student** and **Guardian** sections already in place, so
  the response can be matched to real people.
- You must choose the **levels** applicants can apply to. This keeps applications
  tied to the levels your school is actually admitting for.

Admission responses also unlock the **decision actions** — Accept, Reject, and
Waitlist — covered in
[Review and process submissions](./review-and-process-submissions).

<Callout title="General forms have no student link">
A General form collects answers, nothing more. There is no Accept action and no
student record is created. Use Admission whenever the goal is to enroll someone.
</Callout>

## Type — who can submit

| Type | Who can submit |
| --- | --- |
| **Public** | Anyone with the share link. Use this for open admission campaigns and public sign-ups. |
| **Private** | Only the **audiences** you assign to the form. Use this when the form is meant for a known group — for example existing families. |

A **Public** form needs only its share link. A **Private** form requires you to
pick one or more audiences when you create it; only people in those audiences see
and submit it.

## How they combine

When you create a form, you pick one of three modes — each is a category and a
type bundled together:

| Mode | Category | Type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Admission** | Admission | Public |
| **Public** | General | Public |
| **Private** | General | Private |
