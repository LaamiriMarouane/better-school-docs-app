title: Form types and categories
description: Understand the Admission and General categories, and the Public and Private types.
---

Every form has two settings that decide what it does and who can submit it: a
**category** and a **type**. You set both at once when you create the form, and
the **Forms** list shows them in the **Category** and **Form Type** columns.

## Category — what the form is for

| Category | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Admission** | An application form for new students. Each child on an application can be accepted, rejected or waitlisted, and accepting creates the student and their pre-inscription. |
| **General** | A data-collection form — surveys, requests, sign-ups of any kind. You read the responses, but they are not turned into student records. |

### What makes Admission different

- The form starts with a **Guardian Information** section and a **Student
  Information** section already in place. Families can add more than one
  guardian and more than one child in the same application.
- Before you publish it, you choose the **levels** (and, where a level has
  tracks, the tracks) applicants can apply to. You do this from the builder —
  see [Build a form](./build-a-form).
- Each application gets the decision actions — **Accept**, **Reject** and
  **Waitlist** — covered in
  [Review and process submissions](./review-and-process-submissions).

<Callout title="General forms have no student link">
A General form collects answers, nothing more. There is no Accept action and no
student is created. Use Admission whenever the goal is to enroll someone.
</Callout>

## Type — who can submit

| Type | Who can submit |
| --- | --- |
| **Public** | Anyone with the share link. Use it for open admission campaigns and public sign-ups. |
| **Private** | Only the **audiences** you pick when you create the form. They find it under **My forms** in their own space. There is no share link. |

## How they combine

When you create a form, you pick one of three cards — each is a category and a
type bundled together:

| Card | Category | Type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Admission** | Admission | Public |
| **Public** | General | Public |
| **Private** | General | Private |

An Admission form is always Public, so anyone with its link can apply.
