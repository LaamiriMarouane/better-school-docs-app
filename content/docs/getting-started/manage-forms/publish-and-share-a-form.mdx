---
title: Publish and share a form
description: Open a form to applicants, share its link, and close it when done.
---

A form has to be **published** before anyone can submit it. Publishing also opens
the **submissions** list where responses arrive.

## Form statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Still being designed. Not visible to applicants. |
| **Scheduled** | Published, but its opening time hasn't come yet. |
| **Published** | Open and accepting submissions until its closing time. |
| **Closed** | No longer accepting submissions. Responses already received are kept. |

## Publish the form

<Steps>

<Step>
### Check the levels (Admission forms)

An Admission form needs its levels before it can be published. If none are set,
choosing **Publish** opens the levels dialog first — see
[Build a form](./build-a-form).
</Step>

<Step>
### Publish and set the schedule

Choose **Publish** in the builder header. If the form has no schedule yet, the
**Form Settings** dialog opens:

- **Closes at** — required, and must be in the future.
- **Opens at** — optional. Leave it empty (or use **Open now**) to open the form
  as soon as it is published, or pick a later time to schedule it.

Choose **Save & publish**. The form shows **Published**, or **Scheduled** until
its opening time.
</Step>

<Step>
### Share the link (Public forms)

In the **Forms** list, use the **Copy link** button on the form's row and send the
link to applicants. Anyone with it can submit while the form is open.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Private forms have no link">
A **Private** form has no **Copy link**. Members of its audiences find it under
**My forms** in their own space and fill it in there.
</Callout>

You can change the dates at any time from **Settings** in the builder header.

## Close a form

The form stops accepting responses at its **Closes at** time. To stop it earlier,
open the form and choose **Close to submissions**. The form moves to **Closed**,
and every submission already received stays available for review.

To open a closed form again, choose **Publish** and set a new closing time.
