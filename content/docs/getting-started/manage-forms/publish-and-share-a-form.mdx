---
title: Publish and share a form
description: Open a form to applicants, share its link, and close it when done.
---

A form has to be **published** before anyone can submit it. Publishing also opens
the **submissions** list where responses arrive.

## Form statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Still being designed. Not visible to applicants. |
| **Published** | Open and accepting submissions on its link. |
| **Closed** | No longer accepting submissions. Past responses are kept. |
| **Archived** | Retired and hidden from the active list. |

## Publish the form

<Steps>

<Step>
### Set the schedule

Publishing needs a valid **schedule** — an opening date and a closing date, both
in the future. If none is set, the settings dialog opens so you can add one.
</Step>

<Step>
### Publish

Choose **Publish** in the builder header. The status changes to **Published** and
the form goes live for the period you scheduled.
</Step>

<Step>
### Share the link

From the **Forms** list, use **Copy link** on the form's row. Send that link to
applicants — for a **Public** form, anyone with it can submit.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Private forms target audiences">
A **Private** form is only available to the audiences assigned to it. The link
still exists, but people outside those audiences cannot submit.
</Callout>

## Close a form

When a campaign ends, open the form and choose **Close to submissions**. The form
moves to **Closed**: the link no longer accepts new responses, and every
submission already received stays available for review.
