---
title: Add students
description: Create student records, link their guardians, and enroll them right after saving.
---

A **student** record holds a child's personal, medical, and family details. Adding a
student is the first step before they can be enrolled into a class. Every student must
be linked to at least one **guardian**.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators onboarding students and their families.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- Have the student's name and gender ready — these are required.
- Have at least one **guardian** to link. You can pick an existing guardian or staff
  member, or add a new guardian from this form.
- Adding many students at once? Use **Import** on the Students page (see below).

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the Students page

From the admin sidebar, go to **Academic → Students**, then click **Add Student**.

<Screenshot
  label="Add student form"
  caption="The Add Student form: student information, medical details and guardians."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Fill in the student information

Click the avatar to **upload a profile image** if you have one, then complete the
fields below. First name, last name, and gender are required.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **First Name** | Yes | The student's given name. |
| **Last Name** | Yes | The student's family name. |
| **Gender** | Yes | Used across profiles and reports. |
| **Given Name (Native)**, **Family Name (Native)** | No | The name in the local script, e.g. Arabic. |
| **Email** | No | Used for the portal account if one is created later. |
| **Date of Birth**, **Birth Place** | No | Personal details. |
| **Nationality**, **Country**, **City**, **Address** | No | Identity and contact details. |
| **Previous School**, **Previous School Address** | No | The student's prior school. |

If your school has defined **custom fields** for students, they appear below these
fields. Fill them in the same way; some may be required.
</Step>

<Step>
### Add medical details (optional)

In the **Medical History** section you can record **Allergies** and **Medications**.
Type a value and press Enter to add each one as a tag.
</Step>

<Step>
### Link guardians

In the **Guardians Info** section, link at least one guardian:

- **Select guardians & staff** lets you pick guardians already in the system. You can
  also pick a **staff member** — useful when a parent works at the school.
- **Add Guardian** adds a new guardian here: enter their first name, last name,
  email, and type (e.g. father, mother). The phone number, address, and photo are
  optional. You can add up to three new guardians this way.
</Step>

<Step>
### Start a pre-inscription (optional)

Turn on **Enable Pre-Inscription** to register the student against a **Level** before
a full enrollment is created. When enabled, choosing a level is required.
</Step>

<Step>
### Save and enroll

Click **Submit**. You are taken to the new student's profile, and the **Enroll** dialog
opens so you can enroll the student for the year straight away — the class and payment
plan are optional. Close it if you prefer to enroll later. The dialog does not open
when you started a pre-inscription.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="At least one guardian">
A student cannot be saved without a guardian. If you see *"At least one guardian is
required."*, link or add a guardian before submitting.
</Callout>

## Import many students at once

On the Students page, click **Import**. Download the **Template**, fill in one student
per line, then upload the file. Review the preview and any errors it shows, then click
**Start import**. You can keep working while the import runs; a banner on the Students
page shows its progress and lets you view the results, including an error report for
lines that could not be imported.
