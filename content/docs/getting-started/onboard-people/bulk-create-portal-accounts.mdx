---
title: Bulk create portal accounts
description: Create logins for everyone in a role in one background job.
---

When a school is first set up, dozens or hundreds of people need a login at once.
**Bulk create** makes portal accounts for an entire role in a single action — it runs
as a background job so you can keep working while it processes.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators creating logins in bulk during onboarding.
</Callout>

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the Pending portal accounts list

Go to **Users** (under Settings in the sidebar), then the **Pending portal** tab, and pick the **role**
to process — teacher, student, parent, or staff.
</Step>

<Step>
### Start "Create all pending portal accounts"

Choose **Create all pending portal accounts**. A dialog lets you set two options:

| Option | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Only eligible** | When on, people who cannot receive an account yet are skipped instead of failing. Recommended. |
| **Max items** | Optional cap on how many accounts to create in this run. Leave empty for no limit. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Let the job run

The job processes in the background. A progress card shows how many of the total have
been handled, with a status of *Queued*, *Running*, *Completed*, *Failed*, or
*Cancelled*.
</Step>

<Step>
### Review the result

When the job finishes, a banner reports how many accounts **succeeded**, **failed**,
and were **skipped**. If any failed, use **Retry failed** to attempt those again.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Skipped vs failed">
**Skipped** usually means the person already has an account — that is expected and
safe. **Failed** means something went wrong for that person; retry or create their
account individually.
</Callout>
