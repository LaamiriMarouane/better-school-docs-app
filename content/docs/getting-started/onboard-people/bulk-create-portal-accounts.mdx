---
title: Bulk create portal accounts
description: Create logins for everyone in a role in one action.
---

When a school is first set up, dozens or hundreds of people need a login at once.
**Create all** makes portal accounts for everyone still waiting in a role, in a single
action.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators creating logins in bulk during onboarding.
</Callout>

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the Pending portal accounts list

From the admin sidebar, go to **Access → Users**, then open the **Pending portal
accounts** tab. Use the role list at the top to pick **Teacher**, **Student**,
**Parent**, or one of your staff roles. If you type a search, only the matching people
are included.
</Step>

<Step>
### Click "Create all"

Click **Create all**. Accounts are created only for people who can have one; the others
are left out and listed as not eligible.

| Option | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Max accounts** | Optional cap on how many accounts to create this time. People who are not eligible do not count. Leave empty for no limit. |

Click **Create all** in the dialog to start. If the action cannot run, the reason shows
in the dialog.
</Step>

<Step>
### Review the result

When it finishes, a message shows how many accounts were **created**, how many
**failed**, how many were **skipped**, and how many people were **not eligible**. People
who got an account leave the list.
</Step>

<Step>
### Hand over the login details

Login details are emailed to people who have an email address (for a student, also to
guardians who have one). They are not shown on screen. For anyone without an email
address, create their account one at a time from the list or their profile, so you can
copy the temporary password — see [Create portal accounts](./create-portal-accounts).
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Skipped, not eligible, failed">
**Skipped** means the person already has an account — that is expected and safe.
**Not eligible** means the person cannot have an account yet (missing information, no
active enrollment, or no enrolled child). **Failed** means the account could not be
created for another reason. Both stay in the list; fix what is missing and create their
account again.
</Callout>
