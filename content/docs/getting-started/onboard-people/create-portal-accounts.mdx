---
title: Create portal accounts
description: Give a person a login so they can access the platform.
---

A person record — a teacher, student, guardian, or staff member — does not include a
login. A **portal account** is the login that lets them sign in and use the platform.
Teachers can get one while you add them (see [Add teachers](./add-teachers)); for
everyone else, creating the record and creating the account are two separate steps.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators granting people access to the platform.
</Callout>

## Before you start

A person can get a portal account when:

- They **don't have one yet**.
- Their record has an **email address or a code**.
- For a **student**: they are **enrolled** for the year.
- For a **guardian**: at least **one of their children is enrolled**.

If someone isn't eligible yet, the **Create Portal Account** button is greyed out on
their profile.

## Create an account for one person

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the person's profile

Go to the teacher, student, guardian, or staff member's profile.
</Step>

<Step>
### Create the portal account

Click **Create Portal Account**. For a student or guardian, confirm in the **Create a
portal account?** dialog. For a staff member, choose their **Portal role**, then click
**Create account**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Hand over the login details

The **Portal account created** dialog shows the **Username** and a **Temporary
password**, each with a copy button. The temporary password is shown only once, so copy
it or hand it over before you click **Close**. If the person has an email address,
their login details are also emailed to them (for a student, also to guardians who
have an email address).
</Step>

</Steps>

## Create accounts for several people

People who still need a login appear in the **Pending portal accounts** list.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the Pending portal accounts list

From the admin sidebar, go to **Access → Users**, then open the **Pending portal
accounts** tab.

<Screenshot
  label="Pending portal accounts tab"
  caption="Everyone without a login, filterable by role."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Choose a role

Use the role list at the top to show **Teacher**, **Student**, **Parent**, or one of
your staff roles. Each person is marked **Eligible** or **Not eligible**, and you can
search by name.
</Step>

<Step>
### Create accounts

- For one person, click **Create Portal Account** on their row. The login details
  dialog opens, as above.
- For several, tick the people you want, then click **Create accounts (selected)**.
  Only eligible people can be ticked. A progress card shows how many have been
  processed, and you can keep working meanwhile. If some accounts fail, a summary
  appears with **Retry failed accounts**.

Accounts created from a selection are not shown on screen: login details are emailed to
people who have an email address. For anyone without one, create their account one at a
time so you can copy the temporary password.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Doing a whole role at once">
To create accounts for **everyone** in a role in one action, see
[Bulk create portal accounts](./bulk-create-portal-accounts).
</Callout>
