---
title: Add teachers
description: Create teacher records so they can be assigned to subjects, classes, and the timetable.
---

A **teacher** record holds the personal and professional details of a member of your
teaching staff. Once a teacher exists, they can be assigned to subjects and classes and
placed on the timetable. You can give them a login while you add them, or later.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators onboarding teaching staff.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- Have each teacher's basic details ready: full name, phone number, gender, and
  hiring date.
- Have their email address if you want them to receive their login details by email.
- Optionally collect documents to upload — contract, identity document, and résumé.
- Adding many teachers at once? Use **Import** on the Teachers page: download the
  **Template**, fill in one teacher per line, upload it, then click **Start import**.

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the Teachers page

From the admin sidebar, go to **Academic → Teachers**, then click **Add Teacher**.

<Screenshot
  label="Add teacher form"
  caption="The Add Teacher form, grouped into teacher information and documents."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Fill in the teacher information

Complete the fields below. Fields marked required must be filled before you can save.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **First Name** | Yes | The teacher's given name. |
| **Last Name** | Yes | The teacher's family name. |
| **Phone Number** | Yes | Include the country code. |
| **Gender** | Yes | Used across profiles and reports. |
| **Hiring Date** | Yes | The date the teacher joined the school. |
| **Email** | No | Needed to send login details by email. |
| **Given Name (Native)**, **Family Name (Native)** | No | The name in the local script, e.g. Arabic. |
| **Date of Birth**, **Birth Place** | No | Personal details. |
| **Nationality**, **Country**, **City**, **Address** | No | Contact and identity details. |
| **Previous School**, **Previous School Address** | No | Prior employment history. |

If your school has defined **custom fields** for teachers, they appear below these
fields; some may be required.
</Step>

<Step>
### Add a profile photo (optional)

Click the avatar to upload and crop a profile photo.
</Step>

<Step>
### Upload documents (optional)

In the **Documents** section you can attach a **contract**, **identity document**, and
**résumé**. Accepted formats and size limits are shown above the upload fields.
</Step>

<Step>
### Create a login (optional)

In the **Account Creation** section, tick **Create user account and send credentials
via email** to give the teacher a login right away. Their login details are sent to the
email address you entered.
</Step>

<Step>
### Save the teacher

Click **Submit**. You are taken to the new teacher's profile, where you can review and
continue onboarding.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Creating the login later">
If you leave **Account Creation** unticked, the teacher has no login yet. Use **Create
Portal Account** on their profile when you are ready — see
[Create portal accounts](./create-portal-accounts).
</Callout>
