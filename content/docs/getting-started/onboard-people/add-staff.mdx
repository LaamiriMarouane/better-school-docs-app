---
title: Add staff
description: Create a record for a non-teaching staff member, including drivers.
---

A **staff** record covers the people who run the school but do not teach a class —
secretaries, accountants, drivers, and so on. Teachers have their own record type;
see [Add teachers](./add-teachers). Administrators are not added as staff: only the
school owner can add an admin, from **Access → Users** with **Add Admin**.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators registering non-teaching staff.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- The **Staff Management** module must be turned on (**Settings → Modules**). Without
  it, the **HR → Staff** menu is hidden.
- Each staff member gets a **Role**. Roles come from **Access → Roles & permissions**;
  create the ones you need there first.
- Adding many staff at once? Use **Import** on the Staff page: download the
  **Template**, fill in one person per line, upload it, then click **Start import**.

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the Add Staff form

From the admin sidebar, go to **HR → Staff**, then click **Add Staff**.

<Screenshot
  label="Add staff form"
  caption="The staff form: identity, contact, and role details."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Fill in the staff details

Complete the fields below. Only the required ones are needed to save.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **First Name** | Yes | The staff member's first name. |
| **Last Name** | Yes | The staff member's last name. |
| **Phone Number** | Yes | Include the country code. |
| **Gender** | Yes | Male or female. |
| **Hiring Date** | Yes | The date the staff member was hired. |
| **Role** | Yes | Picked from your school's staff roles, e.g. accountant, driver. |
| **Email** | No | Used later if they get a portal account. |
| **Other details** | No | Native name, nationality, country, city, date and place of birth, address, previous school. |

You can also upload a **profile photo** and fill any **custom fields** your school has
defined for staff.
</Step>

<Step>
### Add driver details (drivers only)

If you pick the **Driver** role, a **Driver Information** section appears. The
**License Expiry Date** is then required; the **Driving License Number** is optional.
</Step>

<Step>
### Upload documents (optional)

When creating a staff member you can attach a **contract**, **identity document**, and
**résumé**. Accepted formats and the size limit are shown above the upload fields.
</Step>

<Step>
### Save the staff member

Click **Submit**. You are taken to the new staff member's profile.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Logging in is a separate step">
Creating a staff record does **not** create a login. To let the staff member sign in,
use **Create Portal Account** on their profile — see
[Create portal accounts](./create-portal-accounts).
</Callout>
