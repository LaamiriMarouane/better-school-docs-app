title: Choose how to start

Right after you create your school account, the platform asks **How would you like to start?**
You pick one of three choices. The choice decides what your school contains when you first open
the dashboard.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
The school owner — the person who created the school account. Other administrators are not sent to this
screen.
</Callout>

## The three choices

| Choice | Best for | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Start empty** | Existing schools | A clean school. You build everything yourself. |
| **Load sample data** (**Recommended**) | First-time users | A realistic example school, ready to explore. |
| **Choose a program** | National curriculum | Your levels, subjects, first year and terms, created from an official program. |

Until you pick one, the platform brings you back to this screen each time you sign in as the
owner.

## Start empty

Click **Start empty**. The dashboard opens with an empty school. Then:

1. Fill in the school details — see [Set up the school profile](./set-up-the-school-profile).
2. Create your first academic year and work through **Year setup** — see
   [Academic year setup](../build-the-academic-foundation/academic-year-setup).

## Load sample data

Click **Load sample data**. The screen shows **Setting up your school…** for a moment, then the
dashboard opens with the example school.

### What the sample school contains

| Area | What is created |
| --- | --- |
| **Academic year** | An active year for the current school year, with two terms: **First Semester** and **Second Semester**. |
| **Levels** | One education level, **Primary**, with three levels: **First Year**, **Second Year** and **Third Year**. **Third Year** offers two tracks: **Sciences** and **Letters**. |
| **Subjects** | Mathematics, French, Arabic, English, Physics, Life and Earth Sciences, Social Studies, Islamic Studies, Physical Education and Computer Science, with weekly lessons for each level. |
| **Classes** | Nine classes, three per level (**1A** to **3C**). |
| **Teachers** | Teachers for each subject, assigned to the classes. |
| **Rooms** | Classrooms, labs and sports areas. |
| **Students and guardians** | Twelve students per class, enrolled for the year, each with their guardians. |
| **Finance** | Services (such as Tuition, Transport, Canteen and Insurance), fees for each level, payment plans and reduction rules. |
| **Grading** | Grade categories: **Exam**, **Homework**, **Quiz** and **Project**. |
| **Timetable** | A timetable setup (Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 17:00, lunch break 12:00 to 13:00) and a group, **All Classes**, ready to generate. See [Generate and activate](../../timetable/generate-and-publish). |

### Clear the sample data

While your school runs on sample data, a banner at the top of the app says **You're exploring
with sample data**. When you are ready to start for real:

- Click **Clear sample data** in the banner, or
- Go to **Settings → School**, open the **School Info** tab, and use the **Sample data** box.

**Clear sample data** deletes the sample classes, teachers, students and timetable setup. Your own
data is kept. **Start over** deletes the sample data and takes you back to the three choices. See
[Set up the school profile](./set-up-the-school-profile).

<Callout title="Keep the tab open">
Clearing takes a moment. The screen shows **Clearing sample data…** — keep the tab open until the
app reloads.
</Callout>

## Choose a program

Click **Choose a program**. The **Set up your school** wizard opens. A stepper at the top shows
where you are. Use **Next** to move on and **Back** to return to an earlier step. When **Next** is
greyed out, the reason is shown next to it. Use the language button at the top to change
the language of the wizard.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Program

Pick a national program from the list. Each card shows the country, the program version and its
languages. Use **Search programs…** to find one by name, authority or country.
</Step>

<Step>
### Education levels

Select the education levels your school offers. Each card shows the ages and how many grades it
has. Select at least one.
</Step>

<Step>
### Levels & tracks

For each education level you kept, tick the **Grades** you run. Where the program has **Tracks**,
tick the ones you offer. Select at least one grade for each education level.
</Step>

<Step>
### Subjects

Confirm the subjects taught at each level. Untick the ones you don't teach. This step is
optional: a level with no subjects is still created.
</Step>

<Step>
### Academic year

Name your first year and date it:

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Year label** | Yes | For example 2025-2026. |
| **Start date** | Yes | The calendar starts at today's date. |
| **End date** | Yes | Must be after the start date. |

Then set the **Terms**:

- **Use national term structure** — the program's terms are listed. Enter a start and end date
  for each one.
- **Define manually** — add your own terms. Give each one a name, a start date and an end date.

Every term must fall inside the year's dates.
</Step>

<Step>
### Review

Check the summary: for each education level, the grades, tracks and subjects you kept, and the
year with its dates. Click **Create school**.
</Step>

</Steps>

The screen shows **Setting up your school…** while the program is applied — levels, subjects,
terms and grading. Then the dashboard opens.

The year is created as a draft. Open **Year setup** to add classes, teachers and enrollments,
confirm each step, and activate the year — see
[Academic year setup](../build-the-academic-foundation/academic-year-setup).

## If something goes wrong

If setup cannot finish, the screen shows **Something went wrong** with these options:

- **Retry sample data** or **Retry program** — try the same choice again.
- **Start empty** — continue with an empty school.
- **Start over** — go back to the three choices.
