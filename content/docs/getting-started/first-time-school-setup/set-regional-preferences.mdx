---
title: Set regional preferences
description: Choose the timezone, currency, and date and time formats your school uses.
---

**Regional preferences** make the platform match how your school works locally — the
**timezone** dates and times are shown in, the **currency** money is displayed in, and
the **formats** used for dates and times. The timezone, currency and country were
already filled in for you when the school account was created — check them early so
everything reads correctly from the start.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators — only administrators can change regional settings.
</Callout>

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the Regional settings

Go to **Settings → School** and open the **Regional** tab.

<Screenshot
  label="Regional settings"
  caption="Timezone, currency, and date/time formats, with a live preview."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Set the timezone

Choose the **School timezone** from the list — you can search by country or city. All
dates and times across the platform are shown in this zone. Under the field, a
**Recommended** badge shows which country the suggestion is based on.
</Step>

<Step>
### Set the currency

Pick your currency in **Currency code** — search the list by code or name. Fees,
payments and expenses are displayed in this currency.

<Callout title="The currency locks once money is recorded">
As soon as a payment or an expense has been recorded, the currency can no longer be
changed. The field is then greyed out and shows: "Locked: payments or expenses are
already recorded in this currency." Choose the right currency before you record any
money.
</Callout>
</Step>

<Step>
### Set the date and time formats

Choose the **Date format** and **Time format** to use across the platform. A **Live
preview** shows how dates and times will look as you change them.
</Step>

<Step>
### Save your changes

Click **Save**. The new preferences apply across the platform.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Tip">
Set regional preferences before creating academic years and fees — it keeps every date
and amount consistent from day one.
</Callout>
