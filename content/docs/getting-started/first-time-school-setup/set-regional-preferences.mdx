---
title: Set regional preferences
description: Choose the timezone, currency, and date and time formats your school uses.
---

**Regional preferences** make the platform match how your school works locally — the
**timezone** dates and times are shown in, the **currency** money is displayed in, and
the **formats** used for dates and times. Set them early so everything reads correctly
from the start.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators — only administrators can change regional settings.
</Callout>

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the Regional settings

Go to **Settings → School** and open the **Regional** tab.

<Screenshot
  label="Regional settings"
  caption="Timezone, currency, and date/time formats, with a live preview."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Set the timezone

Choose your school's **timezone**. All dates and times across the platform are shown in
this zone.
</Step>

<Step>
### Set the currency

Enter the **currency code** — the three-letter code for your currency (for example
`USD`, `EUR`, `MAD`). Fees, payments, and expenses are displayed in this currency.
</Step>

<Step>
### Set the date and time formats

Choose the **date format** and **time format** to use across the platform. A **live
preview** shows how dates and times will look as you change them.
</Step>

<Step>
### Save your changes

Click **Save**. The new preferences apply across the platform.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Tip">
Set regional preferences before creating academic years and fees — it keeps every date
and amount consistent from day one.
</Callout>
