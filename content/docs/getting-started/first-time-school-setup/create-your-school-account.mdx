---
title: Create your school account
description: Sign up to create your school and its first administrator account.
---

Creating your school account is the very first step. It registers your **school** and
creates the **first administrator** — you — who can then set everything else up.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
The person setting up the school for the first time.
</Callout>

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the sign-up page

Go to the sign-up page and choose to create a new school account.

<Screenshot
  label="Sign-up form"
  caption="The sign-up form creates the school and its first administrator together."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Fill in your details and the school details

Complete the fields below.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **School Name** | Yes | The full name of your school. |
| **First Name** | Yes | Your first name — the administrator's. |
| **Last Name** | Yes | Your last name. |
| **Email** | Yes | A valid email; this becomes your login. |
| **Password** | Yes | The form tells you if it is too short. |
| **Organization URL code** | Only if asked | The code that forms your school's web address. It is created for you from the school name. The field appears only if that code is already taken — then choose another one; the form shows whether it is available as you type. |
| **I accept the Terms and Conditions** | Yes | Tick the box to accept the terms. |

If a security check is shown, complete it.

You don't enter a country, currency or time zone: they are detected automatically
from where you sign up. You can change them later in the school settings — see
[Set regional preferences](./set-regional-preferences).
</Step>

<Step>
### Create the account

Choose **Create Account & Start Free Trial**. Your school and administrator account
are created, your free trial starts, and you are signed in automatically — setup
continues straight away.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="What comes next">
Next, set up the school's details — see
[Set up the school profile](./set-up-the-school-profile).
</Callout>
