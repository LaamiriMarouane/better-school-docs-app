---
title: Configure school modules
description: Turn on the optional modules your school needs and leave the rest off.
---

**Modules** are optional feature areas. Turning a module on adds its screens and tools;
leaving it off keeps the platform focused on what your school actually uses. You can
change these at any time.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators — only administrators can change modules.
</Callout>

## Available modules

| Module | What it adds |
| --- | --- |
| **Staff management** | Records and tools for non-teaching staff. |
| **Leave management** | Requesting and tracking staff leave. |
| **Behaviour** | Tracking student behaviour and incidents. |
| **Expenses** | Recording what the school spends. |
| **Transport** | Vehicles, routes, and transport tools. Requires *Staff management*. |
| **Equipment** | Tracking the school's equipment and assets. |

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the Modules settings

Go to **Settings → Modules**.

<Screenshot
  label="Modules settings"
  caption="Each module is a card with a toggle."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Turn modules on or off

Use each module's switch to enable or disable it. Some modules depend on others —
**Transport** requires **Staff management**, so its switch stays disabled until Staff
management is on. Turning Staff management off also turns Transport off.
</Step>

<Step>
### Save your changes

Click **Save**. The enabled modules' screens and tools appear across the platform.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Tip">
Start with only the modules you need now. Enabling a module later is just one toggle
away.
</Callout>
