---
title: Pre-inscriptions
description: "Record a prospective student's intended level (and track) for a year, then turn it into an enrollment."
---

A **pre-inscription** records that a student is expected to join a given **level** for an academic
year — and a **track** when that level offers tracks — before a formal enrollment exists. It's an
internal way to line up intake. You manage them under **Admissions & Enrollment → Applications**
(Direct / Admin tab).

<Callout title="Pre-inscription vs. enrollment">
A pre-inscription names a **level** (e.g. *Grade 9*) and optional **track**. An
[enrollment](./create-an-enrollment) places the student for the year and can add a **class** and
**payment plan**. Pre-inscriptions don't collect fees — those belong to enrollment / billing.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Level vs education level">
You pick a **Level** on the form. **Education level** (cycle) is part of how levels are structured
in settings — it is not chosen separately here.
</Callout>

## Statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Recorded and waiting — the student isn't enrolled yet. |
| **Enrolled** | The student has been enrolled for the year; the pre-inscription is fulfilled. |
| **Rejected** | Turned down, with a required reason. |
| **Cancelled** | Called off, with an optional reason. |

## Record a pre-inscription

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Applications

Go to **Admissions & Enrollment → Applications**, open the Direct / Admin tab, then choose
**Add**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Pick the student, level, and track

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Student** | Yes | Search and select. |
| **Level** | Yes | The level they are expected to join. |
| **Track** | When the level offers tracks | Required if that level has offered tracks for the year. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Save

The pre-inscription is created as **Pending**.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="A few rules">
The academic year can't be **closed**. A student who already has an active enrollment for the year
can't be pre-inscribed, you can't record the **same student + level** twice for a year, and a student
can have only **one Pending** pre-inscription per year.
</Callout>

## Work through the list

While a pre-inscription is **Pending**, you can:

- **Edit** it — change the student, level, or track (when applicable).
- **Reject** it — with a reason the record keeps.
- **Cancel** it — with an optional reason.

You can delete ones that are pending or rejected.

## Turn it into an enrollment

- **Bulk enroll from the list** — select several pending pre-inscriptions of the **same level**,
  choose **Enroll**, then set track (if needed), and optionally class and payment plan. See
  [Bulk enroll students](./bulk-enroll-students) and [Create an enrollment](./create-an-enrollment).

When enrollment succeeds for that student and year, the pre-inscription flips to **Enrolled**.

<Callout title="Level and track must match">
When you enroll, the placement **level** must match the pre-inscription. If the pre-inscription
has a **track**, the enrollment track must match too. Once **Enrolled**, the pre-inscription can't
be edited, cancelled, or deleted.
</Callout>
