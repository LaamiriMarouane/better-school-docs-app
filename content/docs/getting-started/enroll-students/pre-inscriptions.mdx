---
title: Pre-inscriptions
description: "Record a prospective student's intended level for a year, then turn it into an enrollment."
---

A **pre-inscription** records that a student is expected to join a given **level** for an academic
year, before a formal enrollment exists. It's an internal way to line up next year's intake and track
who still needs to be enrolled — not a public application form. You manage them from **Students**, on
the **Pre-inscriptions** tab.

<Callout title="Pre-inscription vs. enrollment">
A pre-inscription names a **level** (e.g. *Grade 9*); an [enrollment](./create-an-enrollment) places
the student in a specific **class** (e.g. *9A*) and starts billing. Pre-inscriptions don't collect
fees or documents — those belong to enrollment.
</Callout>

## Statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Recorded and waiting — the student isn't enrolled yet. |
| **Enrolled** | The student has been enrolled for the year; the pre-inscription is fulfilled. |
| **Rejected** | Turned down, with a required reason. |
| **Cancelled** | Called off, with an optional reason. |

## Record a pre-inscription

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the Pre-inscriptions tab

In **Students**, open **Pre-inscriptions**, then choose **Add**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Pick the student and level

Search for the **student** and choose the **level** they're expected to join.
</Step>

<Step>
### Save

The pre-inscription is created as **Pending**.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="A few rules">
The academic year can't be **closed**. A student who already has an active enrollment for the year
can't be pre-inscribed, you can't record the **same student + level** twice for a year, and a student
can have only **one Pending** pre-inscription per year.
</Callout>

## Work through the list

While a pre-inscription is **Pending**, you can:

- **Edit** it — change the student or level.
- **Reject** it — with a reason the record keeps.
- **Cancel** it — with an optional reason.

You can view the reason later on a rejected or cancelled record, and delete ones that are pending or
rejected.

## Turn it into an enrollment

There are two ways a pending pre-inscription becomes **Enrolled**:

- **Enroll the student normally** — when you [create an enrollment](./create-an-enrollment) for that
  student in the same year, their pending pre-inscription flips to **Enrolled** automatically.
- **Bulk enroll from the list** — select several pre-inscriptions of the **same level**, pick a
  **class** and a **payment plan**, and enroll them in one go.

<Callout title="The class must match the level">
When you enroll, the class's **level** has to match the pre-inscription's level — that's how the two
line up. Once a pre-inscription is **Enrolled**, it's final: it can't be edited, cancelled, or
deleted.
</Callout>

## See the numbers

Pre-inscription totals appear on the [dashboard](../../dashboards/reports-and-statistics) — counts by
**status** and **gender**, new ones over time, and a breakdown **per level** — so you can see how next
year's intake is filling up.
