---
title: Suspend or withdraw an enrollment
description: Change an enrollment's status when a student pauses or leaves the school.
---

An enrollment moves through **statuses** during the year. Two of them handle a student
stepping away:

- **Suspend** — a temporary pause. The student is expected to return, and the
  enrollment can be reactivated later.
- **Withdraw** — the student leaves the class for the rest of the year.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators managing students who pause or leave.
</Callout>

## Suspend an enrollment

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the enrollment and choose Suspend

Find the enrollment and open its actions, then choose **Suspend**.

<Screenshot
  label="Suspend enrollment dialog"
  caption="Suspending requires a reason and accepts optional notes."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Give a reason and confirm

Enter a **reason** (required) and optional **notes**, then confirm. The enrollment's
status becomes *Suspended*.
</Step>

</Steps>

To bring the student back, open the suspended enrollment and **reactivate** it.

## Withdraw an enrollment

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the enrollment and choose Withdraw

Find the enrollment, open its actions, and choose **Withdraw**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Give a reason and confirm

Enter a **reason** (required) and optional **notes**, then confirm. The enrollment's
status becomes *Withdrawn*.
</Step>

</Steps>

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Reason** | Yes | Why the student is being suspended or withdrawn. |
| **Notes** | No | Optional extra detail, stored on the enrollment history. |

<Callout type="warn" title="Suspend vs withdraw">
Use **suspend** when the student will return — it can be reactivated. Use **withdraw**
when the student is leaving the class for good. Both keep the enrollment record and
its history.
</Callout>
