---
title: Suspend or withdraw an enrollment
description: Pause an enrollment, withdraw a student who leaves, bring an enrollment back, or delete one.
---

On **Admissions & Enrollment → Enrollments**, each enrollment's actions menu offers **Suspend**,
**Withdraw**, **Reactivate**, and **Delete**, depending on its status. The enrollment's history
keeps every change.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators managing students who pause or leave.
</Callout>

## Suspend an enrollment

Use **Suspend** for a temporary break. The student keeps their placement and fees are not changed.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Suspend

Open an **Active** enrollment's actions and choose **Suspend**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Give a reason

Enter a **Reason** (required) and optional **Notes**, then choose **Suspend**. The status becomes
**Suspended**.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Withdraw an enrollment

Use **Withdraw** when a student leaves for the rest of the year.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Withdraw

Open an **Active** enrollment's actions and choose **Withdraw**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Confirm

Confirm the withdrawal. The status becomes **Withdrawn**. No further fees are billed; amounts
already due stay on the student's account.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Reactivate an enrollment

Open a **Suspended** or **Withdrawn** enrollment's actions, choose **Reactivate**, and confirm. The
status goes back to **Active**. After a withdrawal, billing resumes for the months still to come.

## Delete an enrollment

**Delete** removes the enrollment permanently and can't be undone. Use it to correct an enrollment
made by mistake. An enrollment with recorded payments can't be deleted — withdraw it instead.
