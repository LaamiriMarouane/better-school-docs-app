---
title: Withdraw an enrollment
description: Withdraw a student from their enrollment for the rest of the academic year.
---

**Withdraw** ends an enrollment for the rest of the year when a student leaves. The enrollment
record and its history are kept.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators managing students who leave.
</Callout>

## Withdraw an enrollment

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Withdraw

On **Admissions & Enrollment → Enrollments**, open the enrollment's actions and choose
**Withdraw**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Confirm

Confirm the withdrawal. The enrollment's status becomes **Withdrawn**.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Suspend">
The admin Enrollments UI today offers **Withdraw** (and change class / payment plan). There is no
**Suspend** action on that screen — don't look for a suspend dialog in the current hub.
</Callout>
