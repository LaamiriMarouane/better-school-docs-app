---
title: Change a student's class
description: Move an enrolled student to another class — and change level or track when needed.
---

**Change Class** updates an active enrollment's placement: **level**, **track** (when offered),
and **class**. The enrollment history keeps the change so the move is traceable.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators correcting or changing a student's placement.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- The student must have an **active enrollment**.
- The destination **level** / **class** must exist for the academic year.

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Change Class

On **Admissions & Enrollment → Enrollments**, open the enrollment's actions and choose
**Change Class**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Set the new placement

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Level** | Yes | Can stay the same or move to another level. |
| **Track** | When the level offers tracks | Required if that level has offered tracks. |
| **New Class** | Yes | The class the student moves to. |
| **Reason** | Yes | Why the change is happening (kept in the history). |
</Step>

<Step>
### Confirm

Choose **Transfer to Class**. The student appears on the new class roster; the previous placement stays in
history.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Fees when the level or track changes">
A class change within the same level and track doesn't affect fees. When you move the student to
another level or track, the payment plan stays, but the fees still to come are recalculated at the
new level's prices. Installments that are already due, paid, or partly paid stay as they are. If
the current plan doesn't include the new level, the move is refused with "Payment plan mismatch":
first use **Change Payment Plan** to switch to a plan that also includes the new level, then
change class.
</Callout>
