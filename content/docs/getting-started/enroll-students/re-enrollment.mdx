---
title: Re-enrollment
description: "Bring returning students into the new year: check the level chain, decide each student's outcome, let families confirm, then enroll them in classes."
---

**Re-enrollment** moves your current students into next year. You decide, level by level, whether
each student is promoted, retained, graduates or leaves. You can make the decision final yourself,
or send it to the family to confirm. Students who are coming back then wait to be enrolled in a
class for the new year.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators preparing a new academic year. Parents answer re-enrollment invitations from their
dashboard (web) or home screen (mobile app).
</Callout>

## Before you start

- **Create the new year** and open its setup — see [Academic year setup](../build-the-academic-foundation/academic-year-setup).
  Decisions are made from the new year, about the students of the year before it.
- **Switch on next year's levels** in the setup's **Levels** step. You can only promote a student
  into a level that runs in the new year.

## 1. Check the level chain

Each level has a **Promotes to** level: the level its students move up to next year. It tells the
app where **Promote** sends a student.

When your school was started from a program or from sample data, the chain is already set for
you. Check it, especially for levels you added yourself:

1. Go to **Academic → Levels** (or the **Levels** step of the year setup).
2. Edit the level to open **Edit Level**.
3. Set **Promotes to** to the next level, then save.

A level with no **Promotes to** is a final level: its students show as **Graduating (no next
level)**, and you can't promote them. If such a level is not really the last one, the
**Admissions / intake** step warns you, with a **Go to the Levels step** button.

## 2. Decide each student's outcome

Open the new year's setup and go to the **Admissions / intake** step. The top shows **From** (the
previous year) and **To** (the year you are setting up).

1. **Select level** to load its students. Narrow the list by class or search by name or code.
2. The **Current → Next** column shows where each student would go.
3. Choose an outcome on the student's row:

| Outcome | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Promote** | The student moves up to the next level next year. |
| **Retain** | The student repeats the same level next year. |
| **Graduate** | Only for a final level: the student finishes school. |
| **Leaving** | The student won't come back next year. |

**Graduate** and **Leaving** apply at once. The student stays in their class until the current year
ends.

For **Promote** and **Retain**, a window asks **How is this finalized?**

- **Decide now (final)** — the student is re-enrolled straight away.
- **Send to parent to confirm** — the family receives an invitation to confirm or decline.

If the level offers tracks, choose the **Track** too. Then click **Confirm**.

### Decide for many students at once

Tick the students (or the box in the header to select them all). In the bar that appears, choose
how decisions are finalized, then click **Promote**, **Retain**, **Graduate** (final levels only)
or **Leaving**. If some students can't be updated, a result window lists them.

### Change a decision

A decided row shows its outcome, and for re-enrolled students their status (**Invited**,
**Confirmed** or **Declined**, or **Pending enrollment**). Click **Undo** to clear the decision and
choose again. You can undo until the student has been enrolled in the new year.

<Callout title="Grades not finished yet?">
If the previous year's terms are not all finalized, the step shows a reminder that decisions made
now may not reflect final grades. It doesn't stop you.
</Callout>

## 3. Open re-enrollment for families

Families can answer invitations only while re-enrollment is open. At the top of the
**Admissions / intake** step, the status shows **Open** or **Closed**.

- Click **Open re-enrollment** when you're ready for families to answer.
- Click **Close re-enrollment** to stop answers.

You can send invitations while re-enrollment is closed; families can answer once you open it.

## 4. Families confirm

When you send a decision to the family, the parents get a notification. While re-enrollment is
open, they see a card reading *Re-enrollment of (child) for (year)* — on the dashboard of the parent portal and
on the home screen of the mobile app — showing the year and, when set, the next level.

The parent taps **Confirm** or **Decline**, then confirms. The answer is final. You get a
notification for each answer.

- **Confirmed** — the student is re-enrolled and waits to be enrolled in a class.
- **Declined** — the student is not re-enrolled. To record that they're leaving, click **Undo** on
  their row and choose **Leaving**.

## 5. Follow the answers

Go to **Admissions & Enrollment → Applications** and open the **Re-enrollment Confirmations** tab.
It shows the invitations for the year selected at the top of the page, with counts of **Invited**,
**Confirmed** and **Declined**.

Filter by level or status, or search for a student. Each row shows the **Student**, **Level**,
**Decision** and **Status**.

## 6. Enroll returning students in classes

Students you promoted or retained with **Decide now (final)**, and students whose family
confirmed, are re-enrolled for the new year but not yet in a class. The **Admissions / intake**
step shows how many are waiting, with **Enroll them**.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the Enrollments step

In the year setup, open the **Enrollments** step (or click **Enroll them**). Returning students are
listed under **Waiting to be enrolled**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Pick a class

For each student, **Pick a class** from the classes of their level. If you don't know yet, choose
**No class yet (assign later)**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Enroll

Click **Enroll**. The student is enrolled for the new year and leaves the list.
</Step>

</Steps>
