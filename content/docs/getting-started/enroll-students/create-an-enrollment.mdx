---
title: Create an enrollment
description: Place a student on a level for the academic year — optionally assign a track, class, and payment plan.
---

An **enrollment** places a student in an academic year at a **level** (and **track** when the
level offers one). A **class** and **payment plan** are optional at create time — you can assign
them later from the Enrollments hub.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators placing students for the year.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Level, track, and education level">
Enrollment uses **Level** (e.g. Grade 9), and **Track** only when that level offers tracks
(filières). **Education level** (the broader cycle) is set on the level itself in academic
structure — it is **not** a field on the enrollment form.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- The **student** record must exist — see [Add students](../onboard-people/add-students).
- The **level** (and any tracks) must be set up for the year.
- Optional: a **class** and a **payment plan** for that level.

## Enroll a student

You can enroll a student straight from the student screens. When you save a new student, the
profile opens with the **Enroll** form ready, unless you saved them as a pre-inscription.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Enroll

Go to **Academic → Students** and open the student's actions menu, or open the student's profile.
Choose **Enroll**. It is offered only while the student has no active enrollment for the year.
</Step>

<Step>
### Fill in the placement

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Level** | Yes | The level the student joins. |
| **Track** | When the level offers tracks | Required if that level has offered tracks for the year. |
| **Class** | No | Assign now or later from Enrollments. |
| **Payment Plan** | No | Assign now or later; fees are billed once a plan is attached. |
| **Enrollment Date** | No | Defaults to today. Billing starts from this date. |
| **Notes** | No | Optional. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Confirm

Choose **Enroll students**. The student now appears on **Admissions & Enrollment → Enrollments**.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Pending pre-inscription">
If the student has a pending pre-inscription, enroll them at that pre-inscription's level. You can
also enroll from **Admissions & Enrollment → Applications** — see
[Pre-inscriptions](./pre-inscriptions) and [Bulk enroll students](./bulk-enroll-students).
</Callout>

## Finish on the Enrollments hub

Go to **Admissions & Enrollment → Enrollments** to review the list. Use **Assign class** or
**Assign plan** in bulk when students were enrolled without a class or plan.

<Callout title="One active placement per year">
A student has one active enrollment placement for the year. To change class or level later, use
[Change class](./transfer-a-student-between-classes) rather than creating a second enrollment.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Enrollment date and fees">
Months before the enrollment date are not billed: a student who joins on 3 October is not billed
for September. When the joining month is billed, its first installment is due on the enrollment
date rather than on a due day that has already passed. Later months follow the usual due day. See
[Installments and schedules](../../finance/fees-and-payments/installments-and-schedules).
</Callout>
