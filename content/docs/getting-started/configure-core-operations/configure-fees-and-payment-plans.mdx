---
title: Configure fees and payment plans
description: The order to set up your school's money side — currency, billing rules, services, fees, payment plans and discounts — before you enroll students.
---

Before families can be billed, the school sets up what it charges and how it is paid. Do it in the
order below, **before you enroll students**: a student's installments are created from the payment
plan attached at enrollment. Each step links to its full guide under
[Fees & Payments](../../finance/fees-and-payments).

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators and finance staff preparing the school year.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- Your levels exist. See [Create levels](../build-the-academic-foundation/create-levels).
- Choose the academic year at the top of the screen. Services, fees and plans are set up for the
  selected year.

## Setup order

<Steps>

<Step>
### Check the currency

Go to **Settings → School** and open the **Regional** tab. Pick your **Currency code**. It can no
longer be changed once a payment or an expense has been recorded. See
[Set regional preferences](../first-time-school-setup/set-regional-preferences).
</Step>

<Step>
### Set the billing rules

Go to **Settings → Finance** and open the **Billing** tab. Choose the day installments fall due,
whether you charge in advance or in arrears, and what a student who joins mid-month pays. Set this
before creating plans, so every schedule follows the same rules. See
[Billing configuration](../../settings/billing-configuration).
</Step>

<Step>
### Create services

Go to **Finance → Services** and add what the school provides — tuition, transport, canteen,
uniforms. See [Create services](../../finance/fees-and-payments/create-services).
</Step>

<Step>
### Price them with fees

Go to **Finance → Fees**. Each fee gives a service an amount — one amount for chosen levels, or an
amount per level — and how often it is charged. See
[Set up fees](../../finance/fees-and-payments/set-up-fees).
</Step>

<Step>
### Bundle fees into payment plans

Go to **Finance → Payment Plans**. A plan groups the fees for one or more levels; each fee is
required or optional. See [Create payment plans](../../finance/fees-and-payments/create-payment-plans).
</Step>

<Step>
### Add reduction rules (optional)

Go to **Finance → Reduction Rules** to create reusable discounts — for example a sibling
discount or a staff-child reduction — and assign them to students. See
[Reduction rules](../../finance/fees-and-payments/reduction-rules).
</Step>

</Steps>

## What happens next

- **At enrollment**, choose a **Payment Plan** for the student (or assign it later). The student's
  installments are created from it. See [Create an enrollment](../enroll-students/create-an-enrollment)
  and [Installments and schedules](../../finance/fees-and-payments/installments-and-schedules).
- **To collect money**, open the **Payment center** from **Finance → Payments**. See
  [Record a payment](../../finance/fees-and-payments/record-a-payment).

<Callout title="Check the first enrollment">
After enrolling the first student with a plan, open their installments. If the due dates or amounts
look wrong, fix the billing rules or the plan before enrolling everyone else.
</Callout>
