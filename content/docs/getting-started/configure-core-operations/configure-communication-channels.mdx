---
title: Configure communication channels
description: Turn on the communication tools your school uses, create your audiences, and know which channels work on their own.
---

Your school reaches families and staff through **announcements**, **events**, **messages** and
**notifications**, plus **reclamations** if you use them. Most of the setup is turning on the right
modules and creating your **audiences**. The full guides are under
[Communication](../../communication).

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators preparing the school year.
</Callout>

## The channels

| Channel | What it's for | What you set up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Announcements** | One-way news to chosen audiences. | Turn on the **Announcements** module and create audiences. |
| **Events** | Dates on the school calendar — holidays, exams, activities, closures. | Create audiences. Events need no module. |
| **Messages** | Two-way conversations: class channels and direct messages. | Turn on the **Chat** module. Class channels create themselves. |
| **Notifications** | Alerts about what concerns each person. | Nothing — they work on their own. |
| **Reclamations** | Complaints from parents (and students, if you allow it) about grades, fees and other matters. | Turn on the **Reclamations** module, and choose whether students can send them. |

## Setup order

<Steps>

<Step>
### Turn on the modules you need

Go to **Settings → Modules** and switch on **Announcements**, **Chat** and **Reclamations** as
needed, then click **Save**. Their pages appear under **Communication** in the menu. See
[Configure school modules](../first-time-school-setup/configure-school-modules).
</Step>

<Step>
### Create your audiences

Go to **Communication → Audiences** and create the groups you will write to most — for example
*All parents*, *All teachers*, or *Year 5 students and guardians*. Announcements and events are
sent to audiences, so create them first. See [Audiences](../../communication/audiences).
</Step>

<Step>
### Put the first dates on the calendar

Go to **Communication → Events** and add the year's holidays and closures, and key dates like
exams. A **Holiday** or **Closure** tells the timetable and transport the school is closed that day.
See [Events](../../communication/events).
</Step>

<Step>
### Decide who can send reclamations (optional)

If you turned on **Reclamations**, go to **Settings → School** and open the **Reclamations** tab.
Switch on **Students can create reclamations** if students may send them themselves, then click
**Save**. Parents can always send reclamations while the module is on.
</Step>

<Step>
### Send a first announcement

Go to **Communication → Announcements** and publish a welcome message to a small audience to check
who receives it. See [Announcements](../../communication/announcements).
</Step>

</Steps>

## Channels that work on their own

- **Class channels** in **Messages** are created automatically when a teacher is assigned to a
  class. Who can message whom is fixed — see [Messages](../../communication/messaging).
- **Notifications** appear in the bell and on the **Notifications** page for everyone they concern.
  See [Notifications](../../communication/notifications).
- **Mobile push**: people get alerts on their phone once they sign in to the mobile app and allow
  notifications.
- **Email**: administrators, teachers, students and parents who have an email address on their
  profile also receive most of their notifications by email.
