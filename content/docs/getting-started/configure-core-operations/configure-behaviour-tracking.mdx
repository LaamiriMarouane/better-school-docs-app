---
title: Configure behaviour tracking
description: Turn on the Behaviour module and set up the categories and levels teachers will record incidents against.
---

Behaviour tracking lets teachers and administrators record positive and negative behaviour, which
turns into points and a leaderboard. Set it up before teachers start recording: they can only pick
from the categories and levels you create. The full guides are under [Behaviour](../../behaviour).

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators preparing the school year.
</Callout>

## Setup order

<Steps>

<Step>
### Turn on the Behaviour module

Go to **Settings → Modules**, switch on **Behaviour**, then click **Save**. The **Behaviour** menu
appears for administrators, and teachers get **Student Incidents** and **Leaderboard**. See
[Configure school modules](../first-time-school-setup/configure-school-modules).
</Step>

<Step>
### Create categories

Go to **Behaviour → Config**. In the **Categories** card, add the kinds of behaviour your school
records — for example *Respect* and *Helpfulness* (positive), *Disruption* and *Lateness*
(negative). Add sub-categories where you need finer labels. See
[Set up categories & levels](../../behaviour/setup).
</Step>

<Step>
### Create levels and their points

On the same page, in the **Levels** card, add the levels of significance — for example *Minor* and
*Major* for negatives, *Bronze*, *Silver* and *Gold* for positives. Each level has **default
points**: positive levels add points, negative levels remove them.
</Step>

<Step>
### Tell your teachers

Teachers record incidents for the classes they teach from **Student Incidents**. See
[Record incidents](../../behaviour/record-incidents).
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Agree on the scale first">
Agree with your teachers on the categories and the points for each level before the year starts,
so the same behaviour is worth the same points in every class. You can archive an item later
without losing past incidents.
</Callout>

## What families see

Students and parents see only their own incidents and class ranking. The school-wide leaderboard is
for staff. See [Points & leaderboard](../../behaviour/leaderboard).
