Once your academic foundation is in place, set up the day-to-day operations: how classes are
scheduled, what families pay, how attendance is taken, how behaviour is recorded, and how the
school communicates. Each article gives the order to follow and links to the full guides.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators preparing the school year.
</Callout>
