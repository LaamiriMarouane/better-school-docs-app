The platform follows a natural order: you **set the school up**, **prepare the year**,
**bring in people**, and then **run it day to day**.

## Setup workflows

The first-time setup happens once. The year setup comes back every school year.

<Steps>

<Step>
### First-time setup

Create the school account, then choose how to start: **Start empty**, **Load sample
data** to explore with a ready-made example school, or **Choose a program** to start from
an official curriculum. Then fill in the school profile, choose modules, and check the
regional preferences. See [First-Time School Setup](../first-time-school-setup).
</Step>

<Step>
### Prepare the year

Each academic year has its own **Year setup**: terms, levels, subjects, classes,
teachers, fees and payment plans, transport, and enrollments, one step at a time. You
choose **Confirm for this year** on each step, then activate the year. See
[Build the Academic Foundation](../build-the-academic-foundation) and
[Academic year setup](../build-the-academic-foundation/academic-year-setup).
</Step>

<Step>
### Onboard people and enroll students

Add students, teachers, guardians, and staff, give them portal accounts, then enroll
students for the year. See [Onboard People](../onboard-people) and
[Enroll Students](../enroll-students).
</Step>

</Steps>

## Daily operations

Once the school is set up, the recurring work begins — teaching, attendance,
behaviour, finance, and communication. The timetable schedules lessons, attendance is
recorded against classes, fees and payments are followed for each student, and
announcements keep families informed.

## How it connects

Each layer builds on the last: classes need levels; payment plans are built from your
fees; the timetable needs subjects, rooms, and teachers. An enrollment only needs the
student and a level for the year — the class and the payment plan are optional and can be added
later.
