title: Understand the main school workflows
description: How the platform's main workflows connect, from setup to daily operation.
---

The platform follows a natural order: you **set the school up**, **build its
structure**, **bring in people**, and then **run it day to day**. Each step depends on
the ones before it.

## Setup workflows

These happen once, at the start.

<Steps>

<Step>
### First-time setup

Create the school account, fill in the school profile, choose modules, and set
regional preferences. See [First-Time School Setup](../first-time-school-setup).
</Step>

<Step>
### Build the academic foundation

Create the academic year and terms, then levels, classes, subjects, and rooms. See
[Build the Academic Foundation](../build-the-academic-foundation).
</Step>

<Step>
### Onboard people and enroll students

Add students, teachers, guardians, and staff, give them portal accounts, then enroll
students into classes. See [Onboard People](../onboard-people) and
[Enroll Students](../enroll-students).
</Step>

</Steps>

## Daily operations

Once the school is set up, the recurring work begins — teaching, attendance,
behaviour, finance, and communication. The timetable schedules lessons, attendance is
recorded against classes, fees are collected against enrollments, and announcements
keep families informed.

## How it connects

Each layer builds on the last: classes need levels; enrollments need classes and
students; fees need payment plans; the timetable needs subjects, rooms, and teachers.
Following the order above keeps every later step unblocked.
