The platform runs the day-to-day work of a school in one place. Here is what each main
area covers.

## Core areas

| Area | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Academic** | Academic years and terms, levels, classes, subjects, rooms, enrollments, and timetables. |
| **People** | Students, teachers, guardians, and staff — their records and profiles. |
| **Access** | Portal accounts and logins, with a role for each kind of user. |
| **Finance** | Services, fees, payment plans, payments, and expenses. |
| **Attendance** | Recording and reviewing student attendance. |
| **Behaviour** | Tracking behaviour and incidents (optional module). |
| **Communication** | Announcements, notifications, and messaging between the school and families. |
| **Transport** | Vehicles, routes, and drivers (optional module). |

## Optional modules

Some areas are **modules** you turn on only if your school needs them — staff
management, leave, behaviour, expenses, transport, and equipment. See
[Configure school modules](../first-time-school-setup/configure-school-modules).

## Web and mobile

The platform is used on the **web app** and a **mobile app**. See
[Supported platforms: web and mobile](./supported-platforms-web-and-mobile).
