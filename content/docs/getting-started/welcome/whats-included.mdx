The platform runs the day-to-day work of a school in one place. Here is what each main
area covers.

## Core areas

| Area | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Academic** | Students, teachers, guardians, levels, classes, subjects, rooms, timetables, and the activity journal. |
| **Admissions & Enrollment** | Applications, enrollments, and related intake work. |
| **Assessments** | Grading setup, scheduled exams, gradebook, assignments, quizzes, and the question bank. |
| **Attendance** | Student, teacher, and staff attendance. |
| **HR** | Staff records, employment, and leave. |
| **Finance** | Services, fees, payment plans, payments, reduction rules, and expenses. |
| **Behaviour** | Tracking behaviour and incidents (optional module). |
| **Communication** | Announcements, audiences, notifications, messaging, and events. |
| **Operations** | Transport (vehicles, routes, trips) and equipment (optional modules). |
| **Settings** | Academic, school, finance, and module configuration, plus users and billing. |

## Optional modules

Some areas are **modules** you turn on only if your school needs them — staff
management, leave, behaviour, expenses, transport, equipment, announcements, and chat. See
[Configure school modules](../first-time-school-setup/configure-school-modules).

## Web and mobile

The platform is used on the **web app** and a **mobile app**. See
