The platform runs the day-to-day work of a school in one place. Here is what each main
area of the administrator sidebar covers.

## Core areas

| Area | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Academic** | Students, teachers, guardians, forms, levels, subjects, course templates, classes, teacher assignments, rooms, the timetable, and the activity journal. |
| **Admissions & Enrollment** | Applications and enrollments. |
| **ASSESSMENTS** | Grading setup, scheduled exams, gradebook, assignments, assignment grades, quizzes, and the question bank. |
| **ATTENDANCE** | Student, teacher, and staff attendance. |
| **Behaviour** | Behaviour setup, student incidents, and the leaderboard (optional module). |
| **HR** | Staff records, leave, and the **Staff month** — hours taught, absences, leave, and pay status for the month. |
| **FINANCE** | Services, fees, payments, payment plans, reduction rules, and expenses. |
| **OPERATIONS** | Transport (vehicles, routes, trips, and transport zones), dismissal at the school gate, and equipment (optional modules). |
| **Reports** | Academic reports and financial reports. |
| **Communication** | Announcements, reclamations from parents and students, audiences, notifications, messages, and events. |
| **Access** | Users (including portal accounts) and roles & permissions. |
| **Settings** | Academic, school, finance, and module settings, campuses, and the audit log. |
| **Billing & Subscription** | Your platform subscription. |

**Support** and **Requests** are in your profile menu: contact the support team, or suggest a
new feature.

## Optional modules

Some areas are **modules** you turn on only if your school needs them — **Staff Management**,
**Leave Management**, **Behaviour**, **Expenses**, **Transport**, **Equipment**,
**Announcements**, **Chat**, **Reclamations**, and **Dismissal**. See
[Configure school modules](../first-time-school-setup/configure-school-modules).

## Web and mobile

The platform is used on the **web app** and a **mobile app**. See
[Supported platforms: web and mobile](./supported-platforms-web-and-mobile).
