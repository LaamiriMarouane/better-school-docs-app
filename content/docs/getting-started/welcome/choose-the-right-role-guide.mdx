What you can see and do depends on your **role**. Use the guide that matches you — and
if you hold more than one role, you can switch between them at any time.

## Roles

| Role | This is you if you… |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Set up and run the school — academics, people, finance, and operations. Only the school owner can add other admins. |
| **Teacher** | Teach classes — your timetable, attendance, lessons, assignments, and grades. |
| **Guardian** | Follow your child's school day — timetable, attendance, results, behaviour, and payments. See the [Parent guide](../../role-guides/parent-guide). |
| **Student** | Follow your own courses, assignments, exams, grades, and timetable. See the [Student guide](../../role-guides/student-guide). |
| **Staff** | Work at the school in another job, such as a bus driver — your trips, your leave, and your attendance, depending on what your role allows. |

## Guides for parents and students

Parents and students have their own step-by-step guides in [Role guides](../../role-guides):

- [Parent guide](../../role-guides/parent-guide), [Your child's schoolwork](../../role-guides/parent-schoolwork),
  and [Payments, bus, pickup, and family tasks](../../role-guides/parent-payments-and-family).
- [Student guide](../../role-guides/student-guide) and [Your courses and work](../../role-guides/student-coursework).

## Getting started either way

Whatever your role, the **Getting Started** section explains the platform basics —
signing in, navigating, and the global search. The setup chapters are written mainly
for administrators; the other sections of this documentation cover each area of the
school in detail.

## More than one role?

Some people hold several roles — for example a teacher who is also a guardian. In the web app, you can
move between them without signing out — see
[Switch between available roles](../access-and-authentication/switch-between-available-roles).
