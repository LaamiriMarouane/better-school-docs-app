What you can see and do depends on your **role**. Use the guide that matches you — and
if you hold more than one role, you can switch between them at any time.

## Role guides

| Role | Use this guide if you… |
| --- | --- |
| **Administrator** | Set up and run the school — academics, people, finance, and operations. |
| **Teacher** | Teach classes — your timetable, attendance, assignments, and grades. |
| **Parent & Student** | Follow a student's day — timetable, attendance, results, and payments. |

Role-specific deep dives (Administrator, Teacher, Parent & Student) are planned as
separate sections. For now, use the table above and the **Getting Started** chapters
that match what you need to do in the product.

## Getting started either way

Whatever your role, the **Getting Started** section explains the platform basics —
signing in, navigating, and the global search. The setup chapters are written mainly
for administrators.

## More than one role?

Some people hold several roles — for example a teacher who is also a parent. You can
move between them without signing out — see
[Switch between available roles](../access-and-authentication/switch-between-available-roles).
