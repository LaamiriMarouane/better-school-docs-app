The **global search** is the fastest way to move around the platform. It opens from
anywhere and does two things at once: it **navigates** you to pages, and it **finds
records** such as students, teachers, and classes.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Everyone — global search works for every role.
</Callout>

## Open the search

- Press **Ctrl K** (**⌘ K** on Mac) from anywhere, or
- Click the **search bar** in the top navigation.

## What you can find

The results are split into two kinds:

| Result type | What it does | Who sees it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pages** | Jumps straight to a screen in the app — e.g. *Students*, *Timetable*, *Fees*. | Everyone |
| **Resources** | Finds actual records by name — students, teachers, classes, and more — grouped by type. | Administrators |

Resource results show an icon or photo, the record's name, and a short subtitle so you
can tell similar records apart. When a type has more matches than shown, the group
notes how many more there are.

## Use the search

<Steps>

<Step>
### Type what you are looking for

Start typing — at least **two characters** for resource results. Matching pages appear
immediately; resources appear a moment later as they are looked up.
</Step>

<Step>
### Move through the results

Use the **↑** and **↓** arrow keys to move between results.
</Step>

<Step>
### Open a result

Press **Enter** (or click) to go to the selected page or record. Press **Esc** to
close the search without leaving the page.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Tip">
Resource results follow the **selected academic year**. If you cannot find a record,
check that the right year is selected in the year switcher.
</Callout>
