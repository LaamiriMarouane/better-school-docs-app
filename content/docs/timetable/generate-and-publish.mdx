When your setup is complete, you're ready to build the schedule. You create a **timetable
group**, run the **generator**, review and fine-tune the result, then **activate** the group so
everyone follows the live week and **class sessions** appear with real dates.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators and academic planners producing and releasing the school schedule.
</Callout>

## Before you start

Make sure the earlier steps are done:

- [Prerequisites](./prerequisites) — subjects, classes, terms, teachers, rooms, and teacher
  assignments all exist.
- [School schedule](../settings/school-schedule) — the school's default week is set.
- [Timetable config](./timetable-config) — the group's weekly grid is set.
- [Teaching load](./teaching-load) — each subject's weekly lessons are set.
- [Scheduling setup](./scheduling-setup) — availability and limits are in.
- [Constraints](./constraints) — the Solver policy is adjusted (or left at its defaults).

## Step 1 — Create a timetable group

A **[timetable group](./timetable-groups)** is a weekly template for a **term** and a chosen set of
**levels**, using one config.

Go to **Academic → Timetable → Groups**, click **Add Timetable Group**, fill in the **Name**,
**Term**, **Levels and tracks**, and **Timetable Configuration**, then click **Create Timetable
Group**. To try a variant of an existing group, use **Clone as new draft** from its row menu
instead.

## Step 2 — Generate

You can generate a **Draft** group that is not locked.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Choose how long to search

Open the group. Next to **Generate**, click the arrow to pick the **Search time**: **Quick**,
**Standard**, **Thorough**, or **Maximum**. A longer search usually places more lessons and gives
a better week.
</Step>

<Step>
### Run the generator

Click **Generate**. The app first checks your setup for problems that would make a timetable
impossible — most often a class–subject with **no teacher assigned**, or more lessons than the
available periods and rooms can hold. If it finds any, a window lists them as
**Validation Errors** (fix these, then generate again) and **Validation Warnings** (worth a look).
</Step>

<Step>
### Watch the progress

The header shows how many lessons are placed and how much time is left. You can leave the page and
come back.
</Step>

<Step>
### Stop early (optional)

Click **Stop generation**, then choose:

- **Stop & keep current result** — keep the best week found so far and carry on editing it.
- **Stop & discard** — throw the partial result away and keep what you had before.

If the current result is worse than the timetable already saved, the window warns you and the keep
button reads **Stop & keep anyway**.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Step 3 — See what couldn't be placed

The generator places every lesson it can. When it finishes, a message tells you either that all
lessons were scheduled, or how many were scheduled and how many are still unscheduled.

If some lessons are unscheduled, click the **unscheduled** badge in the group header. The panel
**Why are these lessons unscheduled?** lists each lesson with the reason and a suggestion. Use
**Open class** to jump to that class's timetable.

| Reason | What usually helps |
| --- | --- |
| **Teacher has no hours left** | Raise the teacher's hour limits, or give some classes to another teacher. |
| **Class timetable is full** | Reduce the class's weekly load, or add periods to the timetable config. |
| **Teacher and class are never free together** | Relax availability or daily limits so their free times overlap. |
| **Blocked by a subject rule** | Allow the subject at more times. |
| **No room available** | Add a room of the right type, or free one up. |
| **Not found in the time allowed** | Generate again with a longer **Search time**. |
| **Lesson too long for the day** | Split it into shorter lessons, or change the breaks. |

Fix what the panel points to and generate again, or place the remaining lessons by hand.

## Step 4 — Review the result

Look over the timetable in the view that suits you:

- **By Classes** — what each class's week looks like.
- **By Teachers** — each teacher's personal schedule.
- **By Rooms** — how each room is used through the week.

Switch between **Grid** (one timetable at a time) and **Master grid** (everything side by side).
Use **Search** (subject, teacher, or room) to highlight where something appears. **Insights** gives
a summary such as teacher workload, and **Print Timetable** prints the week.

## Step 5 — Adjust by hand

You can edit a Draft or Archived group that is not locked:

- **Move a lesson** — drag it to another cell, or drag a lesson from the class's lesson list onto
  the grid. It lands where you let go.
- **On a touch screen** — tap a lesson, then tap the cell where it should go.
- **Conflicts are allowed** — if the move clashes with a teacher, a room, or someone's
  availability, the lesson is still placed. A **Placed with a conflict** message explains the
  clash, the lesson is highlighted, and the header counts the conflicts (click it to jump to them).
  A move is refused only when the lesson physically can't sit there — over a break, past the end of
  the day, or on a slot it can't share.
- **Change a room** — open the lesson and choose **Change room**.
- **Pin a lesson** — use **Pin assignment** to keep a lesson exactly where it is when you generate
  again.
- **Undo / Redo** — step back and forward through your changes.

Changes save automatically; click **Save** to save at once. The Solver policy rules only guide
**Generate** — they never block a move you make by hand.

## Step 6 — Activate

When you're happy, go back to the **Groups** list, open the group's row menu (**⋯**), and choose
**Activate**. A window shows when sessions will start and which group (if any) it replaces.
Confirm with **Activate**. Full detail: [Activate and class sessions](./activate-and-sessions).

## Changing an active timetable

An active group is read-only. To change the live week, either:

- **Deactivate** it from the Groups list. It becomes **Archived**: edit it by hand, then
  **Activate** it again. You can't run the generator on an archived group.
- Or use **Clone as new draft**, generate and refine the clone, then **Activate** it. It replaces
  the old group, which becomes **Archived**.

## Tips and best practices

- **Generate early, even if setup isn't perfect.** The setup check and the unscheduled panel are the
  fastest way to find missing assignments and tight limits.
- **Pin the parts you're sure about** before generating again, so they stay where they are.
- **Review by teacher** before activating — it's the quickest way to spot an unbalanced workload.
- Keep trial variants as **Draft** groups until you are ready to go live.
