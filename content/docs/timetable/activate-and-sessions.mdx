title: Activate and class sessions
description: Turn a weekly timetable template into real dated class meetings — what Activate does, and how sessions appear for teachers and attendance.
---

A [timetable group](./timetable-groups) is a **weekly template**. **Activating** it makes that
template the live plan for its levels and creates **class sessions** — real meetings with a
specific date and time that teachers and families can follow.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators releasing a finished weekly plan so day-to-day teaching can start.
</Callout>

## Template vs session

| Concept | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Weekly template** (timetable group) | The repeating week: *Monday period 1 — Class 6A Maths with Ms. A in Room 12*. |
| **Class session** | One real occurrence: *Monday 10 March, 08:00–08:45 — Class 6A Maths…*. |

Attendance, the Activity Journal, and substitutes are attached to **sessions**, not to the blank
template. See [Class sessions & attendance](../teaching/sessions-and-attendance).

## Before you activate

- The group's week is reviewed (generated and/or edited by hand).
- You are ready for teachers and students to follow this plan for the group's levels.

## Activate a group

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the group

Go to **Academic → Timetable → Groups** and open the group (or use the row actions on the list).
</Step>

<Step>
### Choose Activate

Confirm the preview if the product shows one. If another **active** group already covers the same
levels, activating this one **retires** the overlapping group so only one live plan applies per
level set.
</Step>

<Step>
### What happens

- The group becomes **Active**.
- **Class sessions** are created from the weekly template with real dates and times for the
  coming days.
- Teachers, students, and parents can follow the live schedule.
- The active group is protected from casual grid edits until you unlock or deactivate it.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Deactivate

**Deactivate** stops the group from driving new live sessions and unlocks editing again. Use it
when you need to change the week, then **Activate** again so sessions follow the updated template.

## After activation — day to day

| Who | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| **Teachers** | See their sessions on the timetable, mark attendance, and log what was taught in the Activity Journal. |
| **Administrators** | Review sessions across classes, cancel or adjust a one-off meeting when needed. |
| **Students / parents** | Follow the live class timetable. |

## Tips

- Activate only when the week is good enough to live with — drafts are for trials and variants.
- Prefer a new **draft clone** for a big experiment; activate the winner when you are sure.
- For how teachers run each meeting, see [Class sessions & attendance](../teaching/sessions-and-attendance).
