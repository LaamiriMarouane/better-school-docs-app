title: Activate and class sessions
description: Turn a weekly timetable template into real dated class meetings — what Activate and Deactivate do, and how sessions appear for teachers and attendance.
---

A [timetable group](./timetable-groups) is a **weekly template**. **Activating** it makes that
template the live plan for its levels and creates **class sessions** — real meetings with a
specific date and time that teachers and families can follow.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators releasing a finished weekly plan so day-to-day teaching can start.
</Callout>

## Template vs session

| Concept | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Weekly template** (timetable group) | The repeating week: *Monday period 1 — Class 6A Maths with Ms. A in Room 12*. |
| **Class session** | One real occurrence: *Monday 10 March, 08:00–08:45 — Class 6A Maths…*. |

Attendance and the Activity Journal are attached to **sessions**, not to the blank template. See
[Class sessions & attendance](../teaching/sessions-and-attendance).

## Before you activate

- The group's week is reviewed (generated and/or edited by hand).
- You are ready for teachers and students to follow this plan for the group's levels.

## Activate a group

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the row menu

Go to **Academic → Timetable → Groups** and open the row menu (**⋯**) of the group. Choose
**Activate**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Read the confirmation

The **Activate Timetable Group** window always appears before anything changes:

- **First timetable for these levels** — sessions start from this Monday.
- **Another group is already active for some of these levels** — the window lists the group(s)
  that will be archived. Sessions follow the new timetable from next Monday.
- If the term hasn't started yet, sessions start on the term's first day. If the term has already
  ended, no sessions are created.

Click **Activate** to confirm.
</Step>

<Step>
### What happens

- The group becomes **Active** and is read-only while it stays active.
- **Class sessions** are created from the weekly template with real dates and times.
- Teachers, students, and parents can follow the live schedule.
- Any group it replaced becomes **Archived**.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Deactivate

Deactivate a group when you need to change the live week or stop using it.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Choose Deactivate

On the **Groups** list, open the active group's row menu (**⋯**) and choose **Deactivate**, then
confirm.
</Step>

<Step>
### What happens

- Sessions from tomorrow onwards are removed. Past sessions and today's stay.
- The group becomes **Archived**. You can edit it by hand again.
</Step>

<Step>
### Go live again

When your changes are ready, choose **Activate** on the same group so new sessions follow the
updated week. You can't run the generator on an archived group — to regenerate, use **Clone as new
draft**, generate the clone, and activate it.
</Step>

</Steps>

## After activation — day to day

| Who | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| **Teachers** | See their sessions on the timetable, mark attendance, and log what was taught in the Activity Journal. |
| **Administrators** | Review sessions across classes and adjust or cancel a single session when needed. |
| **Students / parents** | Follow the live class timetable. |

## Tips

- Activate only when the week is good enough to live with — drafts are for trials and variants.
- Prefer a **Clone as new draft** for a big experiment; activate the winner when you are sure.
- For how teachers run each meeting, see [Class sessions & attendance](../teaching/sessions-and-attendance).
