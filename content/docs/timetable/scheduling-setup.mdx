**Scheduling setup** is where you record the facts about your school that the timetable must
respect: when each **teacher** is unavailable, how much they can teach, when each **room** can't be
used, which room is each class's homeroom, and when a class itself is unavailable.

<Callout title="Reality vs. rules">
This page is about **reality** — facts that must hold. The *preferences* (avoid gaps, keep a class
in one room, and so on) live in **Solver policy**. See [Constraints](./constraints).
</Callout>

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators preparing teacher and room information before generating a timetable.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Availability follows the school week">
Availability grids use the days and periods of your timetable configs and the
[School schedule](../settings/school-schedule). Set those first so the grids match how your school
actually opens.
</Callout>

Open **Academic → Timetable → Config**. These facts live on the **Teachers**, **Room
Availability**, **Homerooms**, and **Class Hours** tabs (alongside **Time Grid** and
[Teaching load](./teaching-load)).

<Callout title="Edit many at once">
On the Teachers, Room Availability, and Class Hours tabs, click **Enable bulk select**, tick
several teachers, rooms, or classes, and apply the same availability or limits to all of them.
</Callout>

## Teachers

Pick a teacher in the list on the left. The **Teachers** tab has four sub-tabs.

### Availability

By default, a teacher can be scheduled at **any** time the school is open. Here you mark only the
times a teacher **can't** teach — the exceptions.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the teacher

On **Teachers** → **Availability**, choose a teacher (or use bulk select for several).
</Step>

<Step>
### Mark the unavailable times

Tap or drag on the grid to mark the periods when the teacher is **not** available — for example
every afternoon, or all of Friday. Leave the grid empty and the teacher is available whenever the
school is open. Click **Save changes**.

</Step>

</Steps>

### Limits

On **Limits**, set how much the teacher should teach. Leave a field empty to apply no limit.

| Limit | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Max hours per day** | The most teaching hours a teacher can have in one day. |
| **Max hours per week** | The most teaching hours across the whole week. |
| **Min periods per day** | If the teacher works on a day, they get *at least* this many periods — so they aren't called in for a single period. |
| **Min periods per half-day** | The same idea per morning or afternoon. Needs the half-day divider in [Timetable config](./timetable-config). |
| **Max gap periods** | How many free periods between lessons are fine for this teacher on the same day. |

<Callout title="Firm limits vs. preferences">
**Max hours per day** and **Max hours per week** are always kept. The minimums and **Max gap
periods** are preferences: they override the matching Solver policy rule for this teacher, and the
rule's switch and importance still apply. See [Constraints](./constraints).
</Callout>

### Subject whitelist

Pick the subjects this teacher is eligible to teach. This is the same eligibility you manage in
[Teacher Assignments](./teacher-assignments) → **Subject Eligibility**.

### Room whitelist

If a teacher should only use certain rooms, pick them here. The generator then places that
teacher's lessons only in those rooms. Use **Allow all** to remove the restriction.

## Room Availability

Switch to the **Room Availability** tab. It works like teacher availability: a room can be used at
**any** time by default, and you mark only the times it **can't** be used — for example a room
booked for exams or under maintenance. Leave the grid empty and the room is available all week.

### Capacity and type

Two room facts come from the room itself (set when you create the room) and are always respected:

- **Capacity** — a lesson is only placed in a room big enough for the class.
- **Room type** — if a subject needs a particular kind of room (e.g. a lab), the generator only
  uses rooms of that type for it.

See [Create rooms](../getting-started/build-the-academic-foundation/create-rooms).

## Homerooms

On the **Homerooms** tab, set each level's room policy:

- **Homeroom** — each class has its own room. Pick a room per class, or use **Auto-assign
  distinct rooms** or **Assign shared room** to fill several at once. You can also pin a subject
  to a fixed room for one class under **Subject rooms**.
- **Roamed** — no homeroom; the generator chooses a room for every lesson.

Subjects that need a special room (lab, PE, computer room) always move to a suitable room.

## Class Hours

On the **Class Hours** tab, mark when a **class** itself is unavailable — for example a cohort
that only comes in the morning. Use the quick choices **Full day**, **Morning only**,
**Afternoon only**, or **Alternate**, or mark periods on the grid. Click **Save**.

## Tips and best practices

- Enter unavailability **before** generating — over-blocking a teacher is a common reason lessons
  stay unscheduled (too little time left for their lessons).
- Use **bulk select** to apply the same availability or limits quickly.
- Keep limits realistic. Very tight limits combined with a lot of blocked time leave lessons
  unscheduled; the unscheduled panel tells you which limit is the cause.
