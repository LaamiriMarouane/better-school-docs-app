---
title: Teacher Assignments
description: Set which subjects each teacher can teach, then assign teachers to each class and subject for the term.
---

**Teacher Assignments** is where you say who teaches what. First you set **subject eligibility**
(which teachers may teach each subject at a level). Then you assign a teacher to each
**class–subject** for the term. The timetable generator schedules those assignments.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators preparing teaching assignments before building the timetable.
</Callout>

## Open Teacher Assignments

Go to **Academic → Teacher Assignments**. Work in the academic year selected in the year
switcher.

The page has two tabs:

| Tab | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Subject Eligibility** | For one level, mark which teachers are eligible for each subject. |
| **Class Assignments** | For one term, assign a teacher to each class–subject. |

## Subject Eligibility

Use this tab to build a whitelist per **level**: teachers down the side, subjects across the top.
Toggle a cell to mark a teacher eligible for that subject.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Choose the level

Pick the level at the top of the tab.
</Step>

<Step>
### Toggle eligibility

Click cells to include or exclude teachers for each subject. Optionally show or hide full subject
names.
</Step>

<Step>
### Save changes

Click **Save changes**. Edits stay local until you save — switching level or leaving the page
warns you if anything is unsaved.
</Step>

</Steps>

Eligibility shapes the teacher picker on Class Assignments (eligible teachers are listed first).
Teachers who are not marked eligible can still be chosen under **Other teachers** — eligibility
does not hard-block an assignment.

## Class Assignments

Switch to **Class Assignments**. Pick the **term** (defaults to the active term). Coverage shows
how many class–subject cells are filled and how many are still unassigned.

Assignments belong to the **selected term**. A teacher on Maths for Class 6A in Term 1 is not
copied automatically into Term 2 — you assign Term 2 separately for every subject that runs in
that term, or use **Copy from another term**.

### Copy from another term

Use this when another term already has the teachers you want. Works in either direction (Term 2 →
Term 1 or Term 1 → Term 2) within the same academic year.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the copy dialog

On **Class Assignments**, select the **target** term (the grid you are filling). Click **Copy from
another term**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Choose the source term and conflict mode

Pick the **source** term. Then choose how to treat cells that already have a teacher:

- **Keep existing** (default) — fill only empty cells; leave assigned teachers alone.
- **Override** — replace matching cells with the source term’s teacher.
</Step>

<Step>
### Review the preview and apply

The preview shows how many cells will be filled, overridden, kept, or skipped (subject not offered
in the target term). Click **Apply copy**. Coverage and the teachers load rail refresh for the
target term.
</Step>

</Steps>

Copy only applies where the **same class** still needs the **same subject** in the target term.
It does not clear target cells that have no counterpart in the source, and it does not copy across
academic years.

### Views

| View | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| **Grid** | Seeing every class and subject in a matrix, grouped by grade. |
| **By class** | Working through one class's subjects. |
| **By subject** | Seeing who teaches a subject across all classes. |

### Assign a teacher

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open a cell

Click an empty cell (**Unassigned**) or an existing assignment.
</Step>

<Step>
### Pick the teacher

Search and choose a teacher. Use **Unassigned** to clear the cell.
</Step>

</Steps>

The assignment saves immediately — there is no separate Save on this tab. There are no extra
fields (hours, dates, or notes) on the assignment itself.

### Teachers load rail

Open the **Teachers** panel on the right to see each teacher's load for the term (assigned
periods vs capacity). Bars turn amber when load is high and red when over capacity. This rail is
for review only — you don't edit capacity here.

## Tips and best practices

- Set **Subject Eligibility** first so the Class Assignments picker is easier to use.
- Clear every **Unassigned** cell before generating a timetable — missing assignments are a common
  blocker. See [Generate and activate](./generate-and-publish).
- Use the load rail to spot overloaded teachers before you generate.
