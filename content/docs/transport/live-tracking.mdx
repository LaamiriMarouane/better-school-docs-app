---
title: Live tracking
description: See how parents follow their child's school bus while it runs, and how administrators monitor the trips on the road.
---

While a trip is running, the school and the parents of its riders can follow the bus on a map.
The bus's position comes from the driver's or bus monitor's phone, so live tracking starts when
the crew taps **Start trip** and ends when the trip finishes. Nothing else needs to be set up
beyond routes, stops, and trips.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Parents following their child's bus, and administrators monitoring the fleet.
</Callout>

## What parents see

Parents open **My child's bus** on the web, or **School bus** in the mobile app. Each child with a
bus on the road gets a card.

Each card shows:

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Map** | The school, the family's own stop (**Your stop**), and the bus's current position. Other students' stops are not shown. |
| **Child's status** | For example **Waiting for the bus**, **On the bus — heading to school**, **On the bus — heading home**, **Dropped off**, **Did not board**, or **Absent today**. |
| **Last seen** | On the web, if the bus's position hasn't updated for a while, the card says when it was last seen (**Last seen N min ago**). |

In the mobile app, the card also says whether the bus is far, approaching, at your stop, or has
passed.

When no bus is on the road, the page shows **No bus running right now**. Parents also get a
notification when their child boards, is dropped off, or did not board.

<Callout title="Multiple children">
If a parent has more than one child using the bus, all their running trips appear on the same
screen. They don't need to switch between children.
</Callout>

## What administrators see

In **Operations → Trips**, the **Live** tab lists every trip on the road now. Select a trip to see
the map with the bus's position, the stops along the way and which ones are done, and how many
students are aboard or already delivered.

## How a trip goes live

<Steps>

<Step>
### The crew starts the trip

The driver or bus monitor taps **Start trip** on their **Trips** page. Their phone's location
must be allowed — the trip can't start without it. The trip moves to **Live**.

Trips never start by themselves: if the crew doesn't start a trip, it stays under **Scheduled**.
</Step>

<Step>
### The crew marks each stop

At each stop the crew marks students (**Board**, **Not board**, or **Drop off**), then taps
**Mark stop & next**. Parents see their child's status change as it happens. See
[Trip attendance](./trip-attendance).
</Step>

<Step>
### The trip ends

The trip finishes on its own once every student is marked and every stop is done, or when the
crew taps **Finish trip**. Tracking stops and the trip moves to **Completed**.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Frequently asked questions

### Do parents need to do anything to see live tracking?

No. Parents open **My child's bus** (web) or **School bus** (mobile app), and a card appears
for any trip that is running for their child.

### Where does the bus position come from?

From the phone of the crew member who started the trip. If the phone's location is turned off
during the trip, the crew sees a reminder to turn it back on.

### Does live tracking start automatically?

No. Tracking starts only when the driver or bus monitor taps **Start trip**.

### What happens if a student is marked Absent?

The parent sees **Absent today** on their child's card. Only administrators can mark a student
Absent, usually when the family has told the school in advance.

### What does "Did not board" mean?

The crew marked the child as not boarding at their stop, or the trip finished before the child
was marked. Parents are notified.

### Can parents see the whole route?

No. Parents see the school, their own stop, and the bus. Other students' stops are never shown.

### Is there a separate driver app?

No. Drivers and bus monitors use the same app — **Trips** on the web, or **Today's trips** in the
mobile app — to start trips and mark students.

### What if the trip is cancelled?

Parents are told the bus isn't running today, and the trip no longer appears as running.
