The **Transport** module helps you run your school bus service from start to finish.
You register your **vehicles** and **drivers**, draw **routes** made up of **stops**,
assign **students** to the stop they board at, and then follow each day's **trips** and
**attendance**.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators and transport coordinators who set up and run the school's bus service.
</Callout>

## How the pieces fit together

Transport is built in layers. Each layer depends on the one before it, so it helps to set
things up in this order:

1. **Vehicles** — the buses, minibuses, and cars in your fleet.
2. **Drivers** — the staff who drive, plus any assistants or monitors who ride along.
3. **Routes and stops** — the path a bus follows and the places it picks up or drops off.
4. **Pricing zones** *(optional)* — group stops into zones so families in the same area pay
   the same monthly transport fee.
5. **Student roster** — which student boards at which stop, on which route.
6. **Trips** — the actual run of a route on a given day. Most trips are created
   **automatically** for every school day; you can also add one **manually**.
7. **Attendance** — who boarded, who was dropped off, and who was absent on each trip.

<Callout title="Everything belongs to an academic year">
Routes, zones, rosters, and trips are all scoped to the selected academic year. Pick the
right year in the switcher before you set anything up.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- An **academic year** must be created and selected.
- The **Transport module** must be enabled for your school.
- *(Optional, for billing)* If you charge families for the bus, create a **Transport**
  service and its fee in **Finance** first. See
  [Create services](../finance/fees-and-payments/create-services) and
  [Set up fees](../finance/fees-and-payments/set-up-fees).

## Good to know

- **Trips run themselves.** Once a route has stops and students, a trip is created for it
  automatically on every school day — you don't add them one by one. Holidays and school
  closures are skipped.
- **History is kept.** Each trip keeps its own snapshot of the stops and riders as they were
  that day, so past records stay accurate even if you later change a route.
- **Billing is automatic.** When you assign a student to a stop, their transport fee is added
  to their payment schedule based on the stop's pricing zone (or the level's default fee).
