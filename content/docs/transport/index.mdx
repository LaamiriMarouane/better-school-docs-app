Transport helps you run your school bus service from start to finish. You register your
**vehicles** and **drivers**, draw **routes** made up of **stops**, put each **student** on the
stop they use, and then follow each day's **trips**, **attendance**, and the bus's live position.

In the admin sidebar, transport lives under **Operations**: **Vehicles**, **Routes**, **Trips**,
and **Transport zones**.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators and transport coordinators who set up and run the school's bus service. Drivers
and bus monitors work from their own **Trips** page, and parents follow the bus from
**My child's bus**.
</Callout>

## How the pieces fit together

Set things up in this order, because each step uses the one before it:

1. **Vehicles** — the buses, minibuses, and cars in your fleet.
2. **Drivers and bus monitors** — the staff who drive, and the staff who ride along.
3. **Routes and stops** — the path a bus follows and the places it picks up or drops off.
4. **Transport zones** *(optional)* — group stops into zones so families in the same area pay
   the same monthly transport fee.
5. **Riders** — which student uses which stop, on which route.
6. **Trips** — one run of a route on a given day. Trips are created **automatically** for
   every school day; you can also add one **by hand**.
7. **Attendance** — who boarded, who was dropped off, and who did not board on each trip.

## Before you start

- An **academic year** must be created and selected.
- The **Transport** module must be turned on for your school.
- Drop a **map pin** for the school in **Settings → School → School Info** and save it
  **before** you build routes. That pin is the school stop on every route. The written
  address alone is not enough — without the pin, the road line does not run to or from
  the school, and suggested times and optimized order leave the school out. See
  [Build routes and stops](./build-routes-and-stops).
- *(Optional, for billing)* If you charge families for the bus, create a **Transport**
  service and its fee in **Finance** first, and add it to the payment plans of the families
  who use the bus. See [Create services](../finance/fees-and-payments/create-services) and
  [Set up fees](../finance/fees-and-payments/set-up-fees).

<Callout title="Starting a new year">
The **Set up transport** step of the year setup copies last year's zones (with this year's
prices) and routes, shows where riders live, and suggests stops for returning riders. You don't
have to rebuild everything by hand.
</Callout>

## Good to know

- **Trips appear on their own.** Once a route is active and has stops, a trip is created for it
  on every school day — you don't add them one by one. Holidays and school closures are skipped.
- **History is kept.** A past trip still shows the stops and riders as they were that day, even
  if you change the route later.
- **Billing follows the stop.** Transport is billed only to students who have the transport fee
  on their payment plan. When you put such a student on a stop, their upcoming transport
  installments are re-priced to the stop's zone fee (or the level's default fee). Students
  without the transport fee can still ride; they are marked **Not billable**.
- **Parents are kept informed.** Parents get a notification when their child boards, is dropped
  off, or did not board.
- **Dismissal is separate.** The end-of-day pickup queue at the school gate has its own pages:
  **Operations → Dismissal** and **Dismissal history** for admins, and **Pickup** for parents.
