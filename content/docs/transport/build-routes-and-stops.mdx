description: Set up school bus routes — create pickup and drop-off routes, place stops on the map with planned times, and optionally group stops into transport zones for area-based fees.

A **route** is the path a bus follows, made up of an ordered list of **stops**. A **stop** is a
place where the bus picks up or drops off students. Optionally, you can group stops into
**transport zones** so families in the same area pay the same transport fee.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators and transport coordinators planning bus routes.
</Callout>

## How routes work

- A route has one **direction**: **Pickup (home → school)**, the morning run, or
  **Drop-off (school → home)**, the afternoon run.
- Most schools create a **pickup** route and a matching **drop-off** route for each area.
- The **school is added as a stop automatically** — at the *end* of a pickup route (the bus
  finishes at school) and at the *start* of a drop-off route (the bus starts at school).
- Stops are kept in order by their **planned time**, so the route always reads in sequence.

## Before you start

- Drop a **map pin** for the school in **Settings → School → School Info** and save it
  **before** you create routes. The pin is copied onto the school stop (the end of a pickup
  route, the start of a drop-off route). Typing the address is not enough. Without the pin,
  the road line does not run to or from the school, and **Suggest times** and **Optimize
  order** leave the school out. If a route was created before the pin existed, open that
  route's school stop and save it so the location is copied onto the route.
- Your **vehicles**, **drivers**, and **bus monitors** should already exist if you want to set a
  default crew for the route. See [Manage vehicles](./manage-vehicles) and
  [Manage drivers](./manage-drivers).

## Step 1 — Create a route

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Routes

Go to **Operations → Routes**, then click **New route**.

<Screenshot
  label="Routes list with the Add route button"
  caption="Routes for the selected academic year are listed here."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Fill in the route details

Complete the fields below, then click **Create**.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Route name** | Yes | A clear name, e.g. *North Line — Morning*. |
| **Direction** | Yes | **Pickup (home → school)** or **Drop-off (school → home)**. |
| **Code** | No | A short code, e.g. *RT-A*. |
| **Description** | No | Optional notes about the route. |
| **Color** | No | A colour used to show the route on the map and on badges. |
| **Default vehicle** | No | The bus normally used. It pre-fills every trip created for this route. |
| **Default driver** | No | The driver normally assigned. It pre-fills every trip. |
| **Default assistant** | No | The bus monitor normally assigned. It pre-fills every trip. |
| **Active** | — | Leave on for routes in service. Inactive routes get no daily trips. |
</Step>

</Steps>

## Step 2 — Add stops

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the route and add a stop

Select the route in the list to open it, then click **Add stop**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Place the stop and fill in its details

Click the map to drop a pin at the stop's location (drag the pin to refine it), then complete
the fields.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Stop name** | Yes | The stop's name, e.g. *Main Square*. |
| **Address** | Yes | The street address of the stop. |
| **Location on map** | Yes | Click the map to set the pin. |
| **Planned time** | No | When the bus is due at this stop. It also sets the stop's order in the route. |
| **Zone** | Yes | The transport zone of this stop (see Step 3), or **No zone — use level default fee**. |

<Screenshot
  label="Add stop dialog with the map picker"
  caption="Drop a pin on the map and give the stop a name and planned time."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Repeat for every stop

Add each stop the bus visits. The school stop is added for you; use **Set time** on it to give
it a planned time.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Stop order follows the planned time">
You don't drag stops to reorder them. Set each stop's **planned time** and the route lists them
in time order automatically. Stops without a planned time are placed at the end.
</Callout>

### Work with a route

Once a route has stops, its page gives you a few more tools:

- **Suggest times** — proposes a planned time for every stop along the road. Review the
  proposed times, then click **Apply**.
- **Optimize order** — proposes a better stop order with matching times. Review it, then click
  **Apply**.
- **Route line** — choose which road line the map shows: **Recommended**, **Fastest**, or
  **Shortest**.
- **Stop menu (⋯)** — **Manage students** at this stop, **Edit stop**, or **Delete stop**.
- **Assigned crew** — shows the route's default driver, assistant, and vehicle, with its seat
  count.
- **Export PDF** — downloads the route's boarding list: each stop and the students who use it.

## Step 3 (optional) — Set up transport zones

A **zone** is an area with its own monthly transport fee. Group your stops into zones so that
everyone boarding in the same area is charged the same amount.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Create a zone

Go to **Operations → Transport zones**, then click **New zone** and complete the fields.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Zone name** | Yes | The zone's name, e.g. *Inner city*. |
| **Code** | No | A short code, e.g. *Z1*. |
| **Color** | No | A colour for the zone's stop markers on the map. |
| **Fee (per month)** | Yes | The monthly transport fee for students whose stop is in this zone. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Put stops in the zone

You choose each stop's zone yourself — it is not detected from the map. Either pick the
**Zone** when you add or edit a stop, or use the **Stops by zone** table on the Transport zones
page, where you change the zone of one stop at a time.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="How transport pricing works">
- A student whose stop is **in a zone** is billed that **zone's fee**.
- A student at a stop with **no zone** is billed the **level's default transport fee** (set up
  in [Finance](../finance/fees-and-payments/set-up-fees)).
- A zone's fee is a **single monthly amount** — every student whose stop is in that zone pays it,
  whatever their level.
</Callout>

## Tips and best practices

- Create a **pickup** and a **drop-off** route per area, and give the pair the same colour so
  they're easy to recognise.
- Fill in **planned times** even if they're approximate, or use **Suggest times** — they drive
  the stop order and help drivers stay on schedule.
- Set a **default vehicle and crew** on the route so daily trips need no extra setup.

## Common use cases

- **New neighbourhood served** — add a route, drop its stops on the map, and assign students.
- **Same-area pricing** — create a zone and put the area's stops in it so families pay the same
  fee.
- **Seasonal change** — turn a route inactive instead of deleting it to keep its history.
- **Paper list for the bus** — use **Export PDF** to print the boarding list.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I set up a school bus route for the first time?

Go to **Operations → Routes → New route**, choose its direction, then add stops one by one by
dropping a pin on the map for each location. Set a planned time for each stop, or use
**Suggest times**. Then put students on their stops. Trips are created automatically on every
school day.

### Do I need separate routes for morning pickup and afternoon drop-off?

Yes. A route has a single direction — **Pickup (home → school)** or **Drop-off (school → home)**.
Create one pickup route and one matching drop-off route per area. Giving them the same colour
makes the pair easy to recognise on the map.

### How many stops can I add to a route?

There is no fixed limit. Add as many stops as your route needs. The school stop is always added
automatically — at the end of a pickup route and at the start of a drop-off route.

### Why doesn't the road line include the school?

The school stop uses the **map pin** saved in **Settings → School → School Info**, not the
written address. Set that pin and save it before you create the route. If the route already
exists, open the school stop and save it so the pin is copied onto the route. Until then,
suggested times and optimized order also leave the school out.

### How do I reorder stops?

Set each stop's **planned time** — the route lists stops in time order. Or click
**Optimize order**, review the proposed order, and click **Apply**.

### What is a transport zone and do I need one?

A zone is optional. It groups stops in an area so that every student boarding at a stop in that
zone is charged the same monthly transport fee. If all students pay the same fee regardless of
distance, you don't need zones — use the level's default transport fee instead.

### Do I have to rebuild routes every academic year?

No. The **Set up transport** step of the new year's setup copies last year's routes and zones,
and suggests stops for returning riders. Past trips stay available in the previous year.

### How do parents know which stop to use?

While their child's bus is running, parents see their stop (**Your stop**) on the map in
**My child's bus** on the web, or **School bus** in the mobile app.
