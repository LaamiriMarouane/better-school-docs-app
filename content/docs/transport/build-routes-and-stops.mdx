A **route** is the path a bus follows, made up of an ordered list of **stops**. A **stop** is a
place where the bus picks up or drops off students. Optionally, you can group stops into
**pricing zones** so families in the same area pay the same transport fee.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators and transport coordinators planning bus routes.
</Callout>

## How routes work

- A route has one **direction**: **Pickup** (home → school, in the morning) or **Dropoff**
  (school → home, at the end of the day).
- Most schools create a **pickup** route and a matching **dropoff** route for each area.
- The **school is added as a stop automatically** — at the *end* of a pickup route (the bus
  finishes at school) and at the *start* of a dropoff route (the bus starts at school).
- Stops are kept in order by their **planned time**, so the route always reads in sequence.

## Before you start

- Your **vehicles** and **drivers** should already exist if you want to set a default crew for
  the route. See [Manage vehicles](./manage-vehicles) and [Manage drivers](./manage-drivers).

## Step 1 — Create a route

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open Routes

Go to **Transport → Routes**, then click **Add route**.

<Screenshot
  label="Routes list with the Add route button"
  caption="Routes for the selected academic year are listed here."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Fill in the route details

Complete the fields below, then save.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Name** | Yes | A clear name, e.g. *North Line — Morning*. |
| **Direction** | Yes | **Pickup** (home → school) or **Dropoff** (school → home). |
| **Code** | No | A short code shown on rosters, e.g. *RT-A*. |
| **Description** | No | Optional notes about the route. |
| **Color** | No | A colour used to show the route on the map and on badges. |
| **Default vehicle** | No | The bus normally used. It pre-fills every trip created for this route. |
| **Default driver** | No | The driver normally assigned. It pre-fills every trip. |
| **Default assistant** | No | The assistant normally assigned. It pre-fills every trip. |
| **Active** | — | Leave on for routes in service. Turn off to retire a route. |
</Step>

</Steps>

## Step 2 — Add stops

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the route and add a stop

Open the route you just created and click **Add stop**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Place the stop and fill in its details

Click the map to drop a pin at the stop's location, then complete the fields.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Name** | Yes | The stop's name, e.g. *Main Square*. |
| **Address** | Yes | The street address of the stop. |
| **Location on map** | Yes | Click the map to set the pin. The location is used to show the stop and to detect its pricing zone. |
| **Planned time** | No | When the bus is due at this stop. It also sets the stop's order in the route. |
| **Zone** | No | The pricing zone this stop belongs to (see Step 3). |

<Screenshot
  label="Add stop dialog with the map picker"
  caption="Drop a pin on the map and give the stop a name and planned time."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Repeat for every stop

Add each stop in the order the bus visits it. The school stop is added for you automatically.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Stop order follows the planned time">
You don't drag stops to reorder them. Set each stop's **planned time** and the route lists them
in time order automatically. Stops without a planned time are placed at the end.
</Callout>

## Step 3 (optional) — Set up pricing zones

A **zone** is an area with its own transport fee. Group your stops into zones so that everyone
boarding in the same area is charged the same amount.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Create a zone

Go to **Transport → Zones**, then click **Add zone** and complete the fields.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Name** | Yes | The zone's name, e.g. *Inner city*. |
| **Code** | No | A short code, unique within the year, e.g. *Z1*. |
| **Color** | No | A colour for the zone's stop markers on the map and roster badges. |
| **Fee amount** | Yes | The monthly transport fee charged to students whose stop is in this zone. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Assign stops to the zone

Open a stop and choose its **Zone**, or use the **Stops by zone** view to assign several at
once. When you place a pin on the map, the matching zone can also be detected automatically.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="How transport pricing works">
- A student whose stop is **in a zone** is billed that **zone's fee**.
- A student at a stop with **no zone** is billed the **level's default transport fee** (set up
  in [Finance](../finance/fees-and-payments/set-up-fees)).
- A zone's fee is a **single monthly amount** — every student whose stop is in that zone pays it,
  whatever their level.
</Callout>

## Tips and best practices

- Create a **pickup** and a **dropoff** route per area, and give the pair the same colour so
  they're easy to recognise.
- Fill in **planned times** even if they're approximate — they drive the stop order and help
  drivers stay on schedule.
- Set a **default vehicle and crew** on the route so daily trips need no extra setup.

## Common use cases

- **New neighbourhood served** — add a route, drop its stops on the map, and assign students.
- **Same-area pricing** — create a zone and put the area's stops in it so families pay the same
  fee.
- **Seasonal change** — turn a route inactive instead of deleting it to keep its history.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I set up a school bus route for the first time?

Create a route (Transport → Routes → Add route), choose its direction (Pickup or Dropoff), then
add stops one by one by dropping a pin on the map for each location. Set a planned time for each
stop — this sets the stop order and gives drivers a schedule to follow. Once stops are in place,
assign students to their stops and create trips to run the route.

### Do I need separate routes for morning pickup and afternoon dropoff?

Yes. A route has a single direction — Pickup (home → school) or Dropoff (school → home). Create
one Pickup route and one matching Dropoff route per area. Giving them the same colour makes the
pair easy to recognise on the map.

### How many stops can I add to a route?

There is no fixed limit. Add as many stops as your route needs. The school stop is always added
automatically — at the end of a Pickup route and at the start of a Dropoff route.

### How do I reorder stops?

Set each stop's **planned time**. The route lists stops in time order automatically. You don't
drag stops to reorder them — update the planned times instead.

### What is a pricing zone and do I need one?

A pricing zone is optional. It groups stops in an area so that every student boarding at a stop
in that zone is charged the same monthly transport fee. If all students pay the same fee
regardless of distance, you don't need zones — use the level's default transport fee instead.

### Can the same route be used across multiple academic years?

Routes are scoped to an academic year. When you set up a new year, carry over routes by
duplicating or recreating them. Trip history from previous years is preserved under the old year.

### How do parents know which stop to use?

When a student is assigned to a stop on a route, parents can see the stop name and location in
the School Bus section of the app. The stop also appears on the live tracking view during a trip.
