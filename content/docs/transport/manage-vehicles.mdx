title: Manage vehicles
description: Add the buses, minibuses, and cars in your fleet, and keep track of their running costs.
---

A **vehicle** is any bus, minibus, or car your school uses for transport. You register each
vehicle once, and it then becomes available to assign to routes and trips. The fleet is also
where you keep a running record of each vehicle's costs — fuel, maintenance, insurance, and
inspections.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators and transport coordinators managing the school fleet.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- The **Transport** module must be turned on.
- Have each vehicle's details handy: registration (plate) number, brand and model, fuel type,
  seat count, and odometer reading.

## Add a vehicle

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the fleet

Go to **Operations → Vehicles**, then click **Add Vehicle**.

<Screenshot
  label="Vehicles list with the Add vehicle button"
  caption="Every vehicle in your fleet is listed here."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Fill in the vehicle details

All fields are required. Complete them, then click **Submit**.

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Registration Number** | The license-plate number that identifies the vehicle. |
| **Type** | The kind of vehicle, e.g. *Bus*, *Minibus*, or *Car*. |
| **Brand** | The manufacturer, e.g. *Mercedes*, *Toyota*. |
| **Model** | The model name. |
| **Fuel Type** | The kind of fuel the vehicle runs on. |
| **Initial Mileage** | The odometer reading when you start tracking the vehicle. |
| **Current Mileage** | The latest odometer reading. |
| **Seats** | How many passengers the vehicle holds. Shown next to the vehicle on routes as a capacity reference. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Repeat for every vehicle

Add each bus and car in your fleet. They become available to pick when you build routes and
when you change the crew on a trip.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Adding many vehicles at once">
Click **Import** to add several vehicles from a spreadsheet file instead of one by one.
</Callout>

## Track a vehicle's costs

Open a vehicle (**View Details** in its row menu) to see tabs for the running costs you can log
against it:

| Tab | What it tracks |
| --- | --- |
| **Fuel** | Each refuel and its cost. |
| **Maintenance** | Service and repair records. |
| **Insurance** | Insurance policies and their expiry dates. |
| **Vignette** | Road tax / vignette and its renewal. |
| **Technical Inspection** | Roadworthiness inspections and when they expire. |

<Callout title="Tip">
Recording insurance, vignette, and inspection dates lets the school be reminded before a
vehicle's documents expire, so a bus is never on the road without valid papers.
</Callout>

## Retire a vehicle

To take a vehicle out of service, open its row menu and choose **Deactivate**. The vehicle stays
in your records and on past trips, but it no longer appears when you pick a vehicle for a route
or a trip. Choose **Activate** to bring it back. This is safer than deleting, which would remove
its cost history.

## Common use cases

- **New bus arrives** — add it to the fleet so it can be assigned to a route.
- **Bus sold or scrapped** — deactivate it to keep its history but hide it from new
  assignments.
- **Cost reporting** — log every refuel and service so you can see what each vehicle costs to
  run.
