title: Manage vehicles

---

A **vehicle** is any bus, minibus, or car your school uses for transport. You register each
vehicle once, and it then becomes available to assign to routes and trips. The fleet is also
where you keep a running record of each vehicle's costs — fuel, maintenance, insurance, and
inspections.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators and transport coordinators managing the school fleet.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- The **Transport module** must be enabled.
- Have each vehicle's details handy: registration (plate) number, make and model, and seat
  count.

## Add a vehicle

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the fleet

Go to **Transport** and open the **Vehicles** (Fleet) list, then click **Add vehicle**.

<Screenshot
  label="Vehicles list with the Add vehicle button"
  caption="Every vehicle in your fleet is listed here."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Fill in the vehicle details

Complete the fields below, then save.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Type** | Yes | The kind of vehicle, e.g. *Bus*, *Minibus*, or *Car*. |
| **Brand** | No | The manufacturer, e.g. *Mercedes*, *Toyota*. |
| **Model** | No | The model name. |
| **Registration number** | Yes | The license-plate number that identifies the vehicle. |
| **Fuel type** | No | The kind of fuel the vehicle runs on. |
| **Seats** | Yes | How many passengers the vehicle holds. Used as a capacity reference. |
| **Initial mileage** | No | The odometer reading when you start tracking the vehicle. |
| **Current mileage** | No | The latest odometer reading. |
| **Active** | — | Leave on for vehicles in service. Turn off to retire a vehicle without deleting it. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Repeat for every vehicle

Add each bus and car in your fleet. They become available to pick when you build routes and
when trips are created.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Track a vehicle's costs

Open a vehicle to see tabs for the running costs you can log against it:

| Cost record | What it tracks |
| --- | --- |
| **Fuel** | Each refuel and its cost. |
| **Maintenance** | Service and repair records. |
| **Insurance** | Insurance policies and their expiry dates. |
| **Vignette** | Road tax / vignette and its renewal. |
| **Technical inspection** | Roadworthiness inspections and when they expire. |

<Callout title="Tip">
Recording insurance, vignette, and inspection dates lets the school be reminded before a
vehicle's documents expire, so a bus is never on the road without valid papers.
</Callout>

## Retire a vehicle

To take a vehicle out of service, edit it and turn **Active** off. The vehicle stays in your
records and on past trips, but it no longer appears when you assign vehicles to new routes or
trips. This is safer than deleting, which would remove its cost history.

## Common use cases

- **New bus arrives** — add it to the fleet so it can be assigned to a route.
- **Bus sold or scrapped** — turn it inactive to keep its history but hide it from new
  assignments.
- **Cost reporting** — log every refuel and service so you can see what each vehicle costs to
  run.
