---
title: Assign students
description: Put each student on a route and the stop where they board, so they appear on daily trips and are billed correctly.
---

Assigning a student to a route tells the system **which stop the student uses** and **which
route carries them**. Once assigned, the student appears automatically on every trip for that
route, and their transport fee is added to their payment schedule.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators and transport coordinators building bus rosters.
</Callout>

## How rosters work

- A student can ride **one pickup route** (home → school) and **one dropoff route**
  (school → home) per academic year.
- You assign the student to the **stop** they board at; the route is the one that stop belongs to.
- Assignments can have a **start** and **end date** if a student only rides for part of the year.

## Before you start

- The **route and its stops** must already exist. See
  [Build routes and stops](./build-routes-and-stops).
- The student must be **enrolled** for the selected academic year.
- The student must have an **active transport fee** — that is, a payment plan that includes the
  Transport service. Only students who are eligible for transport appear in the picker. See
  [Set up fees](../finance/fees-and-payments/set-up-fees) and
  [Create payment plans](../finance/fees-and-payments/create-payment-plans).

<Callout title="Why a student might not appear">
If a student is missing from the list of students you can assign, it's almost always because
they don't yet have a transport fee on an active payment plan. Add the transport fee first, then
they'll appear.
</Callout>

## Assign students to a stop

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the route roster

Go to **Transport → Routes**, open a route, and switch to the **Roster** view. You'll see the
route's stops with the students currently assigned to each one.

<Screenshot
  label="Route roster with stops and assigned students"
  caption="The roster lists each stop and the students who board there."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Add students to a stop

Click **Assign students**, search for the students you want, and add them to the chosen stop.
Only eligible (transport-billed) students are shown.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Student** | Yes | The student to add. The picker shows only eligible students. |
| **Stop** | Yes | The stop the student boards at (or is dropped off at). |
| **Start date** | No | When the student begins riding. Leave empty to start now. |
| **End date** | No | When the student stops riding. Leave empty if open-ended. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Save

The student now appears on the route and will be included on every trip generated for it.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Move or remove a student

- **Move to a different stop** — open the roster, find the student, and change their stop. This
  is useful when a family moves house.
- **Stop riding** — set an **end date**, or remove the student from the route. Their transport
  fee is adjusted on their future installments accordingly.

<Callout title="Billing follows the roster">
When you add, move, or remove a student, their future transport installments are updated to
match the stop's pricing zone (or the level's default fee). You don't need to edit invoices by
hand.
</Callout>

## Common use cases

- **New rider** — add the student to the stop nearest their home on both the pickup and dropoff
  routes.
- **Family moves** — change the student's stop; billing updates automatically.
- **Mid-year start or stop** — use the start and end dates so the student is only on trips for
  the right period.
