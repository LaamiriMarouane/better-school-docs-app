---
title: Assign students
description: Put each student on the stop they use, so they appear on daily trips and their transport is billed correctly.
---

Assigning a student to a stop tells the system **which stop the student uses** and **which
route carries them**. Once assigned, the student appears automatically on every trip for that
route.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators and transport coordinators building bus rosters.
</Callout>

## How rosters work

- A student can ride **one pickup route** (home → school) and **one drop-off route**
  (school → home) at a time.
- You assign the student to the **stop** they use; the route is the one that stop belongs to.

## Before you start

- The **route and its stops** must already exist, and the route must be active. See
  [Build routes and stops](./build-routes-and-stops).
- The student must be **enrolled** for the selected academic year.
- *(For billing)* Students who pay for the bus need the **Transport** fee on their payment plan.
  See [Set up fees](../finance/fees-and-payments/set-up-fees) and
  [Create payment plans](../finance/fees-and-payments/create-payment-plans).

## Assign students to a stop

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the stop

Go to **Operations → Routes** and open a route. On the stop you want, open its **⋯** menu and
choose **Manage students**.

<Screenshot
  label="Route roster with stops and assigned students"
  caption="The roster lists each stop and the students who board there."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Pick the students

The dialog has two lists: **Available** students on one side and the students **At this stop**
on the other.

- By default, **Available** shows **Subscribers** — students who have the transport fee on
  their payment plan. Switch to **All students** to see every enrolled student.
- Search for a student and click **Assign**. Students without the transport fee show a
  **Not billable** tag: they can ride, but they aren't charged for the bus.
</Step>

<Step>
### Finish

Click **Done**. The students now appear on the route and are included on every trip created for
it.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Remove a student from a stop

Open the stop's **Manage students** dialog again and click **Unassign** next to the student in
the **At this stop** list. The student no longer appears on the route's trips.

<Callout title="How billing follows the roster">
Assigning or unassigning a student doesn't add or remove the transport fee itself — that is done
on the student's payment plan in **Finance**. For a student who already has the transport fee,
their upcoming unpaid transport installments are re-priced to match the stop's zone fee (or the
level's default fee). You don't need to edit invoices by hand.
</Callout>

## Common use cases

- **New rider** — add the student to the stop nearest their home on both the pickup and
  drop-off routes.
- **Family moves house** — unassign the student from the old stop and assign them to the new
  one. Their upcoming transport installments follow the new stop's fee.
- **Child stops taking the bus** — unassign them from both routes, then update their payment
  plan in **Finance**.
