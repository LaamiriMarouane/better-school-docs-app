title: Manage drivers

A **driver** is a member of staff who drives a vehicle. A **bus monitor** (the trip's
**assistant**) is a second crew member who rides along to help — for example to supervise
younger children or mark attendance. You register them once as staff, then assign them to
routes and trips.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators and transport coordinators who manage transport staff.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- The **Transport** module must be turned on.
- Drivers and bus monitors are **staff members**. See
  [Add staff](../getting-started/onboard-people/add-staff).

## Add a driver

<Steps>

<Step>
### Add the person with the Driver role

Add a new staff member and set their **Role** to **Driver**, or edit an existing staff member
and change their role to **Driver**.

<Screenshot
  label="Add staff form with the Driver type selected"
  caption="A driver is a staff member whose type is Driver."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Fill in the driver details

When the role is **Driver**, the form shows two extra fields. Complete the form, then save.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **First Name** / **Last Name** | Yes | The driver's name. |
| **Phone Number** | Yes | A contact number for the driver. |
| **Email** | No | Used if the driver needs a portal account. |
| **Driving License Number** | No | The driver's license identifier. |
| **License Expiry Date** | Yes | When the driving license expires. |
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Tip">
The **License Expiry Date** lets the school be reminded before a driver's license expires.
</Callout>

## Bus monitors

A bus monitor is a staff member with the **Bus monitor** role. Only bus monitors can be picked
as the **assistant** on a route or trip. On a trip, the **driver and the assistant have the same
abilities**: either one can start the trip, mark students, and finish it.

## Assigning crew to routes and trips

You don't pick the crew on every single trip by hand. Instead:

- **Default crew on the route** — when you build a route you set a **Default driver**,
  **Default assistant**, and **Default vehicle**. Every trip created for that route starts with
  that crew already filled in.
- **Per-trip changes** — if someone is off sick or unavailable for a day, an administrator can
  change the crew on that one trip without touching the route. See
  [Create trips](./create-trips).

Only staff who are currently employed appear in the crew pickers.

## Letting a driver sign in

Drivers and bus monitors who sign in get a **Trips** page listing **Today's trips**, where they
start their trips and mark students. They need a **portal account**. See
[Create portal accounts](../getting-started/onboard-people/create-portal-accounts).

## Common use cases

- **New driver hired** — add them as staff with the **Driver** role and record their license.
- **Driver leaves** — end their employment period so they no longer appear in crew pickers. See
  [Employment periods](../staff-hr/employment-periods).
- **Substitute for a day** — change the crew on the affected trips only.
