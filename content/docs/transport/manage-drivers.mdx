title: Manage drivers

A **driver** is a member of staff who drives a vehicle. An **assistant** (sometimes called a
bus monitor) is a second crew member who rides along to help — for example to supervise
younger children or mark attendance. You register drivers and assistants once, then assign
them to routes and trips.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators and transport coordinators who manage transport staff.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- The **Transport module** must be enabled.
- A driver is a type of **staff member**. If the person isn't in the system yet, add them as
  staff first. See [Add staff](../getting-started/onboard-people/add-staff).

## Add a driver

<Steps>

<Step>
### Add the person as a driver

Add a new staff member and set their staff type to **Driver**, or open an existing staff
member and change their type to **Driver**.

<Screenshot
  label="Add staff form with the Driver type selected"
  caption="A driver is a staff member whose type is Driver."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Fill in the driver details

Complete the fields below, then save.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Name** | Yes | The driver's full name. |
| **Phone** | No | A contact number for the driver. |
| **Email** | No | Used if the driver needs a portal account. |
| **Driving license number** | No | The driver's license identifier. |
| **License expiry date** | No | When the driving license expires. |
| **Active** | — | Leave on for active drivers. Turn off when a driver leaves. |
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Tip">
Record the **license expiry date** so the school can be reminded before a driver's license
expires.
</Callout>

## Assistants and bus monitors

An assistant is any staff member who rides along with the driver. Assistants don't need to be
registered as drivers — any staff member can be assigned as the assistant on a route or trip.
On a trip, the **driver and the assistant have the same abilities**: either one can start the
trip, mark attendance, and complete it.

## Assigning crew to routes and trips

You don't pick the crew on every single trip by hand. Instead:

- **Default crew on the route** — when you build a route you can set a **default driver**,
  **default assistant**, and **default vehicle**. Every trip created for that route starts with
  that crew already filled in.
- **Per-trip changes** — if someone is off sick or unavailable for a day, you can change the
  crew on that one trip without touching the route. See
  [Create trips](./create-trips).

## Letting a driver sign in

If a driver needs to sign in to mark attendance themselves, they need a **portal account** with
a role that grants the transport permissions. Create the account from the staff member's
profile. See [Create portal accounts](../getting-started/onboard-people/create-portal-accounts).

## Common use cases

- **New driver hired** — add them as staff with the Driver type and record their license.
- **Driver leaves** — turn them inactive so they no longer appear in crew pickers.
- **Substitute for a day** — change the crew on the affected trips only.
