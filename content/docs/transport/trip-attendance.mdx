---
title: Trip attendance
description: Mark who boarded and who was dropped off on each trip, handle absences, and keep an accurate record of every journey.
---

**Trip attendance** is the record of which students actually travelled on a trip. Every student
assigned to the route starts on the trip as **Expected**, and the crew updates each one as the
bus visits their stop. This gives the school an accurate record of who travelled, who did not,
and at what time.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Drivers and bus monitors marking students, and administrators reviewing or correcting
attendance.
</Callout>

## Attendance statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Expected** | The student is on the trip but hasn't been marked yet. This is the starting point. |
| **On board** | The student got on the bus. |
| **Dropped off** | The student left the bus at their stop. |
| **No show** | The student was expected but did not board. Also applied to anyone still unmarked when the trip finishes. |
| **Absent** | The school knows in advance the student won't ride today (for example, the family called). Only administrators set this. |

## Mark students during a trip (crew)

Drivers and bus monitors open **Trips** in their menu (**Today's trips** in the mobile app),
start their trip, and follow it stop by stop.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Start the trip

Tap **Start trip**. The phone's location must be allowed, so the school and parents can see
where the bus is.
</Step>

<Step>
### Mark each student at the stop

The current stop lists the students to handle there:

- **Pickup trip** — tap **Board** for each student who gets on, or **Not board** if they don't
  come.
- **Drop-off trip** — at the school, tap **Board** or **Not board** as students get on. At each
  home stop, tap **Drop off** as students get off.

<Screenshot
  label="Trip attendance list with status buttons per student"
  caption="Mark each student as the bus reaches their stop."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Move to the next stop

When every student at the stop is marked, tap **Mark stop & next**. The stop is recorded as
arrived, with the time, and the next stop comes up.
</Step>

<Step>
### Finish the trip

At the end, tap **Finish trip**. Students not marked yet are recorded as **No show** and their
parents are notified. A drop-off trip can't finish while a student is still on the bus — drop
everyone off first.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="The trip often finishes itself">
Once every student has a final status and every stop is done, the trip completes on its own.
If the bus can't complete the route, use **End trip (emergency)** in the trip's menu.
</Callout>

## Who can mark attendance

The **driver** and the **assistant** on a trip can both mark students — they have the same
abilities. Administrators can also correct any student's status from the trip's details in
**Operations → Trips**, and only administrators can mark a student **Absent**.

## Review past trips

Open a trip under **Completed** to see exactly who travelled, who did not, and the times
recorded. This record stays accurate even if the route or roster changes later.

## Common use cases

- **Normal run** — mark students stop by stop; the trip completes itself.
- **Child won't be riding** — when the family tells the school in advance, an administrator marks
  them **Absent**.
- **Follow up on a no-show** — review the trip and contact the family.
