**Trip attendance** is the record of which students actually travelled on a trip. Every student
assigned to the route starts on the trip as **Expected**, and the crew updates each one as the
bus visits their stop. This gives the school an accurate record of who travelled, who was
absent, and at what time.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Drivers, assistants, and bus monitors marking attendance, and administrators reviewing it.
</Callout>

## How attendance works

- When a trip is created, every assigned student is added to it with the status **Expected**.
- The crew marks each student as the bus reaches their stop.
- What you mark depends on the route's direction:
  - On a **pickup** trip (home → school), you mark students **Boarded** as they get on.
  - On a **dropoff** trip (school → home), you mark students **Dropped off** as they leave the bus.
- When everyone has been accounted for and every stop has been visited, the trip **completes
  automatically**.

## Attendance statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Expected** | The student is on the trip but hasn't been marked yet. This is the starting point. |
| **Boarded** | The student got on the bus (pickup trips). |
| **Dropped off** | The student left the bus at their stop (dropoff trips). |
| **Absent** | The student is known in advance not to be riding — a planned absence. You can mark it before the bus reaches the stop (for example, the family told you the child won't take the bus that day). |
| **No-show** | The student was expected but didn't show up at their stop. The crew can mark this at the stop, and it's also applied automatically to anyone left unmarked when the trip finishes. |

## Mark attendance

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the trip

Go to **Transport → Trips** and open today's trip. You'll see the list of stops and the
students expected at each one.
</Step>

<Step>
### Mark each student

As the bus reaches a stop, mark each student **Boarded**, **Dropped off**, or **Absent**. To
move quickly, use **Mark all boarded** at a stop to mark everyone waiting there at once, then
adjust any exceptions.

<Screenshot
  label="Trip attendance list with status buttons per student"
  caption="Mark each student as the bus reaches their stop."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Mark the stop visited

When the bus has finished at a stop, mark the stop as **arrived** (or **skipped** if it was
passed). This records the actual time the bus was there.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="The trip finishes itself">
Once every student has a final status and every stop has been visited, the trip completes on its
own — no need to tap complete. Any student still unmarked at that point is recorded as a
**no-show**.
</Callout>

## Add a note

You can add a short **note** to a student's attendance — for example "picked up by parent
instead" or "left early". Notes stay on the trip's record.

## Who can mark attendance

The **driver** and the **assistant** on a trip can both mark attendance — they have the same
abilities. Administrators can also mark or correct attendance from the trip's page.

## Review past trips

Open any past trip to see exactly who travelled, who was absent, and the times recorded. Because
each trip keeps its own snapshot, this record stays accurate even if the route or roster changes
later.

## Common use cases

- **Normal run** — mark students boarded/dropped off stop by stop; the trip completes itself.
- **Child won't be riding** — when the family tells you in advance, mark them absent.
- **Follow up on a no-show** — review the trip and contact the family.
