Every incident carries **points** — positive incidents add, negative ones subtract — and they roll
up into each student's **net points**. The **leaderboard** turns this into friendly recognition,
ranking students over a period you choose.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators and teachers reading the leaderboard, and students and parents following their own.
</Callout>

## How points add up

- A **positive** incident adds its points; a **negative** one subtracts.
- A student's **net points** is their total over the chosen period.

## The leaderboard (staff view)

Open **Behaviour → Leaderboard** to rank students by net points. You can:

- choose the period — **Week**, **Month**, **Term**, or **Year** — then the week, month, or term to show,
- filter by **class**; administrators can also filter by **level**, while teachers see only the
  classes they teach,
- see each student's **rank**, **net points**, and how many **positive** and **negative** incidents
  they had.

Use it to celebrate top students, or to spot who might need a little support.

## What students and parents see

Students — and their parents, for the child selected in the sidebar — get a motivating view of their
**own** standing:

- **This week's podium** — the dashboard shows the top three in their class this week.
- **Class ranking** — a page in the sidebar with their **Podium achievements** (how many times they
  finished 1st, 2nd, or 3rd) and **Your points over time**, a chart of their net points by
  **Week** or **Month**.
- **Behaviour** — a page in the sidebar with a read-only list of their own incidents.

<Callout title="Privacy">
Students and parents only see their **own** incidents and their class ranking. They don't see other
students' incident details, such as descriptions or locations — those are for staff only.
</Callout>
