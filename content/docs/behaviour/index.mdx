The **Behaviour** module is how your school records and follows student conduct — both the good and
the not-so-good. You define your own **categories** and **levels**, log **incidents** against
students, add **follow-up actions**, and turn it into **points** and a **leaderboard** that
recognises positive behaviour.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators who set up the behaviour system, and teachers who record day-to-day conduct.
</Callout>

## How it works

1. **Set it up** — define **categories** (e.g. *Respect*, *Disruption*) and **levels** of
   significance, each marked **positive** or **negative**, with default points.
2. **Record incidents** — log a behaviour event for a student, and add follow-up **actions** if
   needed.
3. **Points & leaderboard** — positive incidents add points and negative ones subtract them, and the
   **leaderboard** ranks students over a chosen period.

## Good to know

- **It belongs to an academic year.** Categories, levels, and incidents are all scoped to the
  selected year — set them up in the year you're working in.
- **The Behaviour module must be enabled** for your school for these screens to appear.
- **Students and parents see their own.** Students (and their parents) can see their own incidents
  and their class ranking, but not other students' details.
