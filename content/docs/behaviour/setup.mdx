---
title: Set up categories & levels
description: Define the categories, sub-categories, and levels that behaviour incidents are recorded against.
---

Before you record any behaviour, you set up the **vocabulary** your school uses: the **categories**
of behaviour, optional **sub-categories**, and the **levels** of significance. Each is marked
**positive** or **negative**, so the system knows whether it adds or removes points.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators setting up the behaviour system for the year.
</Callout>

## The pieces

| Piece | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Category** | The kind of behaviour — e.g. *Respect*, *Helpfulness*, *Disruption*, *Lateness*. Marked positive or negative, with a colour. |
| **Sub-category** | An optional finer label under a category, with its own **name** and **colour** (for example, under *Disruption*: *Talking*, *Phone use*). |
| **Level** | A tier of significance with **default points** — e.g. *Minor* / *Major*, or *Bronze / Silver / Gold* for positives. Its positive or negative side decides whether points add or subtract. |

When you record an incident, you pick a **category**, optionally a **sub-category**, and a **level** —
together they describe what happened and how much it's worth.

## Create a category

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the behaviour setup

Go to **Behaviour → Setup**, where categories and levels are managed.
</Step>

<Step>
### Add a category

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Name** | Yes | e.g. *Respect* or *Disruption*. |
| **Type** | Yes | **Positive** or **Negative**. |
| **Colour** | No | A colour to recognise it at a glance. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Add sub-categories (optional)

Under a category, add finer labels if you want them, each with a **name** and an optional **colour** —
for example *Talking* and *Phone use* under *Disruption*.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Create a level

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Name** | Yes | e.g. *Minor*, *Major*, *Gold*. |
| **Type** | Yes | **Positive** or **Negative**. |
| **Default points** | Yes | The points an incident at this level is worth. They pre-fill when recording, and can be adjusted. |
| **Order** | Yes | Where the level sits among the levels of the same type. |
| **Colour** | No | A colour for the level. |

<Callout title="Positive vs negative drives the points">
A level marked **positive** adds to a student's points; a **negative** level subtracts. Set generous
positives to reward good behaviour, and proportionate negatives for problems.
</Callout>
