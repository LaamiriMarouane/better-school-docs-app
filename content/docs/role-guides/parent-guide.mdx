As a parent (guardian), you follow your children's school life: their day, their work and
results, their behaviour, and what the family owes the school. This page covers the basics; the
next two pages go through each area in detail.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Parents and guardians with an account at the school. The school creates your account and
sends you your sign-in details.
</Callout>

## Sign in

You can use the **web app** in a browser or the **mobile app** on your phone, with the same
email and password.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Enter your email and password

Use the sign-in details the school sent you. See
[Log in to your account](../getting-started/access-and-authentication/log-in-to-your-account).
</Step>

<Step>
### Choose a new password if asked

The first time, the school usually gives you a temporary password. You are asked to choose a
new one before you continue. See
[Change a temporary password](../getting-started/access-and-authentication/change-a-temporary-password).
</Step>

<Step>
### Land on your home screen

On the web you arrive on your **Dashboard**. In the mobile app you arrive on **Home**.
</Step>

</Steps>

Forgot your password? Choose **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen. In the mobile app, this
opens the reset page in your browser. See
[Reset your password](../getting-started/access-and-authentication/reset-your-password).

## Choose which child you are looking at

Every parent screen shows **one child at a time**. When you sign in, your first child is
selected for you.

| Where | How to switch |
| --- | --- |
| **Web** | The top bar shows the selected child's name and photo. Click it and pick another child. When the sidebar is open, the same picker (**Choose a student**) also appears at the top of the sidebar. |
| **Mobile app** | Tap your child's round photo in the top bar. The **Select child** sheet opens; tap a child to switch. |

Everything you open next — timetable, grades, attendance, and so on — shows that child. If a
screen asks you to select a student, pick a child first.

<Callout title="Exceptions">
On the web, the **Payments** page has its own child buttons, including **All** to see every
child together. The bus and pickup screens show all your children at once.
</Callout>

## Find your way around

### On the web

The sidebar on the left (on the right in Arabic) holds everything:

| Menu | What's inside |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | Your child's day at a glance. |
| **Academic** | **Assignments**, **Courses**, **Grades**, **Behaviour**, **Class ranking**, **Timetable**. |
| **Communication** | **Notifications**, **Messages**, **Forms**, **Events**, **Announcements**, **Reclamations**. |
| **Payments** | Fees, installments, and receipts. |
| **My child's bus** | Live bus tracking. |
| **Pickup** | Tell the school you have arrived to collect your child. |

Your **Profile** is in the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar.

### In the mobile app

Tap the menu button in the top bar to open the menu: **Home**, **Fees & payments**,
**Timetable**, **Courses**, **Assignments**, **Exams**, **Grades**, **Events**, **Attendance**,
**Behaviour**, **Class ranking**, **Dismissal**, **School bus**, **Announcements**,
**Reclamations**, **Notifications**, **Messages**, and **Settings**. Tap your name at the top of
the menu to open your profile; **Logout** is at the bottom.

Items such as **Behaviour**, **School bus**, **Pickup** / **Dismissal**, **Announcements**,
**Messages**, and **Reclamations** appear only when your school uses them.

## Your dashboard

### On the web

The **Dashboard** shows the selected child:

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Re-enrollment** | A card for each child the school asks you to confirm for next year. See [Re-enrollment](./parent-payments-and-family#confirm-re-enrollment). |
| **Attendance Rate**, **Average Grade**, **Upcoming Exams** | Your child's attendance this month, average of recent exams, and exams in the next 30 days. |
| **Next due date**, **Overdue Amount** | The next installment to pay and what is late. |
| **This week — top 3 in your class** | The class behaviour podium (when your school uses behaviour). |
| **Today's Schedule** | Today's classes, teachers, and rooms. |
| **Pending Assignments**, **Recent Grades** | Homework still to do and the latest marks. |
| **Attendance**, **Upcoming payments**, **Upcoming Exams** | Summaries with a **View All** link to the full page. |
| **Events** and **Announcements** | The latest school news. |

### In the mobile app

**Home** greets you and shows the selected child's day:

- **Re-enrollment** cards first, if the school is waiting for your answer.
- **Sara's day** (with your child's name) — the class happening **Now** or the **Next class**.
- **School bus** — your child's bus status, while a trip is running.
- **Pick-up** — your child's pickup status, after you've checked in today.
- **Payments** — **Overdue** and **Due soon**, or **All payments are up to date**.
- **Latest grade**, **Upcoming exams**, and the latest **Announcements**.
- **Shortcuts** to the main screens.

Tap **See all** on a section to open the full screen. Pull down to refresh.

## Your profile and settings

- **Profile** — change your photo, phone number, and address, and use **Change Password**. Your
  name and email are managed by the school.
- **Language** — switch between English, French, and Arabic: from the language menu in the top
  bar on the web, or under **Settings** → **Language** in the mobile app.
- **Theme** — in the mobile app, **Settings** → **Theme** (**System**, **Dark**, **Light**).
