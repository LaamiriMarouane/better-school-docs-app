This page covers the family side of school life: money, getting your child to and from school,
and staying in touch.

## Payments

Open **Payments** on the web, or **Fees & payments** in the mobile app.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Choose a child

On the web, use the child buttons at the top of the page, or **All** to see every child
together. In the mobile app, the page shows the child selected in the top bar.
</Step>

<Step>
### Read the totals

Four tiles sum up the year: **Total Fees**, **Paid**, **Upcoming**, and **Overdue**. If an
amount is still owed from an earlier year, **Previous years owed** appears below them.
</Step>

<Step>
### Go through the installments

Each installment shows its description, due date, amount, what is paid, and what is left
(**Remaining** on the web, **Left to pay** in the mobile app), with its payment status and
whether it is **Overdue**, **Due soon**, or **On time**. Use the filter (web) or the chips
(mobile) to show, for example, only unpaid or overdue installments.
</Step>

<Step>
### Download a receipt

When an installment has been paid, its receipt number appears.

- **Web** — click the receipt number in the **Receipt** column to download it.
- **Mobile app** — tap **Receipt** on the installment, then save, send, or print it from your
  phone's share sheet.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Paying the school">
There is no online payment in the app. Pay the school the way it asks you to; once the school
records your payment, it appears here as paid, with its receipt.
</Callout>

## School bus

If your school runs a bus service, open **My child's bus** on the web or **School bus** in the
mobile app. While a trip is running, each child on the bus gets a card with a map (the school,
your stop, and the bus) and the child's status, such as **Waiting for the bus** or
**Dropped off**. You also get a notification when your child boards, is dropped off, or does not
board. See [Live tracking](../transport/live-tracking).

## Pick up your child

If your school uses pickup at the gate, open **Pickup** on the web or **Dismissal** in the
mobile app.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Tell the school you're here

When you arrive, choose the children you are collecting (**Who are you picking up?** on the
web, **Who is being collected?** in the mobile app). If the school has several gates, choose a
gate (**Choose a gate**). Then tap **I'm here**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Follow your child

Each check-in shows your queue number and its status: **Waiting for release**, then
**On the way**, then **Handed off**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Cancel if needed

While the status is still **Waiting for release**, you can cancel the check-in
(**Cancel check-in**).
</Step>

</Steps>

A child who rides the bus that day can't be checked in this way — the screen asks you to
contact the school to collect them at the gate. See [Pick up your child](../dismissal/parent-pickup).

## Confirm re-enrollment

Before a new school year, the school may ask you whether your child will come back. A card
appears at the top of your **Dashboard** (web) or **Home** (mobile app):
**Re-enrollment of** your child **for** the next year, sometimes with the level they move to.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Choose Confirm or Decline

Tap **Confirm** if your child will return, or **Decline** if not.
</Step>

<Step>
### Confirm your answer

A message reminds you what you are telling the school. Confirm to send it. **This answer is
final** — to change it afterwards, contact the school.
</Step>

</Steps>

The card disappears once you've answered.

## Fill in forms (web)

When the school shares a form with you — a survey, an authorisation, a registration form —
it appears under **Communication** → **Forms** on the web (**My forms**). Each form shows its
closing date (**Closes on**). Choose **Open**, fill it in, and send it; a **Thank you!** message
confirms it. A form you have already answered is marked **Answered**.

Forms are filled in on the web; the mobile app doesn't show them.

## Stay in touch with the school

| What | Where | What you can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Announcements** | Web: **Communication** → **Announcements**. Mobile: **Announcements**. | Read school news and open its attachments. See [Announcements](../communication/announcements). |
| **Events** | Web: **Communication** → **Events**. Mobile: **Events**. | See school events on a calendar. See [Events](../communication/events). |
| **Messages** | Web: **Communication** → **Messages**. Mobile: **Messages**. | Message your child's teachers. Start a conversation with **New message** (web) or the **+** button (mobile app), then search for a teacher. See [Messages](../communication/messaging). |
| **Notifications** | Web: the bell and **Communication** → **Notifications**. Mobile: **Notifications**, plus push notifications on your phone. | See what's new — homework, grades, payments, bus, pickup, events — and mark items as read. See [Notifications](../communication/notifications). |
| **Reclamations** | Web: **Communication** → **Reclamations**. Mobile: **Reclamations**. | Send a complaint to the school about the selected child. |

### Send a reclamation

<Steps>

<Step>
### Start a new one

Choose **New reclamation** (in the mobile app, the **+** button).
</Step>

<Step>
### Describe the issue

Pick the **Type** (**Grade issue**, **Fee issue**, or **General**), then write a **Title** and a
**Description**. Choose **Submit**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Follow it

Your reclamation shows as **Pending**, then **In progress**, and finally **Resolved** or
**Rejected**. Open it to read the school's **Resolution note**.
</Step>

</Steps>

See [Reclamations](../communication/reclamations).
