These guides are written for the people who follow school life day to day, rather than run it.
Each one walks you through the screens you see after signing in, on the web and in the mobile app.

| Guide | For you if you… |
| --- | --- |
| [Parent guide](./parent-guide) | Follow one or more children: signing in, choosing a child, and the dashboard. |
| [Your child's schoolwork](./parent-schoolwork) | Want to see your child's timetable, attendance, lessons, homework, exams, grades, report cards, and behaviour. |
| [Payments, bus, pickup, and family tasks](./parent-payments-and-family) | Pay the school, follow the bus, pick up your child, answer re-enrollment, fill in forms, and talk to the school. |
| [Student guide](./student-guide) | Are a student: signing in, your dashboard, timetable, attendance, behaviour, and messages. |
| [Your courses and work](./student-coursework) | Are a student: lessons, homework, quizzes, exams, grades, and report cards. |

<Callout title="Web or mobile?">
Parents and students can use both the web app and the mobile app with the same account. Most
screens exist in both; when something works differently, the guides say which one.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Some items depend on your school">
Your school chooses which modules it uses — for example behaviour, the school bus, pickup,
announcements, messages, or reclamations. If a menu item described here is missing, your school
doesn't use that module.
</Callout>
