As a student, you follow your own school life: your classes, your work, your results, and
school news. This page covers the basics; [Your courses and work](./student-coursework) goes
through lessons, homework, quizzes, exams, and grades.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Students with an account at the school. The school creates your account and gives you your
sign-in details.
</Callout>

## Sign in

You can use the **web app** in a browser or the **mobile app** on your phone, with the same
email and password.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Enter your email and password

Use the sign-in details the school gave you. See
[Log in to your account](../getting-started/access-and-authentication/log-in-to-your-account).
</Step>

<Step>
### Choose a new password if asked

If the school gave you a temporary password, you are asked to choose a new one before you
continue. See
[Change a temporary password](../getting-started/access-and-authentication/change-a-temporary-password).
</Step>

<Step>
### Land on your home screen

On the web you arrive on your **Dashboard**. In the mobile app you arrive on **Home**.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Find your way around

### On the web

The sidebar on the left (on the right in Arabic) holds everything:

| Menu | What's inside |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | Your day at a glance. |
| **Academic** | **Courses**, **Assignments**, **Exams**, **Grades**, **Timetable**, **Attendance**, **Behaviour**, **Class ranking**. |
| **Communication** | **Notifications**, **Messages**, **Announcements**, **Reclamations**, **Forms**, **Events**. |

Your **Profile** is in the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar.

### In the mobile app

Tap the menu button in the top bar to open the menu: **Home**, **Timetable**, **Courses**,
**Assignments**, **Exams**, **Grades**, **Events**, **Attendance**, **Behaviour**,
**Class ranking**, **Announcements**, **Reclamations**, **Notifications**, **Messages**, and
**Settings**. Tap your name at the top of the menu to open your profile; **Logout** is at the
bottom.

**Behaviour**, **Class ranking**, **Announcements**, **Messages**, and **Reclamations** appear
only when your school uses them.

## Your dashboard

### On the web

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Attendance Rate**, **Average Grade**, **Pending Assignments**, **Upcoming Exams** | Your attendance this month, the average of your recent exams, homework still to do, and exams in the next 30 days. |
| **This week — top 3 in your class** | The class behaviour podium (when your school uses behaviour). |
| **Today's Schedule** | Today's classes, teachers, and rooms. |
| **Pending Assignments**, **Recent Grades** | Homework to do and your latest marks. |
| **Attendance**, **Upcoming Exams** | Summaries with a **View All** link to the full page. |
| **Events** and **Announcements** | The latest school news. |

### In the mobile app

**Home** greets you and shows your day:

- The class happening **Now** or your **Next class**.
- **Due soon** and **Upcoming exams** counts.
- **Due soon** — up to three assignments, marked **Overdue**, **Today**, **Tomorrow**, or with
  their date. Tap one to open it. When there's nothing to do, you see
  **Nothing due — you're all caught up**.
- **Upcoming exams** and your **Latest grade**.
- **Attendance today** — only when you were marked absent or late today.
- **Shortcuts** to the main screens.

Tap **See all** on a section to open the full screen. Pull down to refresh.

## Timetable

Open **Timetable**.

- **Web** — a week view on a large screen, a day view on a small one. Each class shows the
  subject, teacher, and room.
- **Mobile app** — one day at a time. Use the arrows to move between days, or tap the date to
  pick one. A badge marks classes that are **In Progress**, **Completed**, or **Cancelled**.

## Attendance

Open **Attendance**.

- **Web** — a list of your attendance records with their status (**Present**, **Absent**,
  **Late**, and so on). Use the filter to narrow by date or status.
- **Mobile app** — the month's totals (**Present**, **Absent**, **Late**, **Half day**,
  **On leave**, and the **Overall rate**) above a calendar. Tap a day to open its
  **Day detail**.

If a record looks wrong, talk to your teacher or the school office.

## Behaviour and class ranking

These appear when your school uses behaviour tracking.

- **Behaviour** — the positive and negative incidents recorded for you, with points, date, and
  description.
- **Class ranking** — choose **Week** or **Month**.
  - **Web**: **Podium achievements** (past weeks or months you finished 1st, 2nd, or 3rd in your
    class) and **Your points over time**. This week's top 3 is on your **Dashboard**.
  - **Mobile app**: the class podium for the period, **Podium achievements**, and
    **Points over time**.

## Forms (web)

When the school shares a form with you, it appears under **Communication** → **Forms** on the
web (**My forms**). Choose **Open**, fill it in, and send it; **Thank you!** confirms it. A form
you've already answered is marked **Answered**. The mobile app doesn't show forms.

## Messages, news, and notifications

| What | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Messages** | Message your teachers. Start a conversation with **New message** (web) or the **+** button (mobile app), then search for a teacher. See [Messages](../communication/messaging). |
| **Announcements** | Read school news. See [Announcements](../communication/announcements). |
| **Events** | See school events on a calendar. See [Events](../communication/events). |
| **Notifications** | See what's new — new homework, deadlines, grades, timetable changes, events — and mark items as read. The mobile app also sends push notifications. See [Notifications](../communication/notifications). |
| **Reclamations** | Send a complaint to the school (**New reclamation**), if your school allows students to. Otherwise the page says so; ask a parent or the school office. See [Reclamations](../communication/reclamations). |

## Your profile and settings

- **Profile** — change your photo, phone number, and address, and use **Change Password**.
- **Language** — English, French, or Arabic: from the language menu in the top bar on the web,
  or **Settings** → **Language** in the mobile app.
- **Theme** — in the mobile app, **Settings** → **Theme**.
