Everything on this page shows the child you have selected. To look at another child, switch
child first — see [Choose which child you are looking at](./parent-guide#choose-which-child-you-are-looking-at).
You can look at everything, but only your child can hand in work or take a quiz.

## Timetable

Open **Timetable** (web: **Academic** → **Timetable**; mobile: menu → **Timetable**).

- **Web** — a week view on a large screen, a day view on a small one. Each class shows the
  subject, teacher, and room.
- **Mobile app** — one day at a time. Use the arrows to move between days, or tap the date to
  pick one. A badge marks classes that are **In Progress**, **Completed**, or **Cancelled**.

Today's classes also appear on your dashboard.

## Attendance

| Where | How to open it | What you see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Web** | On the **Dashboard**, the **Attendance** card → **View All**. | A list of your child's attendance records with their status. Use the filter to narrow by date or status. |
| **Mobile app** | Menu → **Attendance**. | The month's totals (**Present**, **Absent**, **Late**, **Half day**, **On leave**, and the **Overall rate**) above a calendar. Tap a day to open its **Day detail**. |

Attendance is read-only. If a record looks wrong, contact the school, for example through
[Messages](../communication/messaging).

## Courses and lessons

Open **Courses** (web: **Academic** → **Courses**; mobile: menu → **Courses**).

<Steps>

<Step>
### Pick a subject

You see your child's subjects for the year, each with its number of lessons. Search by name, or
pick a term (or **All terms**).
</Step>

<Step>
### Open a lesson

Open a subject to see its lessons, then pick a lesson to read the content the teacher shared.
In the mobile app, lessons are grouped by chapter.
</Step>

<Step>
### Open attachments and boards (web)

On the web, a lesson can also have **Attachments** to open and **Boards** — whiteboards the
teacher linked to the lesson. As a parent you open a board in **View only**; choose
**Back to lesson** to return.
</Step>

</Steps>

You only see lessons the teacher has made visible to your child's class.

## Assignments

Open **Assignments**. Each card shows the type (file or quiz), the due date, and where it
stands: **Upcoming**, **Open**, **Overdue**, or **Submitted**. Filter by type with the chips at
the top.

Open an assignment to see the instructions, the teacher's attachments, the due date, and your
child's submission:

- **Files your child handed in**, and when.
- **The grade and the teacher's feedback** once marked — under **Your grade** and **Feedback**
  on the web, **Your result** and **Teacher feedback** in the mobile app.
- **Graded — awaiting publication** means the teacher has marked the work but hasn't released
  the grade yet.

You can't submit work for your child — submitting is done from the student's own account.

### Quizzes

A quiz assignment shows the questions, points, time limit, and attempts. Only your child can
take it — the page says **Only the student can start this quiz.** (web) or **Only your child
can take this quiz.** (mobile). Under **Your child's attempts** (web) you can open each
finished attempt with **Review** to see your child's answers and how they were marked. In the
mobile app, tap a finished attempt to open its review. Whether answers can be reviewed depends on
how the teacher set up the quiz.

## Exams

- **Mobile app** — menu → **Exams**. The **Upcoming** tab lists scheduled exams with date and
  duration. The **Past** tab shows results; use the filter to pick a term or subject. **Grade
  pending** means the score isn't published yet; **Doesn't count** marks an exam left out of
  the average.
- **Web** — upcoming exams appear on the **Dashboard** (**Upcoming Exams**). Exam results are
  in **Grades**.

## Grades and report cards

Open **Grades**.

| | Web | Mobile app |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Overview** | A list of **Subjects**, each with its **Average**. | The **Overall average**, then a card per subject. Pick a term, or **All terms**. |
| **One subject** | Select a subject to see its **Subject Average**, **Subject Rank**, **Coefficient**, and each term's exams, **Assignments**, and **Assessments**. | Tap a subject to see each term's average, exams, **Assignments**, and **Other assessments**. |
| **Report cards** | Under **Report Cards**, choose **Download PDF** next to a card. | Under **Report cards**, tap a card to download it and share or save it. |

You only see marks the school has published. A report card appears once the school publishes
it — see [Report cards](../teaching/report-cards).

## Behaviour and class ranking

These appear when your school uses behaviour tracking.

- **Behaviour** — the incidents recorded for your child, positive or negative, with points,
  date, and description. It's read-only.
- **Class ranking** — choose **Week** or **Month**.
  - **Web**: **Podium achievements** (the past weeks or months your child finished 1st, 2nd, or
    3rd in the class) and **Your points over time**. This week's top 3 also appears on the
    **Dashboard**.
  - **Mobile app**: the class podium for the period, **Podium achievements**, and
    **Points over time**.
