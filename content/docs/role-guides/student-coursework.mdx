This page follows your schoolwork from the lesson to the report card. For signing in and finding
your way around, see the [Student guide](./student-guide).

## Courses and lessons

Open **Courses** (web: **Academic** → **Courses**; mobile: menu → **Courses**).

<Steps>

<Step>
### Pick a subject

You see your subjects for the year, each with its number of lessons. Search by name, or pick a
term (or **All terms**).
</Step>

<Step>
### Open a lesson

Open a subject to see its lessons, then pick one to read it. In the mobile app, lessons are
grouped by chapter. You only see lessons your teacher has made visible to your class.
</Step>

<Step>
### Open attachments and boards (web)

On the web, a lesson can also have **Attachments** and **Boards** — whiteboards your teacher
linked to the lesson. Open a board from the lesson; choose **Back to lesson** to return.
</Step>

</Steps>

### Work on a board (web)

A board opens as your teacher made it. To work on it yourself, choose **Enable editing**: you
then work on your own copy, and your changes are saved as you go. **Reset to teacher board**
brings back your teacher's version.

## File assignments

Open **Assignments**. Each card shows the type (file or quiz), the due date, and where it
stands: **Upcoming**, **Open**, **Overdue**, or **Submitted**. Filter by type with the chips at
the top. Open a card to see the instructions, your teacher's attachments, and the due date.

### Hand in your work

<Steps>

<Step>
### Add your files

- **Web** — in **Submit Your Work**, choose **Upload Files** or drop your files on the page.
- **Mobile app** — in **Submit your work**, tap **Add files** and pick them from your phone.

The page tells you how many files you can attach.
</Step>

<Step>
### Add a note (optional)

Write a comment for your teacher if you need to.
</Step>

<Step>
### Submit

Choose **Submit Assignment** (web) or **Submit** (mobile app). The assignment now shows as
**Submitted**, with the files you handed in.
</Step>

</Steps>

If your teacher allows it, you can hand in a new version before the work is graded
(**Resubmit Assignment** on the web, **Resubmit** in the mobile app).

The page also tells you when you can't submit online — for example when the homework is handed
in during class, isn't released yet, or is past its due date. If your teacher accepts late work,
you can still submit after the due date; submit as soon as you can.

### Read your grade and feedback

Once your teacher has graded your work, open the assignment to see your grade and comments —
**Your grade** and **Feedback** on the web, **Your result** and **Teacher feedback** in the
mobile app. **Graded — awaiting publication** means your work is marked but the grade isn't
released yet.

## Quizzes

A quiz assignment shows the number of questions, the points, the time limit, and how many
attempts you have left.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Start the quiz

Choose **Start Quiz**. If you left an attempt unfinished, choose **Resume quiz** to carry on.
</Step>

<Step>
### Answer the questions

Move through the questions with **Previous** and **Next**. Some questions have a hint. If the
quiz has a time limit, answer before the time runs out — what you've answered by then is
handed in.
</Step>

<Step>
### Submit

Submit the quiz at the end. In the mobile app, a summary tells you how many questions you
answered before you confirm. Depending on how your teacher set up the quiz, you then see your
score and whether you passed.
</Step>

</Steps>

You can't start a quiz before it's released, after its due date, or once you've used all your
attempts — the page tells you why.

### Review an attempt

Your finished attempts are listed under **My Attempts** (web) or **Your attempts** (mobile app).
Choose **Review** (web) or tap the attempt (mobile app) to see your answers and how they were
marked. In the mobile app you can also filter the questions: **All**, **Skipped**,
**Incorrect**, or **Partial**, and see **Your answer**, the **Correct answer**, and any
**Explanation**. Whether you can review depends on how your teacher set up the quiz.

## Exams

Open **Exams**.

| | Web | Mobile app |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Coming up** | The **Upcoming** tab lists your scheduled exams. | The **Upcoming** tab lists your exams with date and duration. |
| **Results** | The **Completed** tab shows your results; pick a term. You also see your **Performance by Subject**. | The **Past** tab shows your results; use the filter to pick a term or subject. **Grade pending** means the score isn't published yet. |

An exam marked as not counting (**Doesn't count**) is left out of your average.

## Grades and report cards

Open **Grades**.

| | Web | Mobile app |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Overview** | A list of **Subjects**, each with its **Average**. | Your **Overall average**, then a card per subject. Pick a term, or **All terms**. |
| **One subject** | Select a subject to see its **Subject Average**, **Subject Rank**, **Coefficient**, and each term's exams, **Assignments**, and **Assessments**. | Tap a subject to see each term's average, exams, **Assignments**, and **Other assessments**. |
| **Report cards** | Under **Report Cards**, choose **Download PDF** next to a card. | Under **Report cards**, tap a card to download it and share or save it. |

You only see marks once they're published. Report cards appear once the school publishes them.
