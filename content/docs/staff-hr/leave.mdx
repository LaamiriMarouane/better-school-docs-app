title: Leave
description: "Record staff time off, and approve or reject leave requests."
---

**Leave** records a staff member's time off for a date range. An administrator can record leave
directly, or a staff member can request it and an administrator decides.

<Callout title="Module required">
Leave needs the **Leave Management** module. If you don't see it, ask whoever manages your school's
modules to turn it on.
</Callout>

## Leave types

| Type | For… |
| --- | --- |
| **Sick Leave** | Illness. |
| **Maternity Leave** | Maternity leave. |
| **Unpaid Leave** | Time off without pay. |
| **Other** | Anything that doesn't fit the above. |

<Callout title="No balances or quotas">
Leave is recorded as **dates with a type** — there is no allowance, accrual, or running count of
days. *Unpaid Leave* is simply a type you choose, not a balance.
</Callout>

## Statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Requested by a staff member, waiting for a decision. |
| **Approved** | Granted. Leave an administrator records directly is approved straight away. |
| **Rejected** | Turned down, with a reason the requester can see. |
| **Canceled** | Called off by the staff member before it started. |

## Record leave as an administrator

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the staff member's leaves

Go to **HR → Staff**, open the staff member, then the **Leaves** tab, and click **Add Leave**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Set the type and dates

Choose the **Leave Type**, the **Start Date** and **End Date**, and optionally a **Reason**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Save

Click **Create Leave**. The leave is saved as **Approved** right away — no separate approval step
when an administrator records it.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Request leave as a staff member

Teachers open **Leave** in their menu; other staff open **My leave**. Click **Request Leave**,
choose the type and dates, and send it. The request is created as **Pending** and waits for an
administrator.

While the request is still **Pending** and its start date hasn't come, you can **Edit** or
**Cancel** it.

## Approve or reject requests

Go to **HR → Leave**. In the list, an administrator handles pending requests:

- **Approve** — grants the leave.
- **Reject** — turns it down. Enter a **Rejection Reason**; the requester will see it.
- **Bulk approve / reject** — select several pending requests and act on them at once.

An administrator can't approve their own leave request: another administrator has to decide.
From the same list you can also **Edit** or **Delete** a leave.

<Callout title="Overlaps are blocked">
A staff member can't have two leaves that overlap the same dates. If you try to add or edit one into
an existing leave's range, you'll get an error.
</Callout>

## Who can do what

| Action | Administrator | Staff / teacher |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Record leave directly (approved) | ✅ | — |
| Request leave (pending) | — | ✅ (their own) |
| Approve / reject | ✅ (not their own request) | — |
| Edit or cancel a pending request before it starts | — | ✅ (their own) |
| See all staff's leave | ✅ | Their own only |
