This section covers the **HR** side of managing your team: **employment periods** and **payroll**,
plus **leave** and **staff attendance**. Leave and staff attendance are optional modules — turn
them on for your school to see them.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators managing staff. Teachers can request their own leave and see their own records.
</Callout>

## The pieces

| Tool | Use it to… |
| --- | --- |
| **Employment periods** | Record contracts and pay terms on each staff member — the basis for payroll. |
| **Payroll** | Run monthly payroll, generate payslips, and handle advances. |
| **Leave** | Record staff time off — sick, maternity, unpaid, or other — and approve or reject requests. |
| **Staff attendance** | Mark who was present, absent, late, on a half-day, or on leave, and review the totals. |

In the admin sidebar these live under **HR** (Staff, Leave, Payroll). Employment is managed from
the staff profile or from Payroll → Staff Salaries — not as its own menu item.

## Modules

<Callout title="Leave and staff attendance are optional modules">
**Leave** needs the **Leave Management** module, and **staff attendance** needs the **Staff
Management** module. When a module is off, its pages don't appear. Teachers' attendance still feeds
the dashboards even when Staff Management is off.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- Your **staff** should already be added. See [Onboard people](../getting-started/onboard-people).
- Leave and attendance are recorded **per academic year**, so work in the year shown in the
  switcher at the top of the screen.
- Employment periods are **date-based** (not tied to the academic year switcher). Payroll uses
  the period that covers the month you are paying.
