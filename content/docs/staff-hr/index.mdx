This section covers the **HR** side of managing your team: **employment periods**, **leave**,
**staff attendance**, and the **staff month**. Leave, staff attendance and staff month are part of
optional modules — turn them on for your school to see them.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators managing staff. Teachers and other staff can request their own leave and see their
own records.
</Callout>

## The pieces

| Tool | Use it to… |
| --- | --- |
| **Employment periods** | Record each staff member's contracts — start and end dates, contract type, and pay. |
| **Leave** | Record staff time off — sick, maternity, unpaid, or other — and approve or reject requests. |
| **Staff attendance** | Mark who was present, absent, late, on a half-day, or on leave. |
| **Staff month** | See each month's hours taught, absences, leave, bonuses and advances, record who was paid, and export it for your accountant. |

In the admin sidebar, **HR** holds **Staff**, **Leave**, and **Staff month**. Employment is managed
from the staff profile, not as its own menu item. Staff attendance lives under **Attendance**
(**Staff Attendance** and **Teacher Attendance**).

## Modules

<Callout title="Leave and staff attendance are optional modules">
**Leave** needs the **Leave Management** module, and **staff attendance** and **staff month** need
the **Staff Management** module. When a module is off, its pages don't appear. Teachers' attendance still feeds
the dashboards even when Staff Management is off.
</Callout>
