title: Staff attendance
description: "Mark who was present, absent, late, on a half-day, or on leave each day."
---

**Staff attendance** records one status per staff member per day. You mark it manually — one
person at a time, in bulk, or across a grid.

<Callout title="Module required">
Staff attendance is part of the **Staff Management** module. Teachers' attendance is always available
and feeds the dashboards; managing **non-teaching staff** attendance needs the module turned on.
</Callout>

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, open **Attendance**, then:

- **Teacher Attendance** — for teachers.
- **Staff Attendance** — for other staff.

Staff members see their own records under **My attendance**.

## Statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Present** | At work. |
| **Absent** | Did not attend. |
| **Late** | Arrived late. |
| **Half Day** | Worked part of the day. |
| **On Leave** | Off on leave for the day. |

<Callout title="“On Leave” is set by hand">
Marking someone **On Leave** here is a manual choice. Approving a [leave](./leave) does **not**
fill in attendance for those days — the two are kept separate.
</Callout>

## Mark attendance

You can record attendance in a few ways:

- **One at a time** — set a person's status for a date, with an optional **Check In Time**,
  **Check Out Time**, and **Remarks**.
- **In bulk** — mark several staff for a date in one go.
- **In the grid** — staff in the list against the days of the **Week** or **Month**, so you can fill
  a whole period quickly.

Each staff member has only **one** record per day — marking the same day again updates it rather
than adding a second one.

## Teachers vs. other staff

The **Staff Attendance** page is part of **Staff Management**. **Teacher Attendance** works the same
way but is always available — even with Staff Management off — because it also powers the teacher
numbers on the dashboards.

## Where the numbers appear

Attendance feeds the **attendance figures** on the [dashboard](../dashboards/reports-and-statistics).
