**Staff attendance** records one status per staff member per day, for the academic year. You mark it
manually — one person at a time, in bulk, or across a grid — and the totals roll up into rates you can
review.

<Callout title="Module required">
Staff attendance is part of the **Staff Management** module. Teachers' attendance is always available
and feeds the dashboards; managing **non-teaching staff** attendance needs the module turned on.
</Callout>

## Statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Present** | At work. |
| **Absent** | Did not attend. |
| **Late** | Arrived late. |
| **Half-day** | Worked part of the day. |
| **On leave** | Off on leave for the day. |

<Callout title="“On leave” is set by hand">
Marking someone **On leave** here is a manual choice. Approving a [leave](./leave) does **not**
automatically fill in attendance records for those days — the two are kept separate.
</Callout>

## Mark attendance

You can record attendance in a few ways:

- **One at a time** — set a person's status for a date, with optional **check-in** and **check-out**
  times and a **remark**.
- **In bulk** — mark several staff for a date in one go.
- **In the grid** — a matrix of staff (rows) against dates (columns), so you can fill a whole period
  quickly.

Each staff member can have only **one** record per day — marking the same day again updates the
existing one rather than adding a duplicate.

## Teachers vs. other staff

The grid and the non-teaching-staff views are part of **Staff Management**. **Teachers'** attendance
is handled the same way but is always available — even with Staff Management off — because it also
powers the teacher numbers on the dashboards.

## Review the totals

Attendance rolls up per staff member and across the team, for any date range:

- **Attendance rate** — share of days marked present.
- **Punctuality rate** — present out of those who showed up (present, late, and half-days).
- **Work hours** — totalled from check-in/check-out times where you recorded them.
- **Counts** by status — present, absent, late, half-day, and on-leave records.

These same numbers feed the **attendance figures** on the [dashboard](../dashboards/reports-and-statistics).
