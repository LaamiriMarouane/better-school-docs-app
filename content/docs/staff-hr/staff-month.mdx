---
title: Staff month
description: Each month, see hours taught, absences, leave, bonuses and advances for every staff member, record who was paid, and export it for your accountant.
---

**Staff month** gathers, for one month, what your accountant needs from the school: **hours
taught**, **absences**, **leave**, any **bonus** or **advance**, and whether each person **was
paid**. The platform doesn't calculate pay — you close the month and send the file to your
accountant, who works out the pay in their own software.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators, on the web app. It needs the **Staff Management** module.
</Callout>

## Open Staff month

From the admin sidebar, go to **HR → Staff month**.

The page opens on the current month. Use the **Previous month** and **Next month** arrows next to
the month name to change month.

A staff member appears in the list when they have an employment period that covers the month, or
when they taught hours that month (for example as a substitute). If nobody appears, check their
[employment periods](./employment-periods).

While the month is still running, a note says: *This month is not over yet: hours and absences
count up to today.*

## Summary cards

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Paid** | How many people are marked paid, out of everyone listed. |
| **Hours taught** | Total hours taught by teachers this month. |
| **Bonuses** | Total of all bonuses entered. |
| **Advances** | Total of all advances entered. |

## The list

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Staff member** | Name, role, contract type and pay terms from their employment period. A warning such as **Hourly rate missing** appears when a pay type has no amount — fix it on the [employment period](./employment-periods). |
| **Hours taught** | For teachers: hours, number of sessions, and substitutions. *Not confirmed by the teacher* counts sessions the teacher didn't mark as done. Click the hours to see the sessions. |
| **Absences** | Days absent, half days, and times late, from [staff attendance](./staff-attendance). |
| **Leave** | Days of approved leave by type — sick, maternity, unpaid, other — from [Leave](./leave). |
| **Bonus, advance, note** | What you entered for this month. |
| **Pay** | **Paid** (with date, method and reference) or **Not paid**. |

### See the sessions behind the hours

Click a teacher's hours. A window lists each session of the month — date, time, class and
subject — and how it was counted: **Taught**, **Not confirmed**, **Substitution** or **Not counted
(absent)**.

## Add a bonus, advance or note

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the row

Click the **Bonus, advance and note** (pencil) button on the staff member's row.
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter the amounts

Fill in **Bonus**, **Advance** and **Note for the accountant** as needed. These are passed on to
the accountant — nothing is calculated here.
</Step>

<Step>
### Save

Click **Save**.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Mark people as paid

Once the pay has been handed over, record it:

- For one person, click **Mark as paid** on their row.
- For everyone not paid yet, click **Mark all … as paid** above the list.

In the window, choose **Paid on**, the **Payment Method** (Cash, Credit Card, Bank Transfer or
Cheque) and an optional **Reference**, then click **Mark as paid**.

To undo it, click the **Mark as not paid** (undo) button next to the **Paid** badge. On a closed
month, reopen the month first.

## Close the month

When the month is over and everything is entered, close it so the figures stop changing.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Close

Click **Close the month**, then confirm. The button works only once the month has ended.
</Step>

<Step>
### Export for your accountant

Click **Export for the accountant** to download an Excel file in your language. It has one row per
staff member with all the figures above, and a second sheet listing the sessions.
</Step>

</Steps>

A closed month shows *Closed on … by …*. Its hours, absences and leave are frozen as they were when
you closed it, and bonuses, advances and notes can't be changed. You can still **mark people as
paid** — pay often happens after the file is sent.

To change something, click **Reopen the month** and confirm. The figures are counted again from
the timetable, attendance and leave; bonuses, advances, notes and payments are kept. If you already
sent the file, send the new one too.

<Callout title="Export without closing">
You can export at any time, also before closing. Closing first makes sure the file matches what
stays on screen.
</Callout>
