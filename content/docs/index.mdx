Welcome to the documentation. It walks you through setting up your school, onboarding
people, enrolling students, and running day-to-day operations.

## Sections

- **[Getting started](./getting-started)** — set up your school step by step, from the
  academic foundation to going live.
- **[Dashboards & reports](./dashboards)** — the dashboard you land on and the statistics
  behind it.
- **[Finance](./finance)** — services, fees, payment plans, payments, and expenses.
- **[Staff & HR](./staff-hr)** — staff employment, leave, and staff attendance.
- **[Settings](./settings)** — school details, modules, finance periods, subscription, and
  support.
- **[Transport](./transport)** — vehicles, drivers, routes and stops, riders, daily trips,
  and live tracking.
- **[Equipment](./equipment)** — a simple register of your school's boards and projectors.
- **[Timetable](./timetable)** — set up the school week, generate the timetable, then
  activate it so class sessions appear.
- **[Teaching & Learning](./teaching)** — courses and lessons, assignments, quizzes, exams,
  grading, and class sessions with attendance.
- **[Behaviour](./behaviour)** — record behaviour, organise it with categories, and follow
  points on the leaderboard.
- **[Communication](./communication)** — announcements, events, chat, audiences, and
  notifications.
