School finance has two sides, and each is its own group of articles:

- **[Fees & Payments](./fees-and-payments)** — money coming **in**: services,
  fees, payment plans, installments, and the payments you collect from families.
- **[Expenses](./expenses)** — money going **out**: expense categories and the
  expenses your school records and pays.

## Which one do I need?

| You want to… | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| Price tuition, transport, or meals | [Fees & Payments](./fees-and-payments) |
| Build a payment plan for a level | [Fees & Payments](./fees-and-payments) |
| Collect a payment or issue a refund | [Fees & Payments](./fees-and-payments) |
| Record what the school spends | [Expenses](./expenses) |
| Organise spending into categories | [Expenses](./expenses) |

<Callout title="Work in the right year">
Fees, payment plans, payments, and expenses follow the academic year selected at the top of
the screen. Choose the right year before setting things up.
</Callout>
