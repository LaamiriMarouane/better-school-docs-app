---
title: Record a payment
description: Collect a payment against a student's installments, pay for several siblings at once, and print receipts.
---

Recording a payment settles one or more of a student's **installments**. A single
payment can cover several installments at once and can include a one-off discount.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Front-office and finance staff collecting fees.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- The student must have **installments** — see
  [Installments and schedules](./installments-and-schedules).

<Screenshot
  label="Payment center with a student selected"
  caption="The payment center finds a student and lists their installments."
/>

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Find the student

Open the **Payment center** (from **Finance → Payments**, or **Record payment** on the
dashboard). Search for the student or a guardian and pick the
student.
</Step>

<Step>
### Select the installments to pay

The student's installments are listed, grouped by fee. Use **Pay Now** on one
installment, or tick several and pay them together.

<Screenshot
  label="Installment payment checkout"
  caption="Selected installments on the left, the checkout summary on the right."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Apply a discount (optional)

Under **Apply discount**, choose a **Discount type** (**Percentage** or **Fixed
amount**) and a **Discount value**. The **Final amount** updates automatically.
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter the payment details

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Paid by** | No | The guardian making the payment. |
| **Payment Method** | Yes | **Cash**, **Credit Card**, **Bank Transfer**, or **Cheque**. |
| **Payment Date** | Yes | The day the money was received. It can't be in the future. |
| **Paid by (name)** | No | The payer's name, when it isn't a guardian on file. |
| **Payment Reference** | No | A cheque or transfer number, for example. |
| **Total Amount Paid** | Yes | Pre-filled with the final amount. Lower it for a partial payment. It can't be more than what is still due. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Confirm

Click the pay button. The selected installments' **Amount Paid** increases and their
**Remaining** drops. A partial payment leaves the installment partly settled.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Partial payments">
You do not have to pay an installment in full. Enter a smaller **Total Amount Paid** and
the remainder stays due — collect it with a later payment.
</Callout>

## Pay for several children at once

When the student has brothers or sisters enrolled, the **Installments** list shows **Pay
for several children**.

1. Tick at least two children, then the installments to pay for each. The amount defaults
   to what is left on the ticked lines.
2. Fill in the payment details as above.
3. Click **Record family payment**. Each child gets their own receipt, and you can also
   **Download family receipt**.

## Receipts

Every payment has a receipt. Use **Download receipt** or **Print receipt** on a payment
in **Recent payments** (in the Payment center) or in the **Finance → Payments** list. You
choose the language of the receipt when you download it.
