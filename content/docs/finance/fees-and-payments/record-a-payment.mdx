---
title: Record a payment
description: Collect a payment against a student's installments, with an optional discount.
---

Recording a payment settles one or more of a student's **installments**. A single
payment can cover several installments at once, optionally apply a discount, and is
attributed to a payment method and a payer.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Front-office and finance staff collecting fees.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- The student must have generated **installments** — see
  [Installments and schedules](./installments-and-schedules).

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Find the student

In the **payment center**, search for and select the student whose fees you are
collecting.
</Step>

<Step>
### Select the installments to pay

The student's outstanding installments are listed, grouped by fee. Tick the ones this
payment covers. The total updates as you change the selection.

<Screenshot
  label="Installment payment checkout"
  caption="Selected installments on the left, the checkout summary on the right."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Apply a discount (optional)

Optionally enter a **discount** — either a percentage or a fixed amount. The final
amount updates automatically.
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter the payment details

Complete the payment fields, then submit.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Paid by** | No | The guardian making the payment, if recorded. |
| **Payment method** | Yes | Cash, credit card, or bank transfer. |
| **Total amount paid** | Yes | Pre-filled with the final amount; adjust for a partial payment. |
| **Discount type / value** | No | Percentage or fixed amount taken off the total. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Confirm

Submit the payment. The selected installments' **amount paid** increases and their
**remaining** drops. A partial payment leaves the installment partly settled.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Partial payments">
You do not have to pay an installment in full. Enter a smaller **total amount paid**
and the remainder stays due — collect it with a later payment.
</Callout>
