---
title: Create payment plans
description: Bundle fees into a payment plan for one or more levels, then put students on it.

A **payment plan** bundles one or more **fees** for one or more **levels**. It is what
you attach to an enrollment — once a student is on a plan, their installments are created
from the plan's fees.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators packaging fees into the plans students are billed under.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- The **fees** must already exist — see [Set up fees](./set-up-fees).
- Pick the right **academic year** at the top of the screen.

## Create a plan

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the Payment Plans page

Go to **Finance → Payment Plans**, then click **Add Payment Plan**.

<Screenshot
  label="Create payment plan form"
  caption="The plan form: name, level, type, conditions and fees."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Fill in the plan details

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Name** | Yes | The display name, e.g. *Primary — Full plan*. |
| **Plan Type** | Yes | **Academic** (the main plan students are enrolled with), **Service**, or **Event**. |
| **Levels** | Yes | One or more levels the plan is for. |
| **Conditions** | No | Optional notes or terms shown with the plan. |
| **Fees** | Yes | One or more fees to include. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Mark each fee required or optional

Each fee you add appears in a list under **Fees**. For each one choose:

- **Required** — every student on the plan is billed for it.
- **Optional** — an add-on the family can take or not (for example canteen or
  transport). Tick **Pre-selected** if it should be ticked by default when a student
  joins the plan.
</Step>

<Step>
### Save the plan

Click **Create Payment Plan**. The plan is now available when you create an enrollment
or enroll students in bulk.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Put students on a plan

From the plans list, use **View Students** on a plan:

- **Associate enrolled student** — search for an enrolled student and add them, choosing
  their **Optional services**. A **Quantity** lets you bill each fee several times (for
  example two uniforms).
- **Add a class / level** — pick classes or levels and click **Add students**. Students
  already on the plan, or not eligible, are skipped and listed with the reason.
- **Manage add-ons** — on a student's row, tick or untick their optional services.
- **Remove from plan** — the student is no longer billed for this plan. If money was
  already paid on it, refund or cancel those payments first.

## Change or retire a plan

- While students are on a plan you can still add fees, but not remove fees or change its
  levels or type. Students already on the plan are billed for a new required fee from
  today.
- Use **Archive** to retire a plan you no longer offer, and **Unarchive** to bring it
  back.

<Callout title="What comes next">
Putting a student on a plan creates their installments — see
[Installments and schedules](./installments-and-schedules).
</Callout>
