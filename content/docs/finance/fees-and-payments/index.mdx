The finance area turns what your school charges into amounts students owe and payments
you collect. It is built from a few pieces that stack together:

| Piece | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Service** | Something the school provides — tuition, transport, canteen, uniforms. |
| **Fee** | A priced charge for a service: one amount for chosen levels (**Flat**) or an amount per level (**Per level**), with a periodicity. |
| **Payment plan** | A bundle of fees for one or more levels. Each fee is required or optional. |
| **Installment** | One scheduled portion of what a student owes, with a due date. |
| **Payment** | Money collected against one or more installments. |
| **Reduction rule** | A reusable discount assigned to students (percentage or fixed). |

## How it fits together

<Steps>

<Step>
### Create services

Define what the school provides — see [Create services](./create-services).
</Step>

<Step>
### Set up fees

Price each service and choose how often it is charged — see
[Set up fees](./set-up-fees).
</Step>

<Step>
### Create payment plans

Bundle fees into a plan for your levels — see
[Create payment plans](./create-payment-plans).
</Step>

<Step>
### Set reduction rules (optional)

Define reusable discounts and assign them to students — see
[Reduction rules](./reduction-rules).
</Step>

<Step>
### Attach a plan when enrolling

When a student is enrolled with a plan, their **installments** are created — see
[Installments and schedules](./installments-and-schedules).
</Step>

<Step>
### Collect payments

Record payments in the **Payment center** (open it from **Finance → Payments**) — see
[Record a payment](./record-a-payment). Reverse one with
[Refund a payment](./refund-a-payment).
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Pick the right year">
Choose the academic year at the top of the screen before you set up services, fees, and
plans.
</Callout>
