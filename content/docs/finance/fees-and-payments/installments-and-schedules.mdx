---
title: Installments and schedules
description: Understand how a student's installments are generated from their payment plan, enrollment date, and billing configuration.
---

An **installment** is one scheduled portion of what a student owes — an amount with a
**due date**. Installments are not created by hand: they are **generated** when a
payment plan is attached to an enrollment.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators who want to understand where a student's balance comes from.
</Callout>

The examples below use an academic year that runs **1 September through 30 June**, and
the default billing configuration: due on the **1st** of each month, charged **in advance**.
Other anchors and timings are covered after the default cases. Billing configuration is
set under **Settings → Finance → Billing** — see
[Billing configuration](../../settings/billing-configuration).

## How installments are generated

<Steps>

<Step>
### A plan is attached to an enrollment

When a student is enrolled with a payment plan — or a plan is set on an existing
enrollment — the plan's fees become the basis of the schedule.
</Step>

<Step>
### Months before today are not generated

The schedule starts from **today** in the school timezone, or from the first day of
the academic year when today is earlier than that. Months before today are not
generated. Nothing is generated after the year ends.

The enrollment date can default to today. It does not move the first due date off the
billing anchor.
</Step>

<Step>
### Each fee expands by its periodicity

| Periodicity | What is generated |
| --- | --- |
| **Monthly** | One installment per month, from the first due date through the end of the year. |
| **Quarterly** | One installment every three months, on the same rule. |
| **One-time** or **yearly** | A single installment, due on the first due date. |
</Step>

<Step>
### The schedule appears on the student

The generated installments — each with an amount due and a due date — show on the
student's payment view, grouped by fee.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Due dates

With the default configuration, every month in the schedule is due on the **1st**,
including the month the student joins.

An unpaid installment whose due date is **before today** in the school timezone shows
as **Overdue**. Due today is not overdue. The amount stays collectible.

### Student joins after the 1st — in advance

Enrolled on **3 October**. Charge timing **in advance**. Cycle anchor **1st of each month**.

| Fee | Installments |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly (canteen, activities, …) | **1 October**, then **1 November** through **1 June**. September is not billed. October is billed on the 1st. |
| One-time (registration, supplies, …) | One installment due **1 October**. |

On 3 October those 1 October rows are already **Overdue**. November through June stay pending.

### Student joins on the anchor day

Enrollment date **1 October**. The first due date is **1 October**, then the 1st of
each following month through June. A one-time fee is also due on 1 October.

If the plan is attached on 3 October, those 1 October rows are already overdue: the
student was enrolled on the due date, and that date has passed.

### In arrears

**In arrears** bills at the end of the period. If the student joins after this month's
anchor, that period is skipped. Enrollment on **3 October** with the 1st as the anchor
starts on **1 November**. October is not billed. Later months stay on the 1st.

### Enrollment date as the anchor

When the cycle anchor is **Student enrollment date**, every installment falls on that
day of the month: enrollment on 3 October gives 3 October, 3 November, 3 December, and
so on. A short month uses the last day of that month, then the enrollment day returns.

## Changing a plan

A successful change **replaces** the schedule with the new plan. It does not keep the
old plan's unpaid rows.

| What happened | What the schedule does |
| --- | --- |
| **Change succeeds** | Every unpaid installment of the old plan is removed, **including overdue ones**. Paid installments stay. A partly paid installment is closed at the amount already collected and stays. |
| **New schedule** | Built from **today**, not from the original enrollment date, through the end of the year. The same due-date rules apply. A fee already **paid for that same due date** is not billed again. |
| **Change is rejected** | Nothing is removed and nothing is added. The plan and the installments stay as they were. |
| **Same plan chosen again** | No new installments. |

<Callout title="Review the result">
After a successful change, open the student's installments. Overdue rows from the old
plan are gone. The new plan's fees are there instead, starting in the current period.
</Callout>

### Switch with only overdue rows

The old plan has an unpaid canteen due **1 October** (overdue) and nothing paid. The
switch succeeds. That October row is removed, along with every other unpaid row of the
old plan. The new plan is generated from today: a one-time fee due on the current
anchor (1 October, if they were already enrolled then), and a monthly fee from that
date through June. There is no second copy of the same fee on the same date.

### Switch when something was already paid

A 1 October installment that is already paid stays on the old plan. If the new plan
contains that same fee, it does not add another installment on 1 October. Later months
of the new plan are still generated.

### Switch rejected

The change is saved only if the whole replacement succeeds. If it is rejected — the
plan does not apply to the student's level, the year is closed, or the schedule cannot
be built — the student keeps the previous plan and the previous installments. Overdue
rows stay because the change did not happen, not because a successful change preserves
them.

## Reading an installment

Each installment tracks three figures:

| Figure | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Amount due** | The scheduled amount for that installment. |
| **Amount paid** | How much has been collected against it so far. |
| **Remaining** | Amount due minus amount paid — what is still owed. |

An installment is fully settled when the remaining amount reaches zero.
