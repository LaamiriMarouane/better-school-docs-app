---
title: Installments and schedules
description: Understand how a student's installments are created from their payment plan, enrollment date, and billing settings.
---

An **installment** is one scheduled portion of what a student owes — an amount with a
**due date**. You don't create installments by hand: they are **created for you** when a
student is put on a payment plan.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators who want to understand where a student's balance comes from.
</Callout>

The examples below use an academic year that runs **1 September through 30 June**, and
the default billing settings: **Billing Cycle Anchor** on the **1st of each month**,
**Charge Timing** in advance, and **Student joining mid-month** set to **Full month**.
You change these under **Settings → Finance → Billing** — see
[Billing configuration](../../settings/billing-configuration).

## How installments are created

<Steps>

<Step>
### A plan is attached to an enrollment

When a student is enrolled with a payment plan — or a plan is set on an existing
enrollment — the plan's fees become the basis of the schedule.
</Step>

<Step>
### Billing starts at the enrollment date

The main (**Academic**) plan is billed from the student's **enrollment date**, never
before the first day of the academic year. This holds even if you enter the enrollment
later: a student enrolled on 1 September but entered in October still owes September.

A **Service** or **Event** plan, or an optional service added later, starts the day it
is added. Nothing is billed after the year ends.
</Step>

<Step>
### Each fee expands by its periodicity

| Periodicity | What is created |
| --- | --- |
| **Monthly** | One installment per month, through the end of the year. |
| **Quarterly** | One installment every three months. |
| **Termly** | One installment per term, due on the term's first day. Terms that ended before billing starts are skipped. |
| **One Time** or **Yearly** | A single installment, due on the fee's **Pay by** date if it has one, otherwise when billing starts. |
</Step>

<Step>
### The schedule appears on the student

Open the student, go to **Payments**, then **Installments**. Each installment shows its
amount due and due date, grouped by fee.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Due dates

With the default settings, monthly installments are due on the **1st**. An unpaid
installment whose due date has passed shows as overdue. Due today is not overdue. The
amount stays collectible.

### Student joins after the 1st

Enrolled on **3 October**, with the default settings.

| Fee | Installments |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly (tuition, canteen, …) | October is due on **3 October**, the joining day. Then **1 November** through **1 June**. September is not billed. |
| One-time (registration, supplies, …) | One installment due **3 October**, unless the fee has a **Pay by** date. |

On 3 October nothing is overdue. Payment for October is due that same day.

### Student joining mid-month

The **Student joining mid-month** setting decides what a student who joins after the
month's due day pays for that first month (monthly fees only):

| Option | Enrolled on 3 October |
| --- | --- |
| **Full month** (default) | The full October amount, due on 3 October. |
| **Only the days left (prorated)** | Only the days left in October, due on 3 October. |
| **Start next month** | October is not billed. The first installment is due 1 November. |

### Termly fees

A termly fee bills each term on its first day. If the student joins during a term, that
term is due on the joining day.

### In arrears

With **Charge Timing** set to in arrears, billing happens at the end of the period. If the
student joins after this month's due day, that period is skipped: enrollment on
**3 October** with the 1st as the anchor starts on **1 November**.

### Enrollment date as the anchor

When the **Billing Cycle Anchor** is **Student enrollment date**, every installment falls on
that day of the month: enrollment on 3 October gives 3 October, 3 November, 3 December,
and so on. A short month uses its last day, then the enrollment day returns.

## Waive an installment

When the school agrees that a student doesn't owe an installment (for example no
transport for a month), use **Waive** on that installment in the student's
**Installments**, enter a **Reason**, and confirm. The installment shows as **Waived** and
no longer counts as due.

## Changing a plan

To move a student to another plan, use **Change Payment Plan** on their enrollment, pick
the **New Payment Plan**, and enter a **Reason**. Only academic plans that cover the
student's level are offered.

| What happens | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **Already due on the old plan** | Stays owed as it is, at the old plan's price. |
| **Not yet due and unpaid** | Removed. |
| **Not yet due and partly paid** | Closed at the amount already paid. |
| **New plan** | Billed from today through the end of the year. A one-time fee of the new plan is due from today. |
| **Already paid for the same fee** | Not billed again by the new plan. |

<Callout title="Months already paid through a different fee">
If the new plan bills a month again through a different fee than the one already paid,
the dialog shows **Months already paid on the current plan** and asks you to choose:

- **Move the money to the new plan** — the amount already paid goes onto the new plan's
  installment for that month, which becomes partly paid. Only the difference is owed.
- **Don't bill these months again** — those months count as covered.
- **Bill the new fee as well** — the new plan bills them in full. The amount already paid
  stays on the old installment.
</Callout>

## Reading an installment

Each installment tracks three figures:

| Figure | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Amount Due** | The scheduled amount for that installment. |
| **Amount Paid** | How much has been collected against it so far. |
| **Remaining** | Amount due minus amount paid — what is still owed. |

An installment is fully settled when the remaining amount reaches zero.
