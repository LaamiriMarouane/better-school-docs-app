---
title: Refund a payment
description: Reverse a recorded payment, in full or in part, with a reason.
---

A **refund** reverses a payment that was already recorded — for example after an
overpayment or a cancelled service. You can refund the full amount or part of it.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Finance staff correcting or reversing a collected payment.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- Find the **payment** to reverse in the payments list.
- A refund cannot exceed the amount that was paid.

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the payment and choose Refund

Find the payment in **Finance → Payments**, open it, and choose **Refund**.

<Screenshot
  label="Refund form with the payment summary"
  caption="The refund form shows the original payment, then asks for the refund details."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter the refund details

Complete the fields below.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Refund amount** | Yes | How much to refund. Cannot exceed the original amount paid. |
| **Reason** | Yes | Why the refund is being made (3–500 characters). |
| **Cancelled by** | Yes | The guardian the refund is attributed to. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Process the refund

Confirm. The payment's status reflects the refund, and the student's installment
balances are adjusted accordingly.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="Refunds are traceable">
The refund amount, reason, and who it was attributed to are stored with the payment.
Enter an accurate reason — it is part of the financial record.
</Callout>
