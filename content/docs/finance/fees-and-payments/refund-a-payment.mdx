---
title: Refund a payment
description: Give money back on a recorded payment, in full or in part, with a reason.
---

A **refund** gives back money on a payment that was already recorded — for example after
an overpayment or a cancelled service. You can refund the full amount or part of it.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Finance staff correcting or reversing a collected payment.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- Find the **payment** in **Finance → Payments**.
- You can refund up to what is left on the payment. If part of it was already refunded,
  only the rest can be refunded.

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Choose Refund on the payment

In the **Finance → Payments** list, open the actions on the payment's row and choose
**Refund**.

<Screenshot
  label="Refund form with the payment summary"
  caption="The refund form shows the original payment, then asks for the refund details."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Enter the refund details

The form shows the payment's details and the **Maximum refund amount**.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Refund Amount** | Yes | How much to refund. It can't be more than the maximum refund amount. |
| **Refund Reason** | Yes | Why the refund is being made. |
| **Cancelled by** | No | The guardian the refund is attributed to. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Decide what happens to the charge

- **Item returned: also reduce the charge** — tick it when the family gave the item back
  (for example a uniform). The refunded amount also comes off what the family owes, so
  the installment stays settled. Leave it unticked for a goodwill refund: the installment
  reopens as money owed.
- **This refund cancels the charge** — shown only for months closed by a plan change.
  Tick it and those months are billed again on the student's current plan. Leave it
  unticked for a goodwill refund.
</Step>

<Step>
### Process the refund

Click **Process Refund**. The payment shows the refund, and the student's installment
balances are adjusted.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="Refunds are traceable">
The refund amount, reason, and who it was attributed to stay visible on the payment. Enter
an accurate reason — it is part of the financial record.
</Callout>
