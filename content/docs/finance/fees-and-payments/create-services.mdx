title: Create services

---

A **service** is something the school provides that can be charged for — tuition,
transport, canteen, uniforms, and so on. Services are the foundation of finance: every
fee prices **one service**.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators setting up what the school bills for.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- Pick the right **academic year** at the top of the screen.

<Screenshot
  label="Services page with the Add Service button"
  caption="Services are listed for the selected academic year."
/>

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the Services page

Go to **Finance → Services**, then click **Add Service**.

<Screenshot
  label="Create service form"
  caption="The create service form: name, type and description."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Fill in the service details

Complete the fields below, then click **Create Service**.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Name** | Yes | The display name, e.g. *School bus*. |
| **Service Type** | Yes | **Academic**, **Uniform**, **Books**, **Extracurricular**, **Event**, **Canteen**, **Library**, **Transport**, or **Other**. |
| **Description** | No | Optional notes about the service. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Repeat for every service

Add each service the school charges for. They become available when you set up fees.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Transport services">
Use the **Transport** type for bus fees. When you assign riders to a route, the students
whose plan includes a transport fee are the ones offered first.
</Callout>

<Callout title="What comes next">
A service has no price on its own. Give it an amount by
[setting up fees](./set-up-fees).
</Callout>
