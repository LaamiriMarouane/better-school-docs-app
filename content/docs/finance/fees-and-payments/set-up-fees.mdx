---
title: Set up fees
description: Give a service a price for a level, with a periodicity that drives the installment schedule.
---

A **fee** is a priced rule. It says: *this service*, at *this level*, costs *this
amount*, charged at *this periodicity*. Fees are what payment plans are built from.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators pricing the school's services.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- The **service** must already exist — see [Create services](./create-services).
- The **levels** must exist — a fee is priced per level.

<Screenshot
  label="Fees page with the Add Fee button"
  caption="Fees connect a service and a level to an amount."
/>

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the Fees page

Go to **Finance → Fees**, then start a new fee.

<Screenshot
  label="Create fee form"
  caption="The fee form: name, service, level, periodicity and amount."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Fill in the fee details

Complete the fields below, then save.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Name** | Yes | The display name, e.g. *Grade 1 Tuition*. |
| **Service** | Yes | The service this fee prices. |
| **Level** | Yes | The level the fee applies to. |
| **Periodicity** | Yes | How often it is charged — for example monthly or one-time. This shapes the installment schedule. |
| **Amount** | Yes | The price. Must be greater than zero. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Repeat per level

Create a fee for each service-and-level combination your school charges.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Periodicity drives installments">
The **periodicity** determines how many installments a student gets for that fee — a
monthly fee produces a monthly schedule, a one-time fee a single installment.
</Callout>
