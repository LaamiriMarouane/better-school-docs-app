---
title: Set up fees
description: Price a service — one amount or an amount per level — with a periodicity that drives the installment schedule.
---

A **fee** prices a service. It says: *this service* costs *this amount* for *these
levels*, charged at *this periodicity*. Fees are what payment plans are built from.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Administrators pricing the school's services.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- The **service** must already exist — see [Create services](./create-services).
- The **levels** must exist.

<Screenshot
  label="Fees page with the Add Fee button"
  caption="Fees connect a service and a level to an amount."
/>

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the Fees page

Go to **Finance → Fees**, then click **Add Fee**.

<Screenshot
  label="Create fee form"
  caption="The fee form: name, service, level, periodicity and amount."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Name the fee and pick the service

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Name** | Yes | The display name, e.g. *Primary tuition*. |
| **Service** | Yes | The service this fee prices. |
| **Description** | No | Optional notes. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Choose the pricing

Under **Pricing**, pick one:

| Pricing | What you fill in |
| --- | --- |
| **Flat** | The **Levels** the fee applies to, and one **Amount** for all of them. |
| **Per level** | The levels to price, then an amount for each one. **Set all to** fills every level with the same amount, which you can then adjust. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Choose the periodicity

| Periodicity | What the student is charged |
| --- | --- |
| **Monthly** | One installment per month. |
| **Quarterly** | One installment every three months. |
| **Termly** | One installment per term, due on the term's first day. |
| **Yearly** or **One Time** | A single installment. |

For **Yearly** and **One Time** fees you can also set a **Pay by** date (useful for
uniforms, books, or a trip). Leave it empty and the fee is due the day the student is
billed.
</Step>

<Step>
### Save

Click **Create Fee**. Add a fee for each service your school charges.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Editing a fee that is in use

- Once a fee is on a payment plan or has been billed, you can no longer change its
  **Periodicity** or switch between **Flat** and **Per level**. You can still change the
  amounts and add levels, but not remove levels that are already priced.
- When you change the amount, the app asks **Apply the new price?** Unpaid installments
  due today or later take the new price. Paid installments, and unpaid ones whose due date
  has passed, keep their price.

<Callout title="Periodicity drives installments">
The **periodicity** decides how many installments a student gets for that fee. Which
months are included, and the exact due dates, follow the enrollment date and the billing
settings — see [Installments and schedules](./installments-and-schedules).
</Callout>
