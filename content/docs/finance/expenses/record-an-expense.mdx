---
title: Record an expense
description: Enter a manual expense against a category, with an amount, date, and optional receipt.
---

A **manual expense** is a single piece of spending you enter by hand — a utility bill,
a repair, a purchase. It is filed under an [expense category](./create-expense-categories)
and appears in the expenses list.

<Callout title="Who this is for">
Finance staff recording what the school spends.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- At least one **expense category** must exist — see
  [Create expense categories](./create-expense-categories).
- Pick the right **academic year** at the top of the screen.

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the Expenses tab

Go to **Finance → Expenses**, stay on the **Expenses** tab, and click **Add Expense**.

<Screenshot
  label="Record expense form"
  caption="The expense form: title, category, amount, date and details."
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Fill in the expense details

Complete the fields below, then click **Submit**.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Title** | Yes | A short label, e.g. *October electricity*. |
| **Category** | Yes | The expense category to file this under. |
| **Amount** | Yes | The amount spent. Cannot be negative. |
| **Expense date** | Yes | The date the expense occurred. |
| **Payment Method** | No | **Cash** (the default), **Credit Card**, **Bank Transfer**, **Cheque**, or **Other**. |
| **Vendor name** | No | Who was paid. |
| **Receipt number** | No | A reference for the receipt or invoice. |
| **Description** | No | Optional notes. |
| **Upload Document** | No | Attach a receipt or invoice file. |
</Step>

<Step>
### Save the expense

The expense is added to the **Expenses** tab.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Vehicle expenses">
You record **manual** expenses here. Vehicle expenses appear on their own tab and come from
the fuel, maintenance, and other records kept on each vehicle.
</Callout>
