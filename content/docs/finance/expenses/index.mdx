---
title: Expenses
description: Track what your school spends — categorised, recorded, and grouped by source.
---

The **expenses** side of finance records money the school spends. Expenses are
organised into **categories** and come from a few sources:

| Source | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **Manual expenses** | Expenses you record by hand — see [Record an expense](./record-an-expense). |
| **Vehicle expenses** | Generated from transport activity. |
| **Payroll expenses** | Generated from staff salaries. |

Vehicle and payroll expenses are produced automatically by their modules — you review
them here rather than entering them. Manual expenses are the ones you create yourself.

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Create expense categories

Set up the categories you will file expenses under — see
[Create expense categories](./create-expense-categories).
</Step>

<Step>
### Record expenses

Enter each manual expense against a category — see
[Record an expense](./record-an-expense).
</Step>

<Step>
### Review every source

The expenses area separates manual, vehicle, and payroll expenses into tabs so you can
review each, plus a tab for categories.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Everything belongs to an academic year">
Expense categories and expenses are created within the selected academic year.
</Callout>
