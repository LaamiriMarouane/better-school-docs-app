---
title: Expenses
description: Track what your school spends — categorised, recorded, and grouped by source.
---

The **expenses** side of finance records money the school spends. Expenses are
organised into **categories** and come from a few sources:

| Source | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **Manual expenses** | Expenses you record by hand — see [Record an expense](./record-an-expense). |
| **Vehicle expenses** | Recorded on a vehicle — fuel, maintenance, inspection, insurance, and vignette. |

Vehicle expenses come from the records you keep on each vehicle — you review them here. Manual expenses are the ones you create yourself.

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Create expense categories

Set up the categories you will file expenses under — see
[Create expense categories](./create-expense-categories).
</Step>

<Step>
### Record expenses

Enter each manual expense against a category — see
[Record an expense](./record-an-expense).
</Step>

<Step>
### Review every source

The expenses area separates manual and vehicle expenses into tabs so you can review each,
plus a tab for categories.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout title="Work in the right year">
Expenses are recorded in the academic year selected at the top of the screen.
</Callout>
