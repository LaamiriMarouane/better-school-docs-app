Short answers to the questions that come up most. Each links to the full article.

## Access & accounts

### I can't sign in or I forgot my password

Use the password-reset option on the sign-in screen. If that doesn't help, an administrator can sort
out your account. See [Access & authentication](./getting-started/access-and-authentication).

### Why can I see less than a colleague?

What you see depends on your **role** — administrators see the whole school, while teachers, students,
guardians, and staff see their own work, their child, or their own attendance and leave. Some areas are
also **optional modules** (below).

## Modules & visibility

### Why don't I see Transport / Behaviour / Chat / Announcements / Reclamations / Dismissal?

These are **optional modules**. When a module is off for your school, its pages don't appear. The
modules are **Staff Management**, **Leave Management**, **Behaviour**, **Expenses**, **Transport**,
**Equipment**, **Announcements**, **Chat**, **Reclamations**, and **Dismissal**. Ask whoever manages
your school's modules to turn the one you need on. See
[Configure school modules](./getting-started/first-time-school-setup/configure-school-modules).

### Where did a feature go?

If something disappeared, its module may have been turned off, or you may have switched to a different
**academic year** that isn't set up the same way.

## Academic year & language

### How do I change the academic year?

Use the **year switcher** at the top of the screen. Most figures and lists follow the selected year.

### Only one year can be active — why?

The **active** year is the school's current year, so only one is active at a time. You can prepare the
next year as a **draft** alongside it and enroll students in it ahead of time — only a **closed** year
refuses new enrollments. See [Academic year setup](./getting-started/build-the-academic-foundation/academic-year-setup).

### How do I change the language?

Pick your language from the language selector. **Arabic** is shown right-to-left throughout.

## Students & enrollment

### What's the difference between a pre-inscription and an enrollment?

A **pre-inscription** records an intended **level** for a year; an **enrollment** registers the student
for the year. Choosing a **class** and a **payment plan** is optional when you enroll — you can add
them later. See
[Pre-inscriptions](./getting-started/enroll-students/pre-inscriptions).

### It says the student already has an active enrollment

A student can have only **one active enrollment per academic year**. Withdraw or transfer the existing
one first. See [Suspend or withdraw an enrollment](./getting-started/enroll-students/suspend-or-withdraw-an-enrollment).

### How do I start a new school year?

Create the new year, then work through its **Year setup** — see
[Academic year setup](./getting-started/build-the-academic-foundation/academic-year-setup). It walks
you through terms, levels, subjects, classes, and teachers, lets you copy **Fees & payment plans** from
last year, and has a **Transport** step for zones, routes, and riders. A step counts as ready only once
you choose **Confirm for this year** on it. When the required steps are confirmed, you can
**Activate year**. Students are enrolled again each year.

## Timetable

### The timetable won't generate, or says resources aren't enough

The generator needs enough **rooms, teachers, and time** to place every lesson. Check the
prerequisites and make sure your resources can cover the load. See
[Timetable prerequisites](./timetable/prerequisites).

## Teaching

### A teacher can't see or edit a class's lessons

Lesson content is gated by **teaching assignment** — a teacher edits lessons for the classes and
subjects they're assigned to teach. See [Lessons](./teaching/lessons).

### Where do I take attendance?

Take attendance from a **class session** on the **timetable** (open the session → **Mark
attendance**), or on the dedicated **Student Attendance** page (by day or by session). The **Activity
Journal** logs what was taught — it doesn't take attendance. See
[Class sessions & attendance](./teaching/sessions-and-attendance).

## Behaviour

### Who can see a student's behaviour points?

Students and parents see **only their own** standing. The school-wide leaderboard is for staff. See
[Points & leaderboard](./behaviour/leaderboard).

## Communication

### Announcement or event — which should I use?

An **announcement** is a message to an audience; an **event** is something on the calendar (and some
event kinds close the school). See [Announcements](./communication/announcements) and
[Events](./communication/events).
